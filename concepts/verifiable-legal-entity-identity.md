# Verifiable legal entity identity

Definition: Cryptographically verifiable credentials that prove a legal entity and its authorised roles to a counterparty, such as GLEIF's vLEI.

Sub-question: trust

Related concepts: [[adoption-ladder]], [[preparer-and-reviewer-roles]]

Evidence:
- GLEIF's vLEI governance framework has run since 2022, with a v4.0 glossary in March 2026, qualified issuers re-evaluated annually [gleif-vlei-egf] (search-summary only).
- It attests who an entity or signer is, not the truth of a financial figure; no standard found that attests financial facts.
- No 2026 adoption figures located.

Open questions:
- Can an agent hold a role credential (delegated signer) under vLEI?
- Who would issue an attestation about a figure?
