# Covenant compliance

Definition: The borrower's periodic proof to its lender that financial covenants hold, delivered as an officer-signed compliance certificate with the covenant calculations attached; in private companies it is often the main outside use of the monthly close.

Related concepts: [[preparer-and-reviewer-roles]], [[confidence-routing]]

What the thesis says: C29 (review moves to the EBITDA add-back schedule, agent never proposes add-backs); C21 (covenant-touching items route to a person); C19 (lender clock binds, not filing deadline); C27 (unaudited lender-driven closes go exception-only first).
