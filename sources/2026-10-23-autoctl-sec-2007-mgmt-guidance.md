# SEC 2007 management ICFR guidance (Release 33-8810), Federal Register text

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2007-06-27/html/E7-12299.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: SEC interpretive guidance 27 Jun 2007, 72 FR 35323, on govinfo. Mgmt may find automated controls more efficient to evaluate if ITGCs effective. ITGCs alone do not address FR risks; automated control operation depends on ITGCs. Risk of control failure scales with judgment/complexity, so low-judgment automated controls need less evidence. No requirement for per-item outcome sampling of automated controls.

Quotes:
- "management may determine that automated controls are more efficient to evaluate than manual controls." — SEC, Release 33-8810, section on identifying controls
- "while it general controls alone ordinarily do not adequately address financial reporting risks, the proper and consistent operation of automated controls or it functionality often depends upon effective it general controls." — SEC, role of IT general controls
- "management's judgment of the risk of control failure would be higher for controls whose operation requires significant judgment than for non-complex controls requiring less judgment." — SEC, evaluating operating effectiveness
