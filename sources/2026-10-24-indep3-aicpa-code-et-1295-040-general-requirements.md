# AICPA Code of Professional Conduct, ET 1.295.040 General Requirements for Performing Nonattest Services

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pub.aicpa.org/codeofconduct/ethicsresources/et-cod.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: Official AICPA Code PDF. Nonattest services leave independence unimpaired only if all safeguards are met, including that the client assumes management responsibilities, designates an overseer with suitable skill/knowledge/experience, evaluates results, and accepts responsibility.

Quotes:
- "threats would be at an acceptable level , and independence would not be impaired , when all the following safeguards are met" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.040 .01
- "assume all management responsibilities as described in the" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.040 .01a(i)
- "oversee the service, by designating an individual, preferably within senior management, who possesses suitable skill, knowledge, and/or experience" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.040 .01a(ii)
- "evaluate the adequacy and results of the services performed" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.040 .01a(iii)
- "accept responsibility for the results of the services" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.040 .01a(iv)
- "the member should assess and be satisfied that such individual understands the services to be performed sufficiently to oversee them" — AICPA Code ET 1.295.040.01a.ii
- "however, the individual is not required to possess the expertise to perform or re-perform the services" — AICPA Code ET 1.295.040.01a.ii
