# ISA 610 (Revised 2013), Using the Work of Internal Auditors (IAASB/IFAC)

URL: https://www.ifac.org/system/files/publications/files/ISA-610-%28Revised-2013%29.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Conditions under which an external auditor may use an internal audit function's (IAF) work: objectivity/organizational status, competence, systematic and disciplined approach incl. quality control. If any fails, the work cannot be used. External auditor keeps sole responsibility for the opinion and all significant judgments; more judgment or higher risk means less use. Direct assistance is barred for significant judgments and higher-risk areas. The standard is written for people ("internal auditors"); nothing on automated tools or agents.
