# PSR: Groundbreaking new protections for victims of APP scams start today

URL: https://www.psr.org.uk/news-and-updates/latest-news/news/groundbreaking-new-protections-for-victims-of-app-scams-start-today/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0002F

Summary: PSR press release, 7 Oct 2024. Mandatory reimbursement of APP fraud over Faster Payments and CHAPS; covers individuals, microenterprises, charities. Cap £85,000 standard, £100 optional excess, 35-business-day outcome limit, 13-month claim window. Moves payee-fraud loss onto banks; micro-business scope matters for SMB. Reimbursement is for payments made on/after 7 Oct 2024. Press release, not policy text (PS24/3, PS24/7 are the standard).

Quotes:
- "The reimbursement requirement protects individuals, microenterprises and charities." — PSR
- "The protections will apply to payments made on or after 7 October 2024." — PSR
- "People will be covered up to £85,000 as standard." — PSR
- "The vast majority of consumers can expect to be reimbursed within five business days of making their claim." — PSR
