# ISA (UK) 505 External Confirmations (FRC, revised October 2023, updated October 2025)

URL: https://www.frc.org.uk/documents/8824/ISA_UK_505_Revised_October_2023_Updated_October_2025.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: UK adoption of IAASB ISA 505. Para 15 keeps ISA 505's four conditions for sole reliance on negative confirmations (low assessed risk with control evidence, many small homogeneous items, very low expected exceptions, no reason recipients disregard). Novel: the UK version prohibits negative confirmations outright, and A23 admits non-response does not show the request was received.
