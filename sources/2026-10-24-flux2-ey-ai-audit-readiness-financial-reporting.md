# EY: AI audit readiness for financial reporting

URL: https://www.ey.com/en_us/insights/assurance/ai-audit-readiness-for-financial-reporting
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Big 4 publication (EY, US) on what auditors will expect when AI is used in financial reporting controls. Says AI already drafts close-package narratives and supports management review; auditors will expect controls over completeness, accuracy, relevance of data going in, human review procedures, and will look for evidence of management review and evaluation of the output. Management needs a defensible basis that AI outputs are complete, accurate, relevant. Firm view, not a standard.

Quotes:
- "drafting narratives for close packages, prioritizing exceptions, suggesting classifications or supporting management review" — EY, page text
- "auditors will look for evidence of management review and evaluation of the output." — EY, page text
- "management still needs to be in a position to understand how the output was determined." — EY, page text
- "consistent with broader expectations for information produced by the company that is used as audit evidence" — EY, page text
- "the organization needs a defensible basis for concluding that outputs are complete, accurate and relevant for the objective it was designed for." — EY, page text
