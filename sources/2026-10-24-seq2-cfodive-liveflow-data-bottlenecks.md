# CFO Dive: Data bottlenecks stall CFOs' push for faster month-end close (LiveFlow survey)

URL: https://www.cfodive.com/news/cfo-push-faster-month-end-close-stalled-data-bottlenecks-ai/819283/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Trade-press report (5 May 2026) of a survey by LiveFlow, a finance-software vendor (so vendor research, sample size not stated on the page). Nearly 8 in 10 respondents blame close delays on waiting for data from other systems or departments; close length distribution given. AI use is concentrated in drafting and analysis, not operational close work.

Quotes:
- "Nearly eight in 10 corporate finance professionals blame month-end close delays on having to wait for data from other systems or departments" — CFO Dive, Dive Brief
- "Reconciling information across multiple platforms was another barrier, cited by more than half of survey respondents." — CFO Dive
- "Only 16% of respondents said they complete their month-end close in under three days." — CFO Dive
- "The largest group, 37%, reported cycles of three to five days, followed by 21% at five to 10 days and 16% extending beyond 10 days." — CFO Dive
- "while the underlying operational workload has shifted far less" — CFO Dive paraphrasing LiveFlow analyst Aaryn Ross
- "just 23% relying on AI feaures embedded in finance systems" — CFO Dive (typo "feaures" is in the source)
