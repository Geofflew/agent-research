# PCAOB AS 2310 The Auditor's Use of Confirmation (effective FY ending on or after June 15, 2025)

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2310
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: AS 2310 (adopted 2023): auditor must keep control of the confirmation process and send/receive directly from the confirming party; may use an intermediary for direct electronic transmission but must evaluate intermediary controls over interception/alteration and the company's ability to override them. For cash and AR, confirmation is required unless the auditor directly accesses information maintained by a knowledgeable external source (para .24). Effective date June 15, 2025 fiscal year-ends. Standard is silent on who/what performs the steps on either side.

Quotes:
- "For audits of financial statements for fiscal years ending on or after June 15, 2025." — PCAOB AS 2310, paras .14-.17, .24, Appendix B, header
- "The auditor should maintain control over the confirmation process to minimize the likelihood that information exchanged between the auditor and the confirming party is intercepted or altered." — PCAOB AS 2310, paras .14-.17, .24, Appendix B, header
- "The auditor should send the confirmation request directly to the confirming party and obtain the confirmation response directly from the confirming party." — PCAOB AS 2310, paras .14-.17, .24, Appendix B, header
- "The auditor or the confirming party can engage another party as an intermediary to facilitate direct electronic transmission of confirmation requests and responses between the auditor and the confirming party." — PCAOB AS 2310, paras .14-.17, .24, Appendix B, header
- "the auditor should perform confirmation procedures in accordance with paragraphs .08 through .23, or otherwise obtain relevant and reliable audit evidence by directly accessing information maintained by a knowledgeable external source." — PCAOB AS 2310, paras .14-.17, .24, Appendix B, header
- "assess the relationship of the intermediary with the company - specifically, whether circumstances exist that give the company the ability to override" — PCAOB AS 2310, paras .14-.17, .24, Appendix B, header
