# Salesforce Agentforce pricing page

URL: https://www.salesforce.com/agentforce/pricing/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005C

Summary: Agentforce priced three ways: Flex Credits ($500/100k, 20 credits per action = $0.10), $2 per conversation, per-user licences. Employee-facing agents unmetered inside Max Edition/add-on. Metering unit = action, not outcome or hours.

Quotes:
- "Agentforce actions are 20 Flex Credits, while Agentforce Voice actions are 30 Flex Credits." — Salesforce, pricing page
- "$ 500 USD/Per 100k Credits" — Salesforce, Flex Credits price
- "$ 2 USD/Per conversation" — Salesforce, Conversations price
- "Enabling unmetered employee agent usage for licensed users" — Salesforce, per-user model for employee-facing agents
- "2.75M Flex Credits per org per year" — Salesforce, Max Edition inclusion
