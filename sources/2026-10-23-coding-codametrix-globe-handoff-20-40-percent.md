# Boston Globe: CodaMetrix AI bows out on 20-40% of cases, confidence scores route to coders

URL: https://www.bostonglobe.com/2023/03/21/business/boston-startup-uses-ai-untangle-health-insurance-problems
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: CodaMetrix autonomous coding (Mass General Brigham customer). AI first decides if it can handle chart; 20-40% handed to human coders by specialty, 60-80% coded by AI. Confidence score; low score triggers human review. Denial/cost claims vendor-reported. MGB employs ~200 billing coders.

Quotes:
- "For about 20 to 40 percent of cases, depending on the specialty, the AI bows out and sends the case to human coders." — Globe, routing
- "But in the other 60 to 80 percent of cases, the AI handles the coding itself." — Globe
- "Trickier medical reports generate lower scores, sometimes triggering a human coding expert to review the" — Globe, confidence routing
- "AI-coded claims are 60 percent less likely to be denied by insurers, CodaMetrix said." — Globe, vendor claim
- "The Mass General Brigham physicians organizations employ about 200 people as billing coders" — Globe, workforce
