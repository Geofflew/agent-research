# Audit Analytics and Audit Outcomes: Diagnostic Applications and Financial Reporting Artifacts (seminar abstract, PolyU)

URL: https://www.polyu.edu.hk/af/news-and-events/event/2024/03/20240328-prof-vernon-j-richardson
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Abstract of an archival study (authors not named on page; keynote speaker Vernon Richardson) of audit data analytics implementations by internal audit functions. Finds internal audit ADA is linked to lower later external audit fees and audit lags, strongest for diagnostic analytics, plus fewer material weaknesses, restatements and lower discretionary accruals. Association only; fee drop is consistent with external auditors relying more, but the abstract does not test reliance directly. Abstract only, not the full paper.
