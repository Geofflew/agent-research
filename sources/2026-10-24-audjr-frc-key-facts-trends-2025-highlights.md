# FRC Key Facts and Trends in the Accountancy Profession 2025: Highlights

URL: https://www.frc.org.uk/library/supervision/professional-bodies-supervision/key-facts-and-trends-in-the-accountancy-profession/key-facts-and-trends-in-the-accountancy-profession-2025/highlights/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: FRC, published 15 Oct 2025, covering 2024. Student numbers in UK/ROI nearly flat; RQB students rising; Audit Qualifications awarded fell to about 1,800 from 1,900. No AI mention and no firm-level trainee counts; the 2024 data predates the 2025 graduate cuts.

Quotes:
- "Between 2023 and 2024, student numbers in the UK and ROI decreased by 0.3% and by 0.1% worldwide" — FRC
- "In 2024, there were in total nearly 116,000 students registered with RQBs with addresses either in the UK or ROI, up from nearly 113,000 in 2023." — FRC
- "Around 1,800 members were awarded the Audit Qualification in 2024, down from 1,900 in 2023." — FRC
- "The number of statutory audit firms registered with the RSBs continues to decline" — FRC
