# PCAOB AS 2201: testing design and operating effectiveness of controls

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2007-06-12/html/E7-11311.htm
Type: standard
Read in session: 0005A

Summary: (0008A rehosted from r.jina.ai pcaobus mirror to govinfo FR notice of AS No. 5, 2007; B28-B33 text identical)  AS 2201 paras .42 .44 .45 .46 .B9. Auditor tests control design + operation incl. performer authority and competence.
Effectiveness cannot be inferred from absence of misstatements: no outcome-based shortcut to drop a control.

Quotes:
- "The auditor should test the design effectiveness of controls by determining whether the company's controls, if they are operated as prescribed by persons possessing the necessary authority and competence to perform the control effectively" — PCAOB Auditing Standard No. 5 (now AS 2201), as printed in 72 FR (SEC notice E7-11311)
- "The auditor should test the operating effectiveness of a control by determining whether the control is operating as designed and whether the person performing the control possesses the necessary authority and competence to perform the control effectively." — PCAOB Auditing Standard No. 5 (now AS 2201), as printed in 72 FR (SEC notice E7-11311)
- "the effectiveness of a control cannot be inferred from the absence of misstatements detected by substantive procedures" — PCAOB Auditing Standard No. 5 (now AS 2201), as printed in 72 FR (SEC notice E7-11311)
- "As the risk associated with the control being tested increases, the evidence that the auditor should obtain also increases." — PCAOB Auditing Standard No. 5 (now AS 2201), as printed in 72 FR (SEC notice E7-11311)
- "Whether the control relies on performance by an individual or is automated" — PCAOB Auditing Standard No. 5 (now AS 2201), as printed in 72 FR (SEC notice E7-11311)
