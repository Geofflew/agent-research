# Accountant Can Be Sued For Review (Liberty Finance v. BDO Seidman, N.C. App. 1996)

URL: https://nclawyersweekly.com/?p=30928
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: News report: NC Court of Appeals held a finance company that relied on a reviewed (not audited) steel company statement could sue the accountant for negligent misrepresentation; review carried a disclaimer and no opinion. Overstatement of receivables and retained earnings by $4 million each. Shows review engagements can carry third-party lender liability, at least at the pleading stage in NC. Secondary source; decision itself not read.

Quotes:
- "An accountant who prepared a review of a company’s financial statements can be sued by a third party who relied on that information" — Accountant Can Be Sued For Review (Liberty Finance v. BDO Seidman, N.C. App. 1996)
- "Liberty Finance establishes that CPAs may also be sued for a review, a less rigorous check which involves testing financial numbers supplied by the company." — Accountant Can Be Sued For Review (Liberty Finance v. BDO Seidman, N.C. App. 1996)
- "It did not offer any opinion on the accuracy of the statements, and a disclaimer limited the scope of its written report." — Accountant Can Be Sued For Review (Liberty Finance v. BDO Seidman, N.C. App. 1996)
- "We at least know as a matter of law that reviews have some utility and can be the basis for liability" — Accountant Can Be Sued For Review (Liberty Finance v. BDO Seidman, N.C. App. 1996)
