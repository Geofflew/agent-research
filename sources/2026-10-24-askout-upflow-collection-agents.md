# Upflow docs: collection agents

URL: https://docs.upflow.io/en-us/collection-and-collaboration/emails/collection-agents.md
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Upflow's AI collection agent works on inbound customer emails (reply, promise to pay, dispute, remittance) with per-skill autonomy: off, suggestions only (human sends), or auto. It responds to customers rather than initiating outreach; reminders remain workflow-driven. Auto-mode downgrades to suggestion when unsure.

Quotes:
- "Nothing is sent or created without you." — Upflow docs, Suggestions only mode
- "The agent acts on its own — sends the reply, or creates the promise to pay or dispute — without waiting for you." — Upflow docs, Auto-mode
- "the agent only executes automatically when it is confident." — Upflow docs, note on Auto-mode
- "Collection agents act on the replies that land in your Upflow inbox" — Upflow docs, Before you start
- "reminders stop for a customer once the agent logs a promise to pay" — Upflow docs, Create a collection agent
- "Upflow reads the past six months of email conversations you've had with your customers" — Upflow docs, auto-generate instructions
- "the workflow keeps running its reminders as normal." — Upflow docs, deactivating an agent
