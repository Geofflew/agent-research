# EPC Verification Of Payee (VOP) scheme

URL: https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/what-we-do/other-schemes/verification-payee
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: EPC VOP: payer PSP asks payee PSP to check IBAN + name; reply match/no match/close match/not possible, passed to payer instantly. Built for EU IPR legal duty. Scheme itself says cannot identify person or legal entity; matches name vs bank record only.

Quotes:
- "The Responding PSP will then instantly verify whether the received data match with the concerned data registered for that payee at the Responding PSP." — EPC Verification Of Payee (VOP) scheme
- "The Responding PSP immediately provides the Requesting PSP with a VOP response (e.g., match, no match, close match with the name of the payee, match/verification check not possible)." — EPC Verification Of Payee (VOP) scheme
- "The scheme has been in first instance developed to support PSPs in SEPA who will be required to comply with legal obligations on the verification of a payee in the case of credit transfers" — EPC Verification Of Payee (VOP) scheme
- "It cannot be relied upon to identify a private or a legal person." — EPC Verification Of Payee (VOP) scheme
