# AccountingWeb: Starling adds partner portal to accounting tool

URL: https://www.accountingweb.co.uk/node/230683
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: Starling launched built-in bookkeeping/tax software (Ember, acquired Aug 2025) in March 2026 with no accountant access; partner portal followed (read-only from end June 2026). Where the client only uses Starling accounting, they do the actions themselves; Starling envisions owners self-serving quarterly tasks, accountants stepping in at year-end.
