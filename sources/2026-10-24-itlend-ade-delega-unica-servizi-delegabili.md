# Agenzia delle Entrate - Delega unica: servizi delegabili (cassetto fiscale, fatture elettroniche)

URL: https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/servizi-delegabili-imprese
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: The tax authority's page on the single delegation lets a taxpayer delegate read access to its cassetto fiscale and to consultation and download of its e-invoices (and VAT data). This is the consent mechanism a lender or auditor would need. Only services 4 and 5 (issuing/storing invoices, device registration) are stated as delegable to non-intermediaries; consultation of invoices (1) and VAT data (2) are not, so a lender not registered as an intermediary has no stated direct delegation route to invoice data. The page does not mention lenders.

Quotes:
- "a) la consultazione del Cassetto fiscale delegato" — Agenzia delle Entrate, Servizi delegabili
- "consultazione e acquisizione delle fatture elettroniche o dei loro duplicati informatici" — Agenzia delle Entrate, Servizi delegabili, point b)1
- "I servizi 4 e 5 del punto b) sono delegabili anche a favore di soggetti diversi dagli intermediari." — Agenzia delle Entrate, Servizi delegabili
