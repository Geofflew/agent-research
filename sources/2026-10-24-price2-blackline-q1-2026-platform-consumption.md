# BlackLine Q1 2026 earnings call: platform fee plus consumption for Verity agents

URL: https://www.marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-5-5-blackline-inc-stock/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: BlackLine CEO: platform fee commit, Verity agents consumption layered on top like matching today. Sell units of financial productivity not seats. CFO: consumption nominal in 2026 guide, 50% of exit-2026 ARR non-seat. Metered by consumption, not acceptance.

Quotes:
- "Our platform model allows us to sell units of financial productivity rather than seats, which over time we believe opens access to labor and operational budgets beyond traditional software spend." — earnings call
- "As they then deploy Verity agents and automate work that was previously manual, consumption-based pricing layers on top of that base, similar to how we price capabilities like matching today." — earnings call
- "there's a nominal amount of consumption revenue included in the 2026 guide for the remainder of the year." — earnings call
- "we can reassert that at least 50% of our ARR exiting 2026 will be non-seat based as a result of everything that we just discussed." — earnings call
