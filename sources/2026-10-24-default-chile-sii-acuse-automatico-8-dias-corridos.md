# SII: acuse de recibo automático tras 8 días corridos (propuesta IVA / crédito fiscal)

URL: https://www.sii.cl/destacados/f29/usocreditofiscal.htm
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: SII page on using VAT credit from received invoices. For credit invoices, the acknowledgement of receipt (acuse) is given automatically after 8 calendar days from SII receipt; invoices received in the last 8 days of a month slip to the next month's credit. Accepting content early locks the document against rejection. The default outcome converts waiting into both legal acceptance and tax-credit timing.

Quotes:
- "Cabe destacar que, el Acuse de Recibo, para los documentos no pagados al contado, se da automáticamente al cabo de los 8 días corridos desde que la factura es recepcionada por el SII." — SII, usocreditofiscal page (gloss: for documents not paid in cash, the acknowledgement is given automatically after 8 calendar days from SII receipt)
- "Ten presente que todas aquellas facturas recibidas en el SII los últimos 8 días del mes, tendrán su acuse de recibo automático al mes siguiente." — SII (gloss: invoices received in the last 8 days of the month get automatic acknowledgement the following month, shifting the credit period)
- "Acepta contenido DTE. El documento ya no puede ser rechazado." — SII, list of five options (gloss: accepting content means the document can no longer be rejected)
