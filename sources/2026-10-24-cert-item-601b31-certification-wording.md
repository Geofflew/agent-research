# 17 CFR 229.601 - Exhibit 31 certification wording (Item 601(b)(31))

URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/229.601
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Prescribed certification text. Officer certifies on own knowledge and that controls were designed so information is made known to the officer by others in the entity - the textual basis for sub-certification.

Quotes:
- "Based on my knowledge, this report does not contain any untrue statement of a material fact or omit to state a material fact necessary to make the statements made, in light of the circumstances under which such statements were made, not misleading with respect to the period covered by this report;" — Cornell LII reproduction of eCFR, Item 601(b)(31)(i), paragraphs 2 and 4(a)
- "designed such disclosure controls and procedures, or caused such disclosure controls and procedures to be designed under our supervision, to ensure that material information relating to the registrant" — Cornell LII reproduction of eCFR, Item 601(b)(31)(i), paragraphs 2 and 4(a)

- "is made known to us by others within those entities, particularly during the period in which this report is being prepared" — Item 601(b)(31)(i), paragraph 4(a)
