# Benchmarking IT application controls (Compact, quoting PCAOB AS 5 App. B)

URL: https://www.compact.nl/articles/benchmarking-it-application-controls/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Dutch IT-audit journal (Compact/KPMG-linked authors), SAP-focused. Quotes AS5 App. B benchmarking items B29/B31/B33. Automated control tested once = baseline, reused later years if ITGCs effective + control unchanged + stable app. Baseline re-established periodically; 3 yrs good practice. Rely w/o yearly outcome sampling; reliance rests on ITGCs and change evidence. Secondary mirror of pcaobus.org text (blocked).

Quotes:
- "entirely automated application controls are generally not subject to breakdowns due to human failure." — AS5 Appendix B as quoted by Compact
- "the general controls over program changes, access to programs, and computer operations should be tested effectively when establishing the baseline (as5 item b29)." — Compact, precondition list
- "the benchmark must be re-established after a period of time (as5 item b33)." — Compact
- "these controls are enabled and effective for the whole population, as these controls are normally settings in the it or erp systems." — Compact, on IT application controls
