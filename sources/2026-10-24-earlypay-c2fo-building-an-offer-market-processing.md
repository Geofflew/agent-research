# C2FO Early Pay help: Building An Offer (frequency, rate, market processing, customer acceptance)

URL: http://help.c2fo.com/en/articles/15351659-building-an-offer
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: C2FO help page on the supplier workflow: select customers/divisions, choose one-time or recurring, set APR or discount rate, submit. The offer is evaluated at Market Processing per division and then the customer accepts or not; recurring offers apply to future eligible invoices. Mechanism is supplier-bid rate plus buyer-side acceptance, run in batch cycles, with no agent-to-agent negotiation described.

Quotes:
- "Continues to apply to current and future eligible invoices." — C2FO help, Recurring frequency
- "Your offer is evaluated during Market Processing for each division." — C2FO help, After you submit
- "If accepted, awards appear in" — C2FO help, Customer acceptance step
- "Some divisions use" — C2FO help, Fixed or Variable rates set by the customer
