# Global Internal Audit Standards (IIA, January 2024; effective 2025)

URL: https://www.theiia.org/globalassets/site/standards/globalinternalauditstandards_2024january9.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Standard 7.1 and its guidance: the CAE is best placed reporting functionally to the board, with administrative line to management. Standard 10.3 requires the CAE to keep technology under evaluation and pursue improvements; guidance names data science and analytics tools and testing of entire populations. No text on AI agents; technology language is "must strive", i.e. soft. The PDF text does not state the effective date itself (the 9 Jan 2025 date comes from IIA announcements, not this PDF).

Quotes:
- "The chief audit executive must confirm to the board the organizational independence of the" — Standard 7.1 requirement
- "(also known as “functionally reporting to the board”), rather than directly accountable to management for" — Standard 7.1 considerations
- "While the chief audit executive reports functionally to the board, the administrative reporting relationship" — Standard 7.1 considerations
- "The chief audit executive must strive to ensure that the internal audit function has technology" — Standard 10.3 requirement
- "Tools that assist with data science and analytics." — Standard 10.3 considerations
- "analyses, such as software applications that enable testing of an entire population rather than just a sample." — Domain V guidance (analyses)
- "of data analysis software and other technology that support the review and evaluation processes." — Domain IV, due professional care
