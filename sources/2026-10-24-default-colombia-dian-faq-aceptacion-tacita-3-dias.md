# DIAN FAQ: factura electrónica, acuse de recibo y aceptación tácita (3 días hábiles)

URL: https://micrositios.dian.gov.co/sistema-de-facturacion-electronica/facturacion-preguntas-frecuentes/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: DIAN electronic invoicing FAQ. States tacit acceptance arises when the buyer acknowledges receipt and does not reject within three business days; Commercial Code treats it as irrevocably accepted. Also records an industry conflict: health-sector rules give 27 to 60 days to respond, versus the 3-day MINCIT rule.

Quotes:
- "pasados tres días hábiles no la rechaza, el Código de Comercio, establece que se entiende aceptada de forma irrevocable" — DIAN FAQ no. 38 (gloss: after three business days without rejecting, the Commercial Code establishes it is understood accepted irrevocably)
- "El MINCIT indica 3 días para la aceptación tácita, pero el Ministerio de Salud indica que para las empresas de este sector pueden tener entre 27 hasta 60 días para pronunciarse sobre una factura." — question no. 29 in DIAN FAQ (gloss: MINCIT says 3 days for tacit acceptance, but the Health Ministry lets health-sector firms take 27 to 60 days to pronounce on an invoice)
