# 7 CFR 4279.137 — USDA B&I guaranteed loans: financial statements

URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/7/4279.137
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: USDA Business & Industry guarantee rule. Floor is annual GAAP statements prepared by an accountant (not necessarily audited); lender picks type and frequency. The only dollar trigger for audit is a $10 million loan size, and even then the Agency "may" require it (discretionary). Audits under 4279.71 are tied to public bodies/nonprofits/Tribes meeting 2 CFR 200 Subpart F thresholds, not ordinary private borrowers.

Quotes:
- "At a minimum, annual financial statements prepared by an accountant in accordance with GAAP are required" — 7 CFR 4279.137
- "if the loan amount exceeds $10 million or if circumstances warrant, the Agency may require annual audited financial statements." — 7 CFR 4279.137
- "the lender will determine the type and frequency of submission of financial statements by the borrower and any guarantors." — 7 CFR 4279.137
