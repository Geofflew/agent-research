# Study examines the impact of reporting frequency (CFA Institute on UK 2007-2014 quarterly mandate)

URL: https://blogs.cfainstitute.org/blog/2017/05/23/study-examines-the-impact-of-reporting-frequency/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: CFA Institute Research Foundation brief (Pozen et al, 2017). UK required quarterly 2007, dropped 2014. No change in long-term investment. Analyst coverage rose on mandate. Contradicts myopia story; real effect is on analysts not capex.

Quotes:
- "the frequency of a uk company's earnings reports does not materially affect its level of corporate investment" — study authors, quoted in post
- "the study did not find any significant change in the levels of long-term investments from 2007 to 2010" — post
- "the average number of analysts following uk companies jumped significantly from 2007 to 2009" — post
- "the uk regulators did not force companies to include financial statements in quarterly reports" — post, why UK quarterlies were thin
