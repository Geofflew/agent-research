# Budget and Accrual Setup (Thomson Reuters Legal Tracker help)

URL: https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/legal-tracker/company/budget-and-accruals-setup/budget-accrual-setup
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Shipped workflow that asks outside parties (law firms) for unbilled amounts on a calendar, emails reminders, then emails internal matter owners to approve or modify the firm's estimate before a cutoff, when accruals auto-generate. Business owner sees the pre-filled estimate (anchor) and edits it; optional review mode sends no alerts. Narrow domain (legal spend) but the closest shipped ask-record-accrue loop.
