# Polish Tax Ordinance art. 117ba: joint liability for paying an account not on the white list

URL: https://arslege.pl/ordynacja-podatkowa/k38/a121849/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Statute text (consolidated Dz.U.2026.0.622, hosted on a commercial legal site) making a VAT-registered buyer jointly liable with the supplier for the supplier's VAT arrears on a supply when the invoice was paid by transfer to an account other than the one in the art. 96b VAT Act register on the day of the transfer order. Safe harbour: notify the buyer's tax office within 7 days of the transfer order on first payment to that account; also exempt are split payment and certain bank-own accounts.

Quotes:
- "została dokonana przelewem na rachunek inny niż zawarty na dzień zlecenia przelewu w wykazie podmiotów" — Ordynacja podatkowa art. 117ba para. 1 and 3
- "odpowiada solidarnie całym swoim majątkiem wraz z dostawcą towarów lub usługodawcą" — Ordynacja podatkowa art. 117ba para. 1
- "w terminie 7 dni od dnia zlecenia przelewu" — art. 117ba para. 3 pt 2, deadline for the notification that removes liability
