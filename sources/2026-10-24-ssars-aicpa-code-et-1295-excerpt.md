Summary: Verbatim excerpt of the AICPA Code (2024 edition pages) reproduced as an attachment to a Town of Los Gatos meeting agenda (government host). 1.295.030 says assuming a management responsibility for an attest client impairs independence with no safeguard possible. The designation of an overseer with suitable skill, knowledge, and/or experience, who need not be able to perform or re-perform the service, is in 1.295.040(a)(ii), not 1.295.030. Covers premise 3.

Quotes:
- "the management participation threat would be so significant that no safeguards could reduce the threat to an acceptable level and independence would be impaired" — ET 1.295.030.01
- "oversee the service, by designating an individual, preferably within senior management, who possesses suitable skill, knowledge, and/or experience." — ET 1.295.040.01a(ii)
- "However, the individual is not required to possess the expertise to perform or re-perform the services." — ET 1.295.040.01a(ii)
