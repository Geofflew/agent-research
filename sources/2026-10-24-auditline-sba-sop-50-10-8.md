# SBA SOP 50 10 8: Lender and Development Company Loan Programs (effective 1 June 2025)

URL: https://www.partneresi.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/SOP-50-10-8-effective-6.1.2025-Final.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Searched the full SOP for audited and reviewed. SBA sets no audit threshold for borrowers. The one size line is for CAPLine: borrower-prepared statements at $1M or less, and compiled, reviewed, or audited above $1M, deferring to the lender's own policy for similar non-SBA lines. The SOP also accepts third-party CPA-prepared or reviewed statements to verify a seller's financial data. So for SBA loans the audit-versus-review line is the lender's choice. A newer 8.1 version takes effect 1 October 2026 and was not checked.

Quotes:

- "CAPLine amount is $1,000,000 or less and compiled, reviewed, or" — CAPLine requirements, item iii
- "audited financial statements and tax returns if the CAPLine amount is" — CAPLine requirements, item iii
- "similarly-sized, non-SBA guaranteed commercial lines of credit;" — CAPLine requirements, item iii, the lender-policy benchmark
