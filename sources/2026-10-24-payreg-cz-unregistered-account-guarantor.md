# Czech Financial Administration: paying a VAT payer's unregistered account makes you a guarantor

URL: https://financnisprava.gov.cz/en/financial-administration/news/news-2013/by-paying-on-a-vat-payer-s-unregistered-account-you-run-the-risk-that-you-may-become-a-guarantor
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: 2013 notice from the Czech tax administration that from 1 Jan 2014 (VAT Act section 109(2)(c)) a purchaser paying a supplier's account other than the one the tax administrator has published becomes guarantor for the supplier's unpaid VAT. Accounts are published in the VAT Payers Register online. Note: courts later narrowed this (knowledge test, per secondary sources), not on this page.

Quotes:
- "the purchaser will become a guarantor for the provider's potentially unpaid value added tax" — Czech Financial Administration notice
- "The tax administrator publishes the providers´ bank account numbers in the VAT Payers Register" — Czech Financial Administration notice
- "will come into force from 1 st January 2014" — Czech Financial Administration notice, on VAT Act section 109(2)(c)
