# Service-Public Entreprendre: Tout savoir sur la facturation (verified 7 Aug 2026)

URL: https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F23208
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Official guidance fiche. All firms must be able to receive e-invoices from 1 Sept 2026; e-invoices are issued, sent, received and kept through an approved platform. Software automates numbering and mandatory mentions but the user is told to check the invoice before validation. Corrections: before payment a rectifying invoice "annule et remplace" with a new number; after payment only a credit note (avoir).
