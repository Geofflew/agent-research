Summary: Executed bank credit agreement amendment (SEC Exhibit 10.3, read via r.jina.ai mirror because sec.gov blocks plain fetch) that restates the borrower's financial-statement covenant. Monthly unaudited balance sheet and income statement due 30 days after month end, quarterly statements 45 days after quarter end, audited annual 90 days; officer certifies each as fairly stated. Monthly AR/AP agings, deferred revenue schedule and GL reconciliations also due in 30 days. Actual contract text, not vendor guidance. This excerpt carries no compliance-certificate clause (see lend2-midcap-sight entry).
