# Doyle & Magilke 2013, Decision usefulness and accelerated filing deadlines (J. Accounting Research, IDEAS abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/joares/v51y2013i3p549-581.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Abstract of JAR 51(3) study of SEC 10-K deadline acceleration (90 to 75 to 60 days). Opponents argued shorter deadlines reduce representational faithfulness, especially for small firms. Smaller firms' 10-K market reaction fell at 90 to 75 days; larger firms' rose at 60 days. Also finds changes in reporting quality; abstract gives no accrual or restatement figures.

Quotes:
- "Opponents countered that the accelerated deadline would decrease the representational faithfulness of the disclosures, especially for smaller firms." — Doyle & Magilke abstract
- "However, as these larger firms accelerate their 10‐K deadline to 60 days, we find a significant increase in the market reaction." — abstract
