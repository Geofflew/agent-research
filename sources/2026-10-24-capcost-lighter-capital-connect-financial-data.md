# Lighter Capital: revenue-based financing underwritten on linked financial data

URL: https://www.lightercapital.com/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: US revenue-based lender for SaaS and tech startups advertises up to $10M, no equity or personal guarantees. Underwriting starts when the borrower links financial information in a secure portal, with a term sheet within days. Eligibility is stated as ARR and customer count, not as audited or reviewed statements. The page does not explicitly say audited financials are not required; that is an inference. Vendor marketing.
