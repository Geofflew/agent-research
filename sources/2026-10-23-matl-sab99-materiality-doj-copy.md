# SEC SAB 99 materiality (copy in DOJ Enron exhibit archive)

URL: https://www.justice.gov/archive/enron/exhibit/05-03/BBC-0001/OCR/EXH021-00231.TXT
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: OCR copy of SAB 99 (sec.gov 403s scorer). 5% rule of thumb ok as first step only. Exclusive quantitative reliance rejected. Qualitative factors list: loss to income, covenants, trends, bonuses. Small amounts can be material.

Quotes:
- "there are numerous circumstances in which misstatements below 5% could well be material" — SEC SAB 99
- "whether the misstatement changes a loss into income or vice versa" — SEC SAB 99 qualitative factors
- "whether the misstatement affects the registrant's compliance with loan covenants or other contractual requirements" — SEC SAB 99 qualitative factors
- "whether the misstatement has the effect of increasing management's compensation" — SEC SAB 99 qualitative factors
