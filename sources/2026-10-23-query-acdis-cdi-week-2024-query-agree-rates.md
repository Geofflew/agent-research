# Q&A: Query metrics and technology (CDI Week 2024 industry data), ACDIS

URL: https://acdis.org/node/69025
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: ACDIS industry survey figures on physician query response, agree rates and "clinically indeterminable" answers, with a practitioner reply. Survey data, not a controlled study; shows agree rates below 100% are normal and that an escape option is used by about a third of respondents. Also shows one program removing the "unable to determine" option in favour of "other" and "dismiss".
