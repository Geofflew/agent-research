# AICPA Code of Professional Conduct, ET 1.295.030 Management Responsibilities

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pub.aicpa.org/codeofconduct/ethicsresources/et-cod.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: Official AICPA Code PDF (updated through Sept 2026). Assuming a management responsibility for an attest client impairs independence with no safeguard; .02 lists examples including authorizing/executing transactions on the client's behalf.

Quotes:
- "the management participation threat would be so significant that no safeguards could reduce the threat to an acceptable level and independence would be impaired" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.030 .01
- "Examples of activities that would be considered management responsibilities and, as such, impair independence if performed for an attest client" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.030 .02
- "authorizing, executing, or consummating transactions or otherwise exercising authority on behalf of an attest client or having the authority to do so" — AICPA Code, ET 1.295.030 .02 item c
