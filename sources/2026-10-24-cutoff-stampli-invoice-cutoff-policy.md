# What is an invoice cutoff policy, and how do I set one for month-end?

URL: https://www.stampli.com/resources/invoice-cutoff-policy/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Stampli AP-automation FAQ on setting a month-end cutoff. Gives the common practice (invoices by last business day, accrual submissions due day 1-2), reminders to budget owners at 5 and 1 business days before cutoff, a controller as sole exception approver, and a stated consequence (next period unless material). Generic guidance, not a company's own policy.

Quotes:
- "Common practice is last business day of the month for invoice receipt, with accrual submissions due day 1-2" — Stampli FAQ
- "remind budget owners 5 and 1 business days before cutoff" — Stampli FAQ
- "after cutoff, it's next period unless material" — Stampli FAQ
- "Publish the calendar, automate the reminders, and route exceptions through one approver (the controller)" — Stampli FAQ
- "Departments stop fighting cutoff when late submissions cost them visible budget timing, not AP goodwill" — Stampli FAQ
