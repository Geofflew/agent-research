# C2FO Early Pay help: Choosing a Pricing Strategy (Express Accept, Trending Rate, Name Your Rate)

URL: http://help.c2fo.com/en/articles/15361667-choosing-a-pricing-strategy
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: C2FO supplier help page (dated June 4, 2026). The supplier picks a pricing strategy per offer: C2FO suggests a one-time "Express Accept" rate optimized for acceptance, a "Trending Rate", or the supplier names its own. The offer is evaluated at a market processing run (typically 11 a.m. Central), not per invoice in real time. Rate is set by C2FO suggestion or the human supplier; buyer side is a program, not an agent.

Quotes:
- "A one-time rate optimized for the best overall acceptance" — C2FO help, Express Accept description
- "A C2FO recommended rate for a competitive offer" — C2FO help, Trending Rate description
- "At market processing (typically 11 a.m. Central)" — C2FO help, when Trending Rate evaluation happens
