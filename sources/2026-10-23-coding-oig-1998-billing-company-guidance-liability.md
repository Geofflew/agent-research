# OIG 1998 compliance guidance for third-party billing companies (Federal Register)

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1998-12-18/html/98-33565.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: OIG guidance for billing/coding vendors. Provider clients exposed to liability when relying on vendor expertise. Vendor program should spot-check coder work. Voluntary guidance. Closest official text on who bears coding-vendor risk.

Quotes:
- "an ineffective compliance program may expose the billing company's provider clients to liability where those providers rely on the billing company's expertise" — OIG, Fed. Reg. 18 Dec 1998
- "spot-checking the work of coding and billing personnel periodically should be an element of an effective compliance program" — OIG, footnote 18
- "the OIG has a longstanding concern that percentage billing arrangements may increase the risk of upcoding and similar abusive billing practices" — OIG, Fed. Reg. 18 Dec 1998, footnote 40
- "for billing companies that provide marketing services, percentage arrangements may implicate the anti-kickback statute" — OIG, Fed. Reg. 18 Dec 1998, footnote 40
