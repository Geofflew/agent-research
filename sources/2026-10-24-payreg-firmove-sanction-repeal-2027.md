# Firmove: end of PIT/CIT sanctions for white-list and split-payment errors from 2027

URL: https://firmove.pl/aktualnosci/biznes/prowadzenie-firmy/news-biala-lista-split-payment-koniec-sankcji-2027
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Trade press (27 Aug 2026) on the government bill (Sejm print 2837, UDER107) repealing from 1 Jan 2027 the loss of tax-cost deductibility for paying over PLN 15,000 to an account outside the white list. Ministry's stated reason: KSeF is now mandatory (1 Feb 2026 largest taxpayers, 1 Apr 2026 others, smallest from 1 Jan 2027). Joint VAT liability stays. Per Sejm API the bill passed 18 Sep 2026 and the President signed 15 Oct 2026.
