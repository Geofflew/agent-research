# Data Act Article 35: Interoperability of data processing services

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/35/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Art. 35 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854: open interoperability specifications and harmonised standards for cloud services aim at interoperability, portability of digital assets and, where feasible, functional equivalence between services of the same type. Standards-setting, not a duty on a vendor to expose write actions to outside agents; aspects listed are portability-focused.

Quotes:
- "enhance portability of digital assets between different data processing services that cover the same service type" — Art. 35(1)(b)
- "facilitate, where technically feasible, functional equivalence between different data processing services" — Art. 35(1)(c)
- "the cloud data portability aspects of data syntactic portability, data semantic portability and data policy portability" — Art. 35(2)(b)
