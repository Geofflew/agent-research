# PCAOB AS 2315: Audit Sampling

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2315
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: Sample size driven by tolerable misstatement (substantive) or tolerable rate + likely deviation rate + risk (controls). Standard itself gives 5% tolerable, 0 deviations, 60 items. PPS named as valid random selection. Nonstatistical sample not smaller than statistical.

Quotes:
- "the auditor should determine tolerable misstatement for the population to be sampled for purposes of designing the sampling plan" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .18a
- "the auditor should take into account tolerable misstatement for the population; the allowable risk of incorrect acceptance" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .23
- "and the characteristics of the population, including the expected size and frequency of misstatements" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .23
- "the auditor should consider the tolerable rate of deviation from the controls being tested, the likely rate of deviations, and the allowable risk of assessing control risk too low" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .38
- "if the tolerable rate for a population is 5 percent and no deviations are found in a sample of 60 items, the auditor may conclude that there is an acceptably low sampling risk" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .41
- "he might decide that a tolerable rate of 5 percent or possibly less would be reasonable" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .34
- "random-based selection includes, for example, random sampling, stratified random sampling, sampling with probability proportional to size, and systematic sampling" — PCAOB AS 2315 footnote 4
- "when a nonstatistical sampling approach is applied properly, the resulting sample size ordinarily will be comparable to, or larger than" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .23a
- "tolerable misstatement for the population to be sampled ordinarily should be less than tolerable misstatement for the account balance or transaction class" — PCAOB AS 2315 para .18a
