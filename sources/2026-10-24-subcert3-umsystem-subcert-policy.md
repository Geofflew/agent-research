# University of Missouri System policy 21105: Internal Control - Sub-Certification Process

URL: https://www.umsystem.edu/ums/policies/finance/internal_control_sub
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: A public university system's own written sub-certification policy, adopted "in compliance with the spirit of Sarbanes-Oxley". Names levels: required campus sub-certifications (chancellor, vice chancellor for administration & finance, assistant VC finance, accounting director); discretionary lower levels (deans, department chairs, fiscal officers) who may certify up to nine assertions on financial information and internal controls. Annual (fiscal year-end), not quarterly. Signers are unit/organisational heads and fiscal officers, not named control owners; no mention of automated controls.

Quotes:
- "the process requires senior officers of the university to formally certify the accuracy and completeness of the financial data generated by their organizational units" — UM System policy 21105, Policy Statement
- "the deans, department chairs, and their respective fiscal officers may be required to certify up to nine assertions as to the financial information and internal controls in the areas for which they are responsible" — UM System policy 21105, Policy Statement
- "senior management at the university of missouri system and at the campuses must place reliance on internal controls that are in place" — UM System policy 21105, Policy Statement
- "in compliance with the spirit of sarbanes-oxley legislation requiring greater public accountability" — UM System policy 21105, Policy Statement
