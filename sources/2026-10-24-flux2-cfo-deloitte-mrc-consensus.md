# CFO.com (Deloitte-authored): Reaching a Consensus on Management Review Controls

URL: https://www.cfo.com/news/reaching-a-consensus-on-management-review-controls/662335/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: CFO.com article by John Fogarty (Feb 25, 2016), a Deloitte-authored piece on PCAOB-driven demands for MRC precision. Says precision must meet materiality and be defined and documented; most companies had not formally set it. Gives a sample deficient description (threshold as greater of $X or Y% of a line item) versus an improved one (explicit thresholds, reviewer expectation of what the balance should be). Relevant: a flux threshold of dollar-and-percent per line is the 'typical' description the article calls not sufficiently precise.

Quotes:

- "The level of review precision needs to satisfy materiality criteria and be clearly defined and documented." — CFO.com (Deloitte-authored), page text
