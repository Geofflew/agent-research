# AICPA: practice aid on AI considerations for financial statement auditors (Oct 2026)

URL: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/news/article/aicpa-releases-guidance-to-help-auditors-address-the-use-of-ai-in-financial-reporting-and-auditing
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: AICPA news release 13 Oct 2026. New practice aid, two sections: client uses AI in reporting/controls; auditor uses AI in audit. Data quantity, quality, accuracy, completeness, relevancy, diversity drive AI reliability. Auditor stays responsible; partners must understand and approve AI tools. Audit-evidence evaluation when client uses AI not yet covered: "added in future". Non-authoritative practice aid, press release summary.

Quotes:
- "how the quantity, quality, accuracy, completeness, relevancy and diversity of data can significantly affect the reliability of an AI system." — AICPA news release, section one of practice aid
- "more guidance, including a deeper dive into assessment of the risk of material misstatement and evaluation of audit evidence when a company is using AI, will be added to section one of the guidance in the future." — AICPA news release
- "outlines the responsibilities of engagement team partners to understand AI tools they're using, ensure they're approved by the firm and are being used correctly by staff." — AICPA news release, section two
- "ultimately, the auditor remains responsible for the audit and the audit opinion." — Jennifer Burns, AICPA chief auditor
