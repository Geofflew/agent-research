# Who should own SOX evidence when IT and finance both touch the process (NHIMG FAQ)

URL: https://nhimg.org/faq/who-should-own-sox-evidence-when-it-and-finance-both-touch-the-process/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Practitioner/vendor FAQ. One named control owner per control (finance for business controls, IT for IT-dependent ones) with contributing owners for evidence; machine-generated output is supporting evidence, not ownership; vendors do not become control owner by default. Closest found to an answer on who stands behind a system-performed control: a named human owner. Not a standard.

Quotes:
- "in those environments, current guidance suggests treating system output as supporting evidence, not ownership" — NHIMG FAQ, edge cases
- "the cleanest model is to assign one named control owner, usually in finance for sox-relevant business controls or in it for it-dependent controls, then define contributing owners for each evidence source" — NHIMG FAQ, how it works in practice
- "vendors may hold part of the evidence, but they do not become the control owner by default" — NHIMG FAQ, edge cases
