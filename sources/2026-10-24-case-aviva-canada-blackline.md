# Aviva Canada: 66% of pilot-unit reconciliations auto-certified (BlackLine)

URL: https://www.blackline.com/customers/aviva-canada/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Vendor case study (BlackLine, data as of March 2015). First task automated was account reconciliation, in a pilot unit; 1,800+ GL accounts, 50 team members, matching/validating GL to third-party data in Excel. Goal was to cut over 50% of manual reconciliations in pilot within a year; reached 66% system-certified. Selection logic is a pilot unit plus low-risk rules-driven recs, no task time-mapping. No hours by task; savings stated in dollars ($9k 2014).
