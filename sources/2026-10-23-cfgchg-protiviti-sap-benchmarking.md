# Develop an Effective SAP Automated Controls Benchmarking Strategy (Protiviti)

URL: https://tcblog.protiviti.com/2022/11/16/develop-an-effective-sap-automated-controls-benchmarking-strategy/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006C

Summary: Practitioner (Protiviti) reading of AS 2201 B28-B33: SAP tolerances are configuration (IMG tables); year one documents benchmarked config, later years only monitor for configuration changes. Re-baseline on significant config change; revenue/disclosure controls often not benchmarkable.

Quotes:
- "Provided that key IT general controls (ITGCs) over the ERP application are operating effectively, an organization may continue to rely on the benchmarked automated controls" — Protiviti blog
- "the auditor may conclude that the automated application control continues to be effective without repeating the prior year’s specific tests of the operation of the automated application control" — Protiviti blog quoting AS 2201 B29
- "It is also important to re-baseline in the event of significant configuration changes in the applications." — Protiviti blog
- "the only activity required in years two and three is monitoring for configuration changes" — Protiviti blog
- "typically around high-risk areas such as an organization’s revenue or other financial disclosures" — Protiviti blog
- "many of these automated controls are established via the SAP Customizing Implementation Guide (IMG) with the associated configuration stored in their respective tables" — Protiviti blog
