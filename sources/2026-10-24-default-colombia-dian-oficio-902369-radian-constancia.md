# DIAN Oficio 902369 de 2022: aceptación tácita, constancia en RADIAN

URL: https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/oficio_dian_902369_2022.htm
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: DIAN doctrine (20 April 2022) on electronic invoice as título valor in RADIAN (Decreto 1154 de 2020, Art. 2.2.2.53.4). The petitioner asks whether invoices lacking the issuer's electronic record in RADIAN of the facts giving rise to tacit acceptance stop being título valor; question 2 restates the 3-business-day rule from receipt of goods or service. Shows the tacit default needs a registered event by the issuer, not only silence. Page is HTML-entity encoded so only ASCII fragments are quoted.

Quotes:
- "al comprador y este no reclame de su contenido dentro de los 3" — DIAN Oficio 902369 de 2022, petitioner's question 2 (gloss: buyer does not claim against the content within 3 business days after receipt of goods/service)
