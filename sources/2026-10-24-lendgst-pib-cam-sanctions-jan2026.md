# PIB: 3.96 lakh MSME loans, Rs 52,300 crore sanctioned by PSBs under digital-footprint CAM (19 Jan 2026)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2216047
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Ministry of Finance release giving 1 Apr-31 Dec 2025 volumes for PSB digital-footprint lending, sourced through the Jan Samarth portal for loans within bank-defined thresholds. Footprints named: GST, ITR, bank statements via AA, CIC reports. Documents are uploaded digitally; no physical copies needed. Silent on audited financials; thresholds mean it covers smaller loans only.

Quotes:
- "over 3.96 lakh MSME loan applications amounting to more than" — PIB, 19 Jan 2026, sanctioned 1 Apr-31 Dec 2025
- "Instant in-principle sanction for MSME loans based on digital footprints of the customer (GST, ITR, Bank A/c Statement, CIC reports etc)." — PIB, Key Features
- "for loans within specific thresholds for loan amounts as defined by banks" — PIB, on Jan Samarth application sourcing
- "no necessity to submit physical copies during approval of the loans" — PIB, Benefits
- "Due diligence is being carried out based on digitally fetched data through various APIs." — PIB, Key Features
