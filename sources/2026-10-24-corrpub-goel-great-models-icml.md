# Great Models Think Alike and this Undermines AI Oversight (Goel et al.; ICML 2025, PMLR 267)

URL: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/goel25b.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Peer-reviewed version of arXiv 2502.04313, published in Proceedings of the 42nd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2025), PMLR vol. 267. Proposes CAPA, a similarity metric from overlap in mistakes; judge scores favor similar models; mistakes grow more similar with capability, a correlated-failure risk for AI oversight.

Quotes:
- "LLM-as-a-judge scores favor models similar to the judge, generalizing recent self-preference results" — Goel et al., PMLR abstract
- "model mistakes are becoming more similar with increasing capabilities, pointing to risks from correlated failures" — Goel et al., PMLR abstract
- "Our work underscores the importance of reporting and correcting for model similarity" — Goel et al., PMLR abstract
