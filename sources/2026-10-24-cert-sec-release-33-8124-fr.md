# SEC Final Rule, Certification of Disclosure in Companies' Quarterly and Annual Reports (Federal Register, 9 Sept 2002)

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2002-09-09/html/02-22572.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Federal Register text of Release 33-8124 implementing Section 302. Certification is based on the officer's knowledge; officers must design disclosure controls so information flows to them from others. No mention of sub-certification found in text.

Quotes:
- "designed such disclosure controls and procedures to ensure that material information relating to the issuer, including its consolidated subsidiaries, is made known to them by others within those entities, particularly during the period in which the periodic reports are being prepared;" — SEC, 67 FR 57276, Release 33-8124, govinfo HTML
- "both of the foregoing certification statements are to be made based on the knowledge of the certifying officer. This is not meant to change the current obligations of corporate officers in connection with the discharge of their duties." — SEC, 67 FR 57276, Release 33-8124, govinfo HTML
- "whether the disclosure is informative and reasonably reflects the underlying transactions and events." — SEC, 67 FR 57276, Release 33-8124, govinfo HTML
