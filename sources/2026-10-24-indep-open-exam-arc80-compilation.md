# 16.3 Compilation Engagements (AR-C 80) (CPA exam study guide)

URL: https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/cpa-aud/ssars-engagements/compilation-engagements-arc-80
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: CPA exam study guide summarizing AR-C 80: independence not required for a compilation, but lack must be disclosed in a final paragraph; reasons optional, but if one is given all must be stated. Study guide, not the standard text.

Quotes:
- "Independence is not required to perform a compilation; however, if the accountant is not independent, that impairment must be explicitly disclosed in the compilation report." — study guide on AR-C 80
- "we are not independent with respect to XYZ Company." — study guide, standard disclosure wording
