# Brokers are asking for higher social engineering fraud limits

URL: https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/ca/news/professional-liability/brokers-are-asking-for-higher-social-engineering-fraud-limits-585525.aspx
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0002F

Summary: Insurance Business Canada interview, Trisura VP (Aug 2026). SEF historically small sublimit; brokers now ask higher limits. Callback provisions verify payment-change requests before money moves. Crime cover framed as backup to controls, not replacement. SEF covers voluntary transfer of money, not goods shipped.

Quotes:
- "Historically, it was handled as a small sublimit, a modest aggregated amount tacked onto a policy." — Insurance Business, paraphrasing Lesley Rowe, Trisura
- "We're seeing brokers now ask for higher limits on that," — Lesley Rowe, Trisura
- "Callback provisions verify payment-change requests before money moves" — Insurance Business, describing Rowe
- "It's not meant to replace controls. It's sort of meant to be a backup," — Lesley Rowe, Trisura
