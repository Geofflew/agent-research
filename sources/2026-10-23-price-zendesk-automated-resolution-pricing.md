# Zendesk pricing page: automated resolutions and seats

URL: https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Zendesk base still seat-based per agent. AI agents billed per automated resolution (no human escalation). Plans bundle ARs scaled per agent; extra ARs pay-as-you-go. Hybrid seat + outcome.

Quotes:
- "Paying per automated resolution means that you pay only for customer requests that were successfully resolved by the AI agent, without any escalation to a human agent." — Zendesk pricing page FAQ
- "Zendesk pricing is primarily seat-based (per agent, per month) with plan tiers that include different features." — Zendesk pricing page FAQ
- "Each plan includes a set number of automated resolutions for AI agents, allowing AI agents to autonomously resolve customer issues without human intervention." — Zendesk pricing page, plan comparison
- "5 ARs per agent/month" — Zendesk pricing page, included automated resolutions row
