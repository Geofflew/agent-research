# Carree, Gomaa, Hunton, Vaassen (2011), Decision aid reliance: reliability and pressure to perform (IJAIS 12(3), abstract)

URL: https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/decision-aid-reliance-modeling-the-effects-of-decision-aid-reliab/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Between-participants experiment, 403 students, aid reliability manipulated at 50/60/70/80/90%. Measures reliance on an accounting-information-systems decision aid, not the reviewer's accuracy on seeded errors. Shows reliance depends on pressure and stated reliability; at high pressure reliance can fall. Students, not company accountants.

Quotes:
- "A total of 403 students took part in a four (pressures to perform: one through four) by five (decision aid reliability: 50%, 60%, 70%, 80% and 90%) between-participants experiment." — abstract
- "continually increasing pressures can eventually lead to decreased, rather than increased, reliance" — abstract
