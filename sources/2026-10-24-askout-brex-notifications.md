# Brex help: notifications (missing receipts, reminders cadence)

URL: https://www.brex.com/support/brex-notifications
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Brex documents the missing-receipt chase cadence: SMS/push 2-7 days after spend, weekly Monday email, repayment reminders at 5/14/30 days; approvers get a Monday digest. Asking for more info is a human approver comment. AI is used to flag policy violations and suggest memos, not to chase.

Quotes:
- "you will receive an email every Monday until action is taken." — Brex Help Center, Missing Receipt or Memo Notifications
- "2-7 days after the expense has been made, you will be reminded to add documentation if needed." — Brex Help Center
- "Notifications are sent 5, 14, and 30 days after the repayment request is initially created." — Brex Help Center
- "Brex AI might even suggest memos based on your transaction." — Brex Help Center
- "you can communicate directly with the employee through comments on the expense or request." — Brex Help Center, Request More Information (approver)
- "Brex utilizes AI and your configured policies to automatically flag expenses that may be out of compliance." — Brex Help Center
