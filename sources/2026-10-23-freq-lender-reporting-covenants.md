# Private credit covenant compliance checklist (reporting deadlines)

URL: https://workwisesolutions.org/guides/covenant-compliance-checklist.html
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Vendor guide for private credit monitoring. Typical loan: monthly financials ~30d, quarterly + compliance cert ~45d, audited annual 90-120d. Late reporting itself a covenant breach signal. Vendor marketing, treat as practitioner norm not survey.

Quotes:
- "a typical loan requires monthly financials within about thirty days, quarterly financials and a compliance certificate within about forty-five, and annual audited statements within roughly ninety to one hundred twenty" — guide, section 5
- "if the financials do not arrive, you cannot test anything" — guide, why reporting covenants feed all others
