# Malaescu & Sutton 2015, Reliance of External Auditors on Internal Audit's Use of Continuous Audit

URL: https://stars.library.ucf.edu/scopus2015/1240
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Abstract only (UCF repository record, J. Information Systems 2015). Experiment, 87 experienced external auditors, 2x2: traditional vs continuous IA x prior-year material weakness absent/present. CA raises willingness to rely, magnified when prior-year ICFR report clean. Audit budget cut only if no prior MW AND CA functioning. Reliance conditional, not automatic. PCAOB cited as encouraging more reliance.

Quotes:
- "we find that auditors are willing to rely more on internal audit work in a continuous audit environment than in a traditional environment" — Malaescu & Sutton, abstract
- "this effect is magnified when the prior year audit report on the effectiveness of internal controls indicates that controls are working properly" — Malaescu & Sutton, abstract
- "In recent years, the PCAOB has encouraged greater such reliance to improve audit efficiency." — Malaescu & Sutton, abstract
- "An experiment is conducted with 87 experienced external auditors" — Malaescu & Sutton, abstract
- "which is reduced only when the company has no prior year material weakness and a functioning continuous audit module is put in place" — Malaescu & Sutton, abstract
