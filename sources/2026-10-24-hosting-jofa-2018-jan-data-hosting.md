# How data-hosting services affect independence (JofA, Jan 2018)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/issues/2018/jan/how-data-hosting-services-affect-independence/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: JofA explainer of new ET 1.295.143 Hosting Services. Hosting = accepting responsibility for maintaining internal control over client data/records; a management responsibility that impairs independence. Lists what is and is not hosting via 10 scenarios (applies to hard copy and electronic).

Quotes:
- "hosting services can impair independence when a CPA takes responsibility for maintaining internal control over an attest client's data or records" — JofA, Jan 2018
- "Be the sole host of a client's financial or nonfinancial information system;" — JofA, Jan 2018
- "Be the custodian for the client's data such that the client's data are incomplete and accessible only through the CPA; or" — JofA, Jan 2018
- "Provide business continuity or disaster recovery services to the client." — JofA, Jan 2018
- "Accepting responsibility to perform a management function creates the threat to independence, and this is stated plainly in the opening sentence of the interpretation." — JofA, Jan 2018
- "The pivotal question is whether the member has accepted responsibility for maintaining custody or control of the information." — JofA, Jan 2018
- "Duffie and ABC separately license and maintain the software on their respective servers." — JofA, Jan 2018, Scenario 6 (not hosting)
- "gives S. Rose, CPA, permission to access XYZ's books via the software to perform bookkeeping services" — JofA, Jan 2018, Scenario 7 (not hosting)
- "she gives B Co. a copy of the schedules and the underlying information" — JofA, Jan 2018, Scenario 9 (not hosting)
- "The new interpretation applies to hard copy and electronic files, as both result in assumption of a management responsibility, which threatens independence." — JofA, Jan 2018
- "The new hosting-services interpretation becomes effective on Sept. 1, 2018" — JofA, Jan 2018 (later moved to July 1, 2019)
