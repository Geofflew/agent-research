# The CPA Journal (Apr 1992): Analytical procedures, quality review of SAS 56

URL: http://archives.cpajournal.com/old/12268860.htm
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: 1992 CPA Journal article by Anthony Mancuso summarizing SAS 56 (forerunner of AU 329 / AS 2305). Lists precision of the expectation as one of four factors in how effective an analytical procedure is; says significant unexpected differences need corroboration and that without explanations the auditor must do other procedures; supporting schedules should satisfy the auditor. Old secondary paraphrase, useful for lineage only.

Quotes:
- "The precision of the expectation." — The CPA Journal (Apr 1992), page text
- "Significant unexpected differences should be evaluated and corroborated with management's responses to inquiries." — The CPA Journal (Apr 1992), page text
- "If explanations cannot be obtained, then sufficient evidence about the assertion should be obtained by performing other procedures" — The CPA Journal (Apr 1992), page text
- "such unexplained differences may indicate an increased risk or material misstatement" — The CPA Journal (Apr 1992), page text
