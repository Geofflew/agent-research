# SSARS AR-C section 90, Review of Financial Statements (independence requirement)

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://irp.cdn-website.com/897bf1f7/files/uploaded/ar-c-00090.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Full text of AICPA AR-C section 90 (SSARS Nos. 21, 23, 24, 25) as reproduced in a PDF hosted by a CPA firm site. Paragraph .10 requires independence for a review and requires withdrawal if independence is impaired. Covers premise 1 (no review report without independence). Reproduction host is not the AICPA; text is the standard's.

Quotes:
- "The accountant must be independent of the entity when performing a review of financial statements in accordance with SSARSs." — AR-C 90.10, Independence
- "If, during the performance of the review engagement, the accountant determines that the accountant's independence is impaired, the accountant should withdraw from the review engagement." — AR-C 90.10, Independence
