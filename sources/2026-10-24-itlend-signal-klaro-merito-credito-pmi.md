# The Signal Media - Un modello fintech per misurare il merito di credito delle pmi (Klaro)

URL: https://www.thesignalmedia.com/un-modello-fintech-per-misurare-il-merito-di-credito-delle-pmi/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: March 2026 press interview with the chair of Klaro, an Italian fintech that builds a certified credit profile for micro-enterprises from open-banking and tax data (e-invoicing, cassetto fiscale) shared by the firm with banks under informed consent. Claims bank underwriting falls from 20-30 days and 4-5 working hours to a faster standard procedure, and targets 3 million firms that do not file accounts. Founder's statements, not independent evidence; no bank customer or volume is named. Closest found to lenders reading authority data on borrower consent, but the firm shares the data and the bank gets a Klaro document, not a direct SdI feed.

Quotes:
- "Non raccogliamo dati dall'esterno. È l'impresa stessa che li condivide, in modo diretto e controllato." — Andrea Mignanelli, Klaro chair, quoted in The Signal
- "Decide cosa condividere, con chi, e per quanto tempo." — The Signal, on the firm's consent control
- "L'incrocio tra open banking e cassetto fiscale crea un dataset sull'impresa che non ha equivalenti in Europa" — Andrea Mignanelli
- "passa da venti-trenta giorni e quattro-cinque ore di lavoro operativo" — Mignanelli, on bank underwriting cost
- "tre milioni di imprese che non depositano bilanci" — Mignanelli, target market
