# Federal Reserve FEDS Note: Private Credit, Characteristics and Risks

URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/private-credit-characteristics-and-risks-20240223.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: Fed staff note Feb 2024. Low private credit defaults credited partly to periodic covenant monitoring. But recent large-corporate deals lack maintenance covenants. Cov-lite creep is a competitive-pressure risk.

Quotes:
- "periodic monitoring of borrowers through loan covenants, as well as the ability to renegotiate flexibly with a relatively small group of creditors when borrowers are in distress" — Federal Reserve Board staff, FEDS Note, private credit defaults section
- "industry commentary suggest recent deals are devoid of financial maintenance covenants as private credit managers look to compete with banks in the large corporate market segment" — Federal Reserve Board staff, FEDS Note, private credit defaults section
- "fund managers might choose riskier deals, offer more covenant-lite loans, or more generally reduce underwriting standards as opportunities dry up when the economy slows down" — Federal Reserve Board staff, FEDS Note, dry powder section
