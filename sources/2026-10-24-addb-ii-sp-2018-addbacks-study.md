# Institutional Investor: S&P Global Warns of Aggressive Private Equity Tactics (S&P 2018 add-back study)

URL: https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/index.php/article/2bsxgei6dn46g3vadxc00/portfolio/s-p-global-warns-of-aggressive-private-equity-tactics
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Press coverage of S&P Global Ratings Sept 2018 study of 2015 deals (S&P pages block fetch). Realized EBITDA 29% (2016) and 34% (2017) below projections. Add-backs 47% of EBITDA in LBOs, 50% for B-rated vs 21% BB. Analyst: 2013 leverage guidance raised add-backs.

Quotes:
- "Ebitda, or earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, turned out to be 29 percent lower in 2016 and 34 percent lower last year" — Idzelis, Institutional Investor, Sept 25 2018
- "add-backs represented 50 percent of Ebitda estimated by companies with B ratings, and 21 percent for those with BB grade" — Idzelis, Institutional Investor
- "There was a notable increase in Ebitda add-backs, both in terms of frequency and magnitude, when the leverage guidelines came out" — S&P analyst Steve Wilkinson, quoted
- "Overstated earnings in 2015 was the primary contributor to a jump in leverage over the past two years" — Idzelis, Institutional Investor
