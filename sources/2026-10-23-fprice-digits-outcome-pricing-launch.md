# Digits outcome-based pricing for accounting firms

URL: https://digits.com/blog/outcome-based-pricing/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005A

Summary: Digits CEO post, 7 Apr 2026. Firms pay per client only if >=95% of transactions zero-touch. Below: free. Finance vendor already prices on outcome = counter to C13. But outcome = no human edit before close; skipped review counts as success. Guarantee is on automation rate, not accuracy.

Quotes:
- "digits is free for that client" — Seibert, Digits blog
- "firms only pay for clients where digits actually eliminates the tedium, meaning 95% or more of that client's transactions are zero-touch" — Seibert
- "these transactions were neither created nor edited by a human accountant before the books were closed" — Seibert, defining zero-touch
- "zero-touch is binary. either your team had to touch a transaction before close, or they didn't." — Seibert
- "starting today, we're changing the business model to guarantee our results" — Seibert
- "in december, a partner firm with 100+ cas clients found their team had touched just 2% of the transactions" — Seibert, customer claim
- "reggie marable, head of sales at sierra, has joined digits as a strategic advisor" — Seibert; Sierra link to support-agent outcome pricing
