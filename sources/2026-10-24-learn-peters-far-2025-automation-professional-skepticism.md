# Auditor Automation Usage and Professional Skepticism (Peters; FAR summary, 2025)

URL: https://foundationforauditingresearch.org/publications/auditor-automation-usage-and-professional-skepticism/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Foundation for Auditing Research summary of Peters (working paper, 2022). Experiment with 119 professional auditors: auditors reduce effort and are less effective reviewing automated work than identical work by a human colleague; a counterarguing mindset intervention offsets this; no spillover to later unautomated tasks (a counter-point to deskilling-by-carryover). Notes the role shift from preparing workpapers to reviewing.
