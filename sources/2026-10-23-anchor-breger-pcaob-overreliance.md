# Dissertation abstract: disaggregate expectations and auditor evaluation of management explanations (Breger 2016, SIU Carbondale)

URL: https://opensiuc.lib.siu.edu/dissertations/1152
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Dissertation abstract. States PCAOB inspections found auditors over-rely on mgmt explanations for fluctuations, no corroborating evidence. Experiment w/ senior auditors tried fix; result counterintuitive (aggregate expectations → MORE evidence gathering). Remedy for explanation over-reliance not simple.

Quotes:
- "Recent Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (PCAOB) inspections on substantive analytical procedures have found that auditors often over-rely on management explanations for significant fluctuations by failing to gather and evaluate corroborating evidence." — abstract
- "In an experiment involving senior auditors, results indicate that auditors are actually more likely to gather and evaluate corroborating evidence when they used aggregated data in developing their expectations." — abstract
