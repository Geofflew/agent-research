# Microsoft Support: machine translation of support pages

URL: https://support.microsoft.com/help/14149/machine-translation-of-microsoft-support-pages
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Microsoft publishes machine-translated support articles to users, vendor admits lower quality and errors. No pre-publication human review; user corrections evaluated by Microsoft expert only before the correction publishes. Raw MT shipped at scale for content users rely on (troubleshooting). Counter to C9 "full human review of every output". Vendor page, undated.

Quotes:
- "machine translated articles, for obvious reasons, are usually of a lower quality than professionally translated ones." — Microsoft Support
- "the machine translation service is designed for users who do not understand english well." — Microsoft Support
- "still, it does produces linguistic and technical errors." — Microsoft Support
- "corrections will be evaluated by a microsoft expert before they get published." — Microsoft Support, on user-proposed corrections
