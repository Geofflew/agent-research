# Insurers eyeing AI risk at CPA firms

URL: https://www.dig-in.com/news/insurers-eyeing-ai-risk-at-cpa-firms
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Apr 2026. Accountant PL insurers: no AI claims yet, no pricing data, no standard questionnaire. Underwriters ask AI policy, protocols, policing. Governance evidence, not error-rate metrics. Cyber underwriting precedent took years.
