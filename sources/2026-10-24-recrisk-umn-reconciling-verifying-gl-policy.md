# University of Minnesota policy: Reconciling and Verifying General Ledger Accounts

URL: https://policy.umn.edu/finance/reconciliation
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Published administrative policy of a public university. Sets a floor of quarterly reconciliation for all balance sheet accounts with preparer and approver, no named risk tiers; leaves depth of reconciliation and review to unit judgement on significance, risk and volume.

Quotes:
- "reconcile balance sheet accounts quarterly (at a minimum) on a timely basis" — UMN Administrative Policy, Policy Statement
- "Balance sheet account reconciliations must have both a preparer and approver review to ensure the transactions are reconciled and appropriate for the account." — UMN Administrative Policy, Policy Statement
- "Units must consider significance of activity, compliance risk, transaction detail volume, knowledge, and experience of personnel performing analyses, in the design and implementation of account reconciliations and the subsequent review process." — UMN Administrative Policy, Policy Statement
