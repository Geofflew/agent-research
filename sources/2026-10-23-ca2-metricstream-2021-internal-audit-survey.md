# MetricStream 2021 internal audit survey (via Help Net Security)

URL: https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2021/07/21/internal-audit-processes/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Vendor-run survey of internal auditors, 2021, reported by trade press. Only 39% implementing or using continuous monitoring/auditing (incl. "in process of implementing"). 60% not agile. 80% still on office tools or point solutions. Vendor sells GRC software, so self-interested; sample size not on page.

Quotes:
- "Only 39% of the respondents said that they are in the process of implementing, or have already implemented continuous monitoring and continuous auditing and are actively using them" — MetricStream survey, as reported by Help Net Security
- "80% of organizations still use office productivity software or point solutions while only 10% of respondents said they use one integrated solution for policy, audit, risk, and compliance management" — MetricStream survey, as reported by Help Net Security
- "60% of internal auditors do not follow an agile approach to internal auditing" — MetricStream survey, as reported by Help Net Security
