# Bansal et al., Does the Whole Exceed its Parts? The Effect of AI Explanations on Complementary Team Performance (CHI 2021)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14779
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Peer-reviewed user studies on three datasets with an AI of accuracy comparable to humans. Explanations raised acceptance of the AI recommendation whether or not it was correct, so they did not improve team accuracy. Abstract only checked here; supports the reliance-on-displayed-AI-output mechanism, not specifically confidence scores.

Quotes:
- "explanations increased the chance that humans will accept the AI's recommendation, regardless of its correctness" — Bansal et al., abstract
- "prior studies observed improvements from explanations only when the AI, alone, outperformed both the human and the best team" — Bansal et al., abstract
