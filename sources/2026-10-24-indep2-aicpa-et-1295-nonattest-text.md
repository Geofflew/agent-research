Summary: Reproduction (municipal meeting packet, via r.jina.ai reader) of AICPA Code Part 1 section 1.295 with AICPA 2024 copyright footers. 1.295.030 says a member assuming a management responsibility for an attest client impairs independence with no safeguard available, and lists examples. 1.295.040 lists the client-side safeguards: assume management responsibilities, designate an overseer with suitable skill, knowledge, and/or experience, evaluate adequacy and results, accept responsibility. Official pub.aicpa.org page is login-gated (not fetchable).

Quotes:
- "the management participation threat would be so significant that no safeguards could reduce the threat to an acceptable level and independence would be impaired" — ET 1.295.030 .01
- "management responsibilities involve leading and directing an entity, including making significant decisions regarding the acquisition, deployment, and control of human, financial, physical, and intangible resources" — ET 1.295.030 .01
- "authorizing, executing, or consummating transactions or otherwise exercising authority on behalf of an attest client or having the authority to do so" — ET 1.295.030 .02 example c
- "deciding which recommendations of the member or other third parties to implement or prioritize" — ET 1.295.030 .02 example f
- "oversee the service, by designating an individual, preferably within senior management, who possesses suitable skill, knowledge, and/or experience" — ET 1.295.040 .01 a.ii
- "evaluate the adequacy and results of the services performed" — ET 1.295.040 .01 a.iii
- "accept responsibility for the results of the services" — ET 1.295.040 .01 a.iv
