# Data Act Article 34: interoperability for in-parallel use of data processing services

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/34/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Art. 34 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 extends the Art. 30(2)-(5) open-interface and export duties to ongoing in-parallel use of data processing services (multi-cloud), not just switching. This is the closest Data Act hook to ongoing access, but it runs between data processing services for portability/interoperability; it grants no customer right to have a third-party agent post or approve transactions in a SaaS ledger, and egress charges (cost-based) remain allowed. The Art. 30(1) functional-equivalence duty is IaaS-only (see dataact-art30 entry). Whether an agent platform is itself a "data processing service" is untested.

Quotes:
- "shall also apply mutatis mutandis to providers of data processing services to facilitate interoperability for the purposes of in-parallel use of data processing services" — Data Act Art. 34(1)
- "the providers of data processing services may impose data egress charges, but only for the purpose of passing on egress costs incurred, without exceeding such costs" — Data Act Art. 34(2)
