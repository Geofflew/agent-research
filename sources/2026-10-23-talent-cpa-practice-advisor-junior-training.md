# CPA Practice Advisor: Stop Training CPAs for Yesterday's Work

URL: https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/?p=189548
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Sep 2026 trade piece. Robert Half: 74% F&A leaders plan to add perm headcount H2 2026; 75% saw skills shortages delay projects. Juniors' routine work was informal training ground; AI absorbs it; most firms no replacement. Proposes review-AI-output as training.

Quotes:
- "Robert Half's latest research found that 74% of finance and accounting leaders plan to increase permanent headcount in the second half of 2026." — CPA Practice Advisor, citing Robert Half
- "75% of leaders saw skills shortages delay projects in the past year, the highest rate of any professional field surveyed." — CPA Practice Advisor, citing Robert Half
- "Those tasks gradually became an informal training ground, exposing new professionals to source documents, engagement processes, and the details behind the work." — CPA Practice Advisor, Stop Training CPAs for Yesterday's Work
- "AI is now absorbing much of that work, improving efficiency while also removing part of the informal learning process that helped junior accountants build experience." — CPA Practice Advisor
- "Most firms have yet to replace that process with a more deliberate development model." — CPA Practice Advisor
- "The most direct way to learn a tool is to audit it." — CPA Practice Advisor
- "Understanding why a reviewer rejected something is the fastest available education in professional standards." — CPA Practice Advisor
