# The Management Review Controls (MRC) Dilemma (AuditBoard / CNM LLP, 2017)

URL: https://www.auditboard.com/blog/the-management-review-control-dilemma
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Vendor blog citing 2016 PCAOB Big Four inspections. 40 of 211 issuer audits (~1 in 5) had MRC deficiencies; top deficiency. Defines MRC as manager reviewing analyst-prepared reconciliation/estimate and signing off. Marketing source; use only for the stat.

Quotes:
- "Out of 211 issuer inspections, the PCAOB identified 40 audits (nearly 1 in 5) with management review control deficiencies." — CNM LLP
- "Management review controls (MRCs) are the top deficiency, followed by failure to test reports." — CNM LLP
- "a manager will review the specific financial document (e.g., bad debt reserve, etc.) prepared by a financial analyst" — CNM LLP
