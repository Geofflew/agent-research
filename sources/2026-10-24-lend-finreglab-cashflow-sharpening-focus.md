# FinRegLab / NYU Stern: Sharpening the Focus - Using Cash-Flow Data to Underwrite Financially Constrained Businesses (Jun 2025)

URL: https://finreglab.org/?p=3247
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: US non-profit research (with NYU Stern) on two fintech lenders: electronic bank cash-flow data adds substantial predictive signal over owner credit scores and firm characteristics, most for young firms and low-score owners. Evidence that lenders underwrite from transaction data, not borrower-prepared statements; US, small business, bank-account feeds not tax data. Funded in part by US Commerce MBDA and Visa; Plaid provided connectivity.

Quotes:
- "adding cash-flow information substantially increases the predictive signal of models that rely primarily on the business owners' personal credit scores and firm characteristics" — source page
- "The effects are larger for low-score owners whose businesses are young (less than five years old)." — FinRegLab report summary
- "Plaid provided account connectivity services to the pilot programs by mission-based lenders to allow loan applicants to authorize their account data to be accessed for underwriting" — source page
