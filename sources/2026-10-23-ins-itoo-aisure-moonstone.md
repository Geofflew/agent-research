# iTOO on pricing aiSure AI insurance

URL: https://www.moonstone.co.za/when-ai-becomes-the-risk-who-will-insure-it/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Trade interview, iTOO MD, aiSure South Africa launch with Munich Re. Underwriting: agree measurable KPI (accuracy/error threshold), assess data, training, track record, set triggers. 98pct fraud-detection example. No local claims history; rely on Munich Re data; start 5pct share.

Quotes:
- "the product starts by agreeing measurable performance indicators - for example, an accuracy rate or an acceptable error threshold" — Moonstone interview with iTOO managing director Justin Naylor
- "underwriters then assess the model's data, training, and historical performance before setting the policy triggers" — Moonstone interview with iTOO managing director Justin Naylor
- "the bank and the developer could agree that the model should identify 98% of fraudulent transactions" — Moonstone interview with iTOO managing director Justin Naylor
- "it turns a question about how reliable an ai system is into something that can be tested, underwritten, and attached to a financial consequence" — Moonstone interview with iTOO managing director Justin Naylor
- "we know we can have losses. we don't know what they'll be" — Moonstone interview with iTOO managing director Justin Naylor
- "a new product might start with the insurer taking about 5% of the risk" — Moonstone interview with iTOO managing director Justin Naylor
- "it issued its first ai policy in 2018" — Moonstone interview with iTOO managing director Justin Naylor
