# EY India: How shared service platforms add value to private equity firms

URL: https://www.ey.com/en_in/insights/consulting/global-capability-centers/how-shared-service-platforms-add-value-to-private-equity-firms
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: EY (consultancy, marketing-adjacent thought leadership) argues PE firms can run common shared-services platforms across portfolio companies, including finance and accounting. Savings are projections, not measured. Mentions cross-charging: the sponsor sells services to its portfolio companies.

Quotes:
- "Over a five-year period, projected savings include 50% to 60% through labor arbitrage, 10% to 15% through centralization and standardization initiatives, and 12% to 18% through automation and digitization." — EY, savings section
- "Shared services also unlock new revenue streams through cross-charging models, where PE firms offer specialized services to their portfolio companies." — EY, benefits section
- "Core functions such as finance and accounting, human resources, information technology, procurement and taxation across industries have a strong potential for offshoring." — EY
