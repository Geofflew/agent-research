# Nasdaq kill switch and pre-trade risk management tool (Nasdaq Rule 6130 filing, Federal Register 2014)

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2014-02-24/html/2014-03781.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: SEC notice of SR-NASDAQ-2014-017, 24 Feb 2014 (79 FR 10218). Exchange-level per-MPID net notional exposure limit; alerts at 50/75/85/90/95 pct; on breach ports disabled, open orders cancelled, re-auth by Nasdaq ops after explanation. Also describes Nasdaq PRM order-level checks built for 15c3-5.

Quotes:
- "enables participants to establish a pre-determined level of net notional risk exposure" — Nasdaq proposed Rule 6130(a), kill switch
- "to have order entry ports disabled and open orders administratively cancelled when the value of executed orders exceeds the nnre level" — Nasdaq proposed Rule 6130(a)
- "the participant must request reactivation of the mpid before trading will be reauthorized" — Nasdaq proposed Rule 6130(d)
- "participants will be required to explain why a kill switch was triggered and why it is safe for the exchange to re-authorize the mpid for order entry" — Nasdaq filing, purpose section
