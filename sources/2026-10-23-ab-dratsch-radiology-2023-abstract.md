Summary: Radiology 2023 abstract, doi 10.1148/radiol.222176. 27 radiologists, 50 mammograms, purported AI BI-RADS advice. 12 of 40 test cases wrong advice. Accuracy collapses at all experience levels w/ wrong AI. Bias worst for inexperienced. Replaces RSNA release.
