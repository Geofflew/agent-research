# Fan, Thomas, Yu: The Impact of Financial Covenants in Private Loan Contracts on Classification Shifting (Management Science 2019, abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v65y2019i8p3637-3653.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Peer-reviewed abstract. Opposite channel: borrowers near EBITDA-covenant violation misclassify core expenses as special items (reported-earnings management) to avoid violation. Shows the reported-earnings route to EBITDA slack coexists with contractual-definition route.

Quotes:
- "misclassifying core expenses as income-decreasing special items allows the firm to increase EBITDA and thereby potentially avoid debt covenant violations." — Fan, Thomas, Yu, abstract
- "firms misclassify core expenses as special items when at least one EBITDA-related financial covenant is close to being violated." — Fan, Thomas, Yu, abstract
- "classification shifting appears to be an additional" — Fan, Thomas, Yu, abstract (continues 'earnings management technique')
