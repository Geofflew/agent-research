# Route Fifty: machine translations, good enough for government work?

URL: https://route-fifty.com/management/2015/05/machine-translations-good-enough-for-government-work/286910
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: 2015 news. US agencies (NH Revenue, VA DBVI, Fairfax schools) post raw Google Translate with disclaimers; only English version relied on in decisions. DOL presentation: MT seldom, if ever, sufficient without trained human reviewer. Risk-tiered: raw MT for informational web content with disclaimer, human review where legal duty. Old (2015) and agency-level, not a formal rule.

Quotes:
- "it is seldom, if ever, sufficient to use machine translation without having a human who is trained in translation available to review and correct the translation to ensure that it is conveying the intended message" — Department of Labor presenters, quoted in Route Fifty
- "only the English version will be relied on by the department in its decision making and in court." — VA DBVI website disclaimer, quoted in Route Fifty
