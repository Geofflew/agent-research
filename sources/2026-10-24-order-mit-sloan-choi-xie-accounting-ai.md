# How generative AI can make accountants more productive (MIT Sloan, 5 Aug 2025)

URL: https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/how-generative-ai-can-make-accountants-more-productive
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: MIT Sloan summary of Choi (Stanford) and Xie (MIT) field study: 79 SMEs, 277 accountants, AI bookkeeping software. Tasks AI does: transaction classification, contract summary, anomaly detection. Adopters closed 7.5 days faster (association, self-selected). Only ~10% of surveyed accountants used AI daily, ~10% not at all. Accountants over-follow AI on uncertain categories. SMB bookkeeping context, not enterprise close.

Quotes:
- "it can classify transactions, summarize contracts, and detect anomalies in bookkeeping." — MIT Sloan, Zach Church, 5 Aug 2025
- "a 7.5 day reduction in monthly close time." — MIT Sloan, Zach Church, 5 Aug 2025
- "about 10% of whom were using ai in their daily routines; another 10% had not experimented with ai at all." — MIT Sloan, Zach Church, 5 Aug 2025
- "when ai suggests diverging categories for uncertain transactions, accountants tend to still follow ai's suggestions" — MIT Sloan, Zach Church, 5 Aug 2025
- "62% who were worried about errors and accuracy in ai-generated reporting" — MIT Sloan, Zach Church, 5 Aug 2025
- "reallocation of approximately 8.5% of accountant time from routine data entry" — MIT Sloan, Zach Church, 5 Aug 2025
