# CBC: Canada Translation Bureau to cut a quarter of workforce (Mar 2025)

URL: https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ottawa/translation-bureau-to-cut-a-quarter-of-its-workforce-over-next-5-years-1.7488601
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: CBC 20 Mar 2025. Federal Translation Bureau business plan 2025-30: cut 339 positions (~1/4 of ~1,350) via attrition. PSPC says projected demand decrease; fall economic statement frames bureau text repository as AI training data. Hard headcount fall at large institutional in-house translation shop. Union: quality decline; AI not ready to replace a human translator.

Quotes:
- "a federal public service union is condemning a move by canada's translation bureau to cut its workforce by one-quarter over the next five years." — CBC, 20 Mar 2025
- "according to cape, the bureau plans to cut 339 positions over that period through attrition." — CBC, on CAPE union statement
- "pspc spokesperson michèle larose said the reduction is a response to a projected decrease in demand for the translation bureau's services." — CBC, PSPC spokesperson
- "listed the bureau's workforce at roughly 1,350 employees." — CBC, on 2024 PSPC LinkedIn post
