# PIB: GSTR-3B staggered due dates (22 Jan 2020)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1600249&reg=48&lang=2
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Finance Ministry release stating the base GSTR-3B due date is the 20th of every month, then staggering smaller filers (22nd or 24th by state). Gives filer counts by band: about 8 lakh above Rs 5 crore, 49 lakh in 15 states, 46 lakh in 22 states/UTs (roughly 1 crore filers). Older (2020) but the 20th remains the monthly date for large filers.

Quotes:
- "Presently the last date of filing GSTR-3B returns for every taxpayer is 20 th of every month." — PIB, Ministry of Finance, 22 Jan 2020
- "Thus, around 8 lakh regular taxpayers would have the last date of GSTR-3B filing as 20 th of every month without late fees." — PIB, Ministry of Finance
- "This category would have around 49 lakh GSTR-3B filers" — PIB, on the 22nd-of-month band
- "For the remaining 46 lakh taxpayers from the 22 States/UTs" — PIB, on the 24th-of-month band
