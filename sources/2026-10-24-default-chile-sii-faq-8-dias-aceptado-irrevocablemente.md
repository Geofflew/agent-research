# SII FAQ: acuse de recibo para uso de crédito fiscal (silence = irrevocable acceptance)

URL: https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/factura_electronica/001_003_6605.htm
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Chile SII FAQ (updated 25/06/2025). Buyer must register acceptance or claim of each electronic tax document (DTE) on the SII site; otherwise the document is deemed irrevocably accepted once 8 days pass from its receipt in the SII database. Silence is the default outcome and it is legally binding.

Quotes:
- "irrevocablemente aceptado el documento" — SII FAQ 001.003.6605.008, answer text (gloss: otherwise the document is understood irrevocably accepted after 8 days from receipt in the SII database)
