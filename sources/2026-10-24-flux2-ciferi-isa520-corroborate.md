# Ciferi: ISA 520 Analytical Procedures, a practical guide (quotes ISA 520.7)

URL: https://ciferi.com/blog/isa-520-analytical-procedures-guide
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Audit-training firm guide reflecting the IAASB 2024 Handbook text of ISA 520. Restates ISA 520.7 (inquire of management AND obtain appropriate audit evidence relevant to management's responses) and states management's explanation alone is not sufficient; a verbal explanation does not meet ISA 520.7. Also says threshold must be set before comparing and not exceed performance materiality. Commentary, not the standard itself; no fetchable HTML copy of ISA 520 verbatim was found.

Quotes:
- "the auditor must inquire of management and obtain appropriate audit evidence relevant to management" — Ciferi, page text
- "Management's explanation alone is not sufficient. The auditor must corroborate it." — Ciferi, page text
- "That is inquiry without corroboration, and it is the single most common reason analytics fail inspection." — Ciferi, page text
- "A verbal explanation alone does not meet ISA 520.7" — Ciferi, page text
- "The threshold must not exceed performance materiality and must be low enough to detect material misstatements." — Ciferi, page text
- "Set and document the threshold before performing the comparison." — Ciferi, page text
