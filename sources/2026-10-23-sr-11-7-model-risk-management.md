# Model risk management guidance (Fed SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12), as adopted by FDIC FIL-22-2017

URL: https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2017/fil17022.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: FDIC letter adopting SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12 guidance verbatim (Fed letter HTML has no body text; OCC pages 404; guidance PDFs unusable). Sets effective challenge as guiding principle; senior mgmt responsible, internal audit checks. Framework needs development, implementation, use, validation, governance. Applies to FDIC banks >= $1B assets unless model use significant. Independent validation/ongoing monitoring wording not on this page.

Quotes:
- "the fdic is adopting the supervisory guidance on model risk management previously issued by the board of governors of the federal reserve system" — FDIC FIL-22-2017, Summary (SR 11-7 and OCC Bulletin 2011-12)
- "critical analysis by objective, informed parties who can identify model limitations and assumptions and produce appropriate changes." — FDIC FIL-22-2017, quoting the guidance definition of effective challenge
- "it is expected to be senior management's responsibility to ensure effective challenge takes place, and internal audit should ensure that appropriate effective challenge is being carried out." — FDIC FIL-22-2017
- "an effective model risk management framework should include: disciplined and knowledgeable development that is well documented and conceptually sound, controls to ensure proper implementation, processes to ensure correct and appropriate use, effective validation processes, and strong governance, policies, and controls." — FDIC FIL-22-2017, Highlights
