# Zendesk pricing: automated resolutions

URL: https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005C

Summary: Zendesk AI agents billed per automated resolution: $1.50 committed, $2.00 pay-as-you-go. Action Builder credits $200/100K actions. Seat pricing remains for humans ($19-$115/agent/mo) - hybrid seat + outcome.

- "Action credits $200 per 100K actions/subscription term" — Zendesk, pricing page
- "Suite Professional For teams optimizing support operations with advanced automation and AI-driven insights. $115 agent/month paid yearly" — Zendesk, pricing page
