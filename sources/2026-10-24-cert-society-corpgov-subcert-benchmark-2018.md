# Benchmarking: SOX 302 Sub-Certifications (Society for Corporate Governance quick survey)

URL: https://www.societycorpgov.org/blogs/randi-morrison/2018/01/03/benchmarking-sox-302-sub-certifications
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Quick survey of about 100 issuer members: number of sub-certifiers per filing, forms used, which roles sub-certify (accounting, tax, treasury, internal audit, legal, IR).

Quotes:
- "the number of individuals at respondent companies required to provide a sub-certification for each Form 10-Q and Form 10-K to support the SOX 302 CEO & CFO certifications is most commonly 11 - 20 (24% of respondents), followed by 50+ (19% of respondents)" — Randi Morrison, Society for Corporate Governance, 3 Jan 2018
- "38% of respondents have a single version/form of sub-certification that all Disclosure Committee (DC) members are asked to sign" — Randi Morrison, Society for Corporate Governance, 3 Jan 2018
