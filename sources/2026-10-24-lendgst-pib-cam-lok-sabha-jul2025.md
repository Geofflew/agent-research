# PIB: New Digital Credit Assessment Model leverages real-time digital data (Lok Sabha reply, 28 Jul 2025)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2149373
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: MoS Finance written reply in Lok Sabha. Contrasts manual underwriting on physical documents with fully digital submission and assessment; model live at all banks with differing loan-amount thresholds; decisions within at most one day; 98,995 applications sanctioned 1 Apr-15 Jul 2025. Eligibility criteria unchanged; bank rule engines still apply each bank's credit risk policy.

Quotes:
- "Under Traditional / Manual methods, banks rely on physical documents submitted by customers for manual underwriting." — PIB, MoS Finance reply, 28 Jul 2025
- "credit request and data submission as well as assessment is done entirely through digital process" — PIB, 28 Jul 2025
- "The model is live with all banks with different loan amount threshold." — PIB, 28 Jul 2025
- "a total of 98,995 MSME loan applications have been sanctioned by the Public Sector Banks (PSBs) under New Credit Assessment Model" — PIB, for 1 Apr-15 Jul 2025
- "decided within maximum of upto one day significantly reducing the turn around time" — PIB, 28 Jul 2025
- "does not involve any fundamental changes in the basic eligibility criteria for MSME loans" — PIB, 28 Jul 2025
- "Business Rule Engines (BREs) of banks will capture all risks as per its credit risk management policy." — PIB, 28 Jul 2025
