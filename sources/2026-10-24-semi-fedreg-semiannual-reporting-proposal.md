# Semiannual Reporting, SEC proposed rule (Federal Register, 91 FR, 7 May 2026, govinfo text)

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-05-07/html/2026-09095.htm
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: SEC proposing release, File S7-2026-15, FR doc 2026-09095. Opt-in Form 10-S, one per year plus 10-K. Deadline 40/45 days same as 10-Q. Six-month statements need auditor review not audit (Rule 10-01(d)/8-03). Comments due 6 Jul 2026. Cost section: Nasdaq 2019 survey avg $334,698/quarter, median $75,000; interim review ~6% of annual audit fee per quarter. Proposal only; no adoption found.
