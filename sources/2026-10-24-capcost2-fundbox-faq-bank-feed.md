# Fundbox FAQ: application needs a connected business bank account

URL: https://www.fundbox.com/faq
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Vendor FAQ of a US small-business lender (line of credit, invoice financing, revenue-based financing). Underwriting input is 3 months of bank transactions via Plaid, kept connected for the loan life; no financial statements or audit named. A live data feed substituting for statements at small size, but the page gives no loan size or price.

Quotes:
- "You will need a business checking account with at least 3 months of transactions." — Fundbox FAQ, What do I need for my application
- "Fundbox uses a third party data provider, Plaid, to initially connect, and remain connected with your bank account." — Fundbox FAQ
- "Fundbox ingests a wide variety of data from its partners, including but not limited to cashflow data found in bank accounts, payments and/or invoicing transactions." — Fundbox FAQ, What data is required
