# Accounting ethics in the age of AI (Journal of Accountancy, Aug 2026)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/issues/2026/aug/accounting-ethics-in-the-age-of-ai/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: JofA (Kelly Mullins, 5 Aug 2026): Code has no AI-specific independence rule; existing nonattest and information-systems rules apply; fall back on conceptual framework (identify threats, evaluate, safeguard, document). Addresses building/hosting AI for attest clients, not firm use of agents to keep client books.

Quotes:
- "providing AI-related nonattest services, such as developing or hosting an AI system for an attest client, can raise independence concerns. The existing rules on nonattest services and information systems still apply." — JofA, Aug 2026
- "In situations the Code doesn't explicitly address, fall back on the conceptual framework: identify threats, evaluate their significance, and apply safeguards as needed." — JofA, Aug 2026
