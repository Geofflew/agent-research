# La Trobe University Balance Sheet Reconciliation Policy (repealed version 2)

URL: https://policies.latrobe.edu.au/document/view.php?id=288&version=2
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Public university policy (marked repealed, but a real operating policy) with four risk tiers: Critical monthly, Important quarterly, Static 6-monthly, Annual accounts yearly. Frequency set by Corporate Finance and CFO by risk assessment; a master listing names preparer and authoriser per reconciliation. Every tier still has preparer and authoriser; CFO review is periodic.

Quotes:
- "The requirement of 'when' to prepare balance sheet reconciliations is determined by Corporate Finance in consultation with the Chief Financial Officer according to an assessment of risk." — La Trobe policy, Part A
- "Static: 6 Monthly Reconciliation completed 6-monthly before 5th work day after month-end close." — La Trobe policy, risk table
- "Annual Accounts: Yearly Reconciliation completed yearly before 5th work day after year-end close." — La Trobe policy, risk table
- "Any new balance sheet accounts created within the accounting period." — La Trobe policy, list of accounts assessed Critical
- "This master listing will be reviewed and updated monthly (attached)." — La Trobe policy, clause 8
- "cannot be the same person." — La Trobe policy, Part B
