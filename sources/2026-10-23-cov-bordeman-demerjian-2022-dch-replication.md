# Bordeman & Demerjian (2022), Do borrowers intentionally avoid covenant violations? (abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/joares/v60y2022i5p1741-1774.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: JAR replication of Dichev & Skinner, 2000-2019 extension. Threshold-avoidance inference is robust overall but sensitive to histogram bin width, and unsupported for debt-to-EBITDA, the most common financial covenant. Counter-evidence to a clean bunching premise.

Quotes:
