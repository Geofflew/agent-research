# Faria-e-Castro, Gopalan, Pal, Sanchez, Yerramilli (2022), EBITDA Add-backs in Debt Contracting: A Step Too Far? (St. Louis Fed WP 2022-029, abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/p/fip/fedlwp/94811.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Fed working paper abstract. Syndicated-loan covenants use non-GAAP EBITDA. 2013 leveraged lending guidance shock raised add-backs; add-backs raise delinquency, default, downgrade odds. Arrangers sell down more and monitor less. Abstract only.

Quotes:
- "Financial covenants in syndicated loan agreements often rely on definitions of EBITDA that deviate from the GAAP definition." — Fed WP 2022-029 abstract
- "we show that these addbacks increase the likelihood of loan delinquency and default, and also increase the likelihood of the borrower experiencing a ratings downgrade" — Fed WP 2022-029 abstract
- "Greater use of non-GAAP EBITDA addbacks also makes it more likely that lead arrangers lower their loan share exposures through secondary market sales." — Fed WP 2022-029 abstract
- "covenants based on customized measures of EBITDA hurt loan performance by worsening lead arrangers’ incentives to monitor borrowers and by hampering their ability to take timely corrective actions" — Fed WP 2022-029 abstract
