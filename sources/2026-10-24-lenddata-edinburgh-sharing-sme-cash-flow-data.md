# Sharing SME Cash Flow Data (UK CCDS open-banking policy)

URL: https://www.ecfc.business-school.ed.ac.uk/sites/ecfc/files/2023-05/sharing_sme_cash_flow_data.pdf
Type: primary
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: Working paper (Jan 2023), University of Edinburgh. Difference-in-differences on the UK policy forcing nine banks to share SME current-account data with other lenders. Non-banks lend more and SMEs form new relationships with them; effect holds with credit history already observable. Measures lending relationships, not rates or defaults.

Quotes:
- "This evidence suggests that open banking can alleviate the hold-up problem, and stimulate competition in the SME lending market." — authors, abstract
- "increases the probability to form a new borrowing relationship for a firm by 1.38 percentage points (i.e. 26% compared to the mean)" — authors, section 4.1
- "This result holds even if firms’ credit histories are already observable by other lenders, suggesting a unique role of cash flow information." — authors, abstract
- "The policy has modest real effects, as affected firms increase their liquidity buffers rather than investment." — authors, abstract
