# FloQast blog: Strict Tie-Out Mode (2018)

URL: https://www.floqast.com/blog/strict-tie-out-mode
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: 2018 feature post. System blocks sign-off until the reconciliation has tied out to the trial balance (within the admin's materiality threshold); if it later goes out of balance, prior signers are notified and must re-sign. Tie-out is the system's gate, so the signer does not have to re-foot. Dated; check against current behavior.

Quotes:
- "prevents errors by allowing signatures only after a reconciliation has" — FloQast blog
- "if a previously tied-out reconciliation goes out of balance, any user who signed off on the reconciliation is notified of the change" — FloQast blog
- "within the administrator's prescribed materiality threshold." — FloQast blog, footnote
