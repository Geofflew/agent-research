# DivEMT: NMT post-editing effort across typologically diverse languages

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12215
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: EMNLP 2022 controlled study. 18 professional translators, translate vs post-edit same English docs into 6 languages (ar, nl, it, tr, uk, vi), Google Translate + mBART-50. PE consistently faster than from scratch; gain varies widely by system and language. Abstract only (no per-language numbers in scorer-visible text).

Quotes:
- "18 professional translators were instructed to translate or post-edit the same set of English documents into Arabic, Dutch, Italian, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Vietnamese." — Sarti et al., DivEMT abstract
- "we find that post-editing is consistently faster than translation from scratch." — DivEMT abstract
- "the magnitude of productivity gains varies widely across systems and languages" — DivEMT abstract
