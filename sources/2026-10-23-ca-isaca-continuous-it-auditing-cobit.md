# ISACA Journal 2020, Defining Targets for Continuous IT Auditing Using COBIT 2019

URL: https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2020/volume-5/defining-targets-for-continuous-it-auditing-using-cobit-2019
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: ISACA Journal vol 5, 28 Aug 2020, Ian Cooke. Defines CA vs continuous monitoring: difference = process owner (audit vs management). Perceived benefit: periodic sample review to 100 pct transaction testing. Needs tech tools, risk indicators, two+ clashing data sources. Practitioner view, not standard.

Quotes:
- "continuous auditing is not the same as continuous monitoring." — Cooke, ISACA Journal 2020
- "the key difference is the owner of the process. continuous monitoring is a management process that monitors whether internal controls are operating effectively on an ongoing basis." — Cooke, ISACA Journal 2020
- "one of the key perceived benefits was the change from periodic reviews of a sample of transactions to ongoing audit testing of 100 percent of transactions." — Cooke, ISACA Journal 2020
- "continuous auditing is performed by audit and is designed to enable the internal auditor to report on subject matter within a much shorter time frame than under the traditional retrospective approach" — Cooke, ISACA Journal 2020
