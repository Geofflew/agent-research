# Macquarie University Balance Sheet Account Reconciliation Procedure (BlackLine auto-certification clause)

URL: https://policies.mq.edu.au/document/view.php?id=225&version=1
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Public university procedure (marked repealed) that writes auto-certification into policy. Monthly reconciliation only for sub-ledger accounts, balances over $10m and designated high-risk; all others quarterly. Accounts with predictable balances may be auto-certified in BlackLine within a $10,000 tolerance, each needing the Director's approval and a twice-yearly written re-confirmation. A rotational independent review samples mainly the monthly (high-risk) set.

Quotes:
- "All other Balance Sheet accounts must be reconciled at least quarterly." — Macquarie procedure, Frequency of Reconciliations
- "Certain Balance Sheet Account Reconciliations have expected balances that can be predicted in advance due to their inherent nature." — Macquarie procedure, Pre-set Expectations
- "Blackline includes a feature to load expected balances and auto-certify accounts which are within a tolerance of this balance." — Macquarie procedure, Pre-set Expectations
- "Tolerance is set at $10,000 per Balance Sheet Account Reconciliation." — Macquarie procedure, Pre-set Expectations
- "Use of this feature within Blackline requires approval by the Director, Financial Control and Treasury for each impacted Balance Sheet Account." — Macquarie procedure
- "are required to confirm in writing to the Director, Financial Control and Treasury bi-annually that the preloaded expected balance is still appropriate" — Macquarie procedure
- "if a zero balance, confirmation that the account is appropriate to be zero" — Macquarie procedure, Content of Reconciliations
- "Reviews will focus on reconciliations that are required to be reviewed monthly because of their inherent nature or risk profile" — Macquarie procedure, Independent Rotational Quality Reviews
