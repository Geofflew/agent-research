# AICPA independence rules for information system services (JofA, Mar 2018, exposure draft)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/news/2018/mar/aicpa-independence-rules-for-information-system-services-201818577/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: JofA report on PEEC exposure draft revising ET 1.295.145. Lists operating activities that impair independence (monitoring network performance, managing systems or software applications, help desk, ongoing maintenance, security) vs discrete one-off services that do not.

Quotes:
- "If the member takes on a management responsibility for an ongoing function, process, or activity, management participation and self-review threats impair independence." — JofA, Mar 2018
- "Manages systems or software applications;" — JofA, Mar 2018 (impairing)
- "Monitors network performance;" — JofA, Mar 2018 (impairing)
- "Maintains the security of the attest client's networks and systems." — JofA, Mar 2018 (impairing)
- "if the member does not assume management responsibilities and the service is part of a discrete, nonrecurring activity, independence would not be impaired" — JofA, Mar 2018
- "Greater clarity on the types of network maintenance activities members may perform, with a focus on performing one-off services versus continuing maintenance, will likely lead to more activities impairing independence than the existing standard." — JofA, Mar 2018
