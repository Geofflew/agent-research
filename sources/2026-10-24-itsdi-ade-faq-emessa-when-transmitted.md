# Agenzia delle Entrate FAQ: an invoice is "emessa" when transmitted to SdI

URL: https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/schede/comunicazioni/fatture-e-corrispettivi/faq-fe/risposte-alle-domande-piu-frequenti-categoria/modalita-di-trasmissione-delle-fatture-elettroniche
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Authority FAQ, quoting art. 1(3) D.Lgs. 127/2015: B2B supplies and their variations are issued exclusively as e-invoices via SdI. An invoice counts as issued on the date it is transmitted to SdI, which fixes VAT register entry and the tax period of revenue (example: sent 8 Jan 2024, so 2024). Issuance is therefore a dated act recorded by the authority.

Quotes:
- "sono emesse esclusivamente fatture elettroniche utilizzando il sistema di interscambio" — AdE FAQ, quoting art. 1 c. 3 D.Lgs. 127/2015
- "una fattura si ha per emessa quando la stessa risulta trasmessa al sistema di interscambio" — AdE FAQ
- "si considera emessa in tale data" — AdE FAQ, invoice transmitted to SdI on 8 January 2024
