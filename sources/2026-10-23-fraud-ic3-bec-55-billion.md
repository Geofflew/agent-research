# FBI IC3 PSA: Business Email Compromise, The $55 Billion Scam

URL: https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA240911
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: FBI IC3 BEC cumulative stats Oct 2013-Dec 2023. 305k incidents, $55.5B exposed loss. Funds routed via custodial/payment-processor/crypto accounts. Core prevention tip: secondary channel verify of account-info changes.

Quotes:
- "Between December 2022 and December 2023, there was a 9% increase in identified global exposed losses." — FBI IC3, Statistical Data
- "Domestic and international incidents: 305,033 Domestic and international exposed dollar loss: $55,499,915,582" — FBI IC3, Statistical Data
- "Total U.S. victims: 158,436 Total U.S. exposed dollar loss: $20,089,561,364" — FBI IC3, Statistical Data
- "the BEC scam has been reported in all 50 states and 186 countries" — FBI IC3, Statistical Data
- "Use secondary channels and/or two-factor authentication to verify requests for changes in account information." — FBI IC3, Recommended Prevention Tips
- "If you discover a fraudulent transfer, time is of the essence." — FBI IC3, How to Protect Yourself/Business
