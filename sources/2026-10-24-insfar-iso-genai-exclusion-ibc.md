# ISO's generative AI exclusion is already on thousands of CGL policies

URL: https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/cyber/isos-generative-ai-exclusion-is-already-on-thousands-of-cgl-policies-589971.aspx
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0002F

Summary: Trade press on Verisk/ISO GenAI exclusions for CGL, available Jan 2026: CG 40 47 01 26 (broad), CG 40 48 (ad injury), CG 35 08 (products/completed ops). Berkley, Chubb, Travelers, Berkshire, AIG filed adopting by April. 2,369 exclusion records, 49 states. Berkley absolute AI exclusion in D&O/E&O/fiduciary. No admitted replacement product. CGL only; no crime-policy exclusion stated.

Quotes:
- "ISO endorsement CG 40 47 01 26 became available for carrier attachment to commercial general liability policies in January." — Insurance Business US
- "Two narrower variants also took effect: CG 40 48 removes advertising injury only" — Insurance Business US
- "2,369 generative AI exclusion records already in force" — Insurance Business US, citing nationwide state-filings review
- "W.R. Berkley has introduced an absolute AI exclusion across its D&O, E&O, and fiduciary products." — Insurance Business US
- "found no admitted standalone product available to replace the coverage the exclusions remove" — Insurance Business US
- "Organizations that can demonstrate human oversight of AI outputs and formal approval protocols are better positioned on coverage and terms." — Insurance Business US, on Aon 2026 AI risk report
