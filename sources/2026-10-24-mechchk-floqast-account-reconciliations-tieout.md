# FloQast Account Reconciliations product page (prep, tie-out, auto sign-off)

URL: https://www.floqast.com/close-management/products/account-reconciliations
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Product page. FloQast pulls the GL balance from the ERP, stores support, alerts owners if the GL shifts, auto-signs-off unchanged and zero-balance accounts, and uses materiality thresholds to confirm each rec ties. Anything that does not tie becomes a tracked item. Reviewer and preparer roles stay separate via strict sign-off. Marketing page; says nothing on whether a rec can complete with no person beyond zero/unchanged accounts.

Quotes:
- "auto sign-offs maintain completeness for unchanged and zero balance accounts." — FloQast product page
- "materiality thresholds confirm each reconciliation ties within acceptable parameters." — FloQast product page
- "anything that doesn't tie becomes a tracked item with an owner, a description, and an age." — FloQast product page
- "strict sign-off keeps the two roles separate." — FloQast product page
