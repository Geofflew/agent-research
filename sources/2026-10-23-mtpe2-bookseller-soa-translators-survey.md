# The Bookseller: SoA survey, a third of translators lost work to generative AI (Apr 2024)

URL: https://www.thebookseller.com/news/a-third-of-translators-report-losing-work-to-generative-ai-systems-soa-survey-reveals
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Bookseller 12 Apr 2024 on Society of Authors survey, 787 members. 1/3 translators lost work to genAI; 4 in 10 saw income fall; ~3/4 worry about future income. Caveat: literary translators, member self-report, not headcount. Counter to C9 "flat" and "pay stable".

Quotes:
- "a survey of 787 members of the society of authors (soa) has found that a third of translators and a quarter of illustrators have lost work to generative artificial intelligence (ai) systems." — Bookseller, 12 Apr 2024
- "over a third of illustrator respondents and four in 10 translators have seen the income from their work decrease in value because of generative ai." — Bookseller reporting SoA survey
- "almost three-quarters from both groups worried about their future income because of ai." — Bookseller, translators and illustrators
