# PIB: GSTR-1 lagging GSTR-3B; ITC time-bar reminder (10 Sep 2018)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1545562&reg=48&lang=2
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Ministry admits far more taxpayers filed GSTR-3B (the summary) than GSTR-1 (the invoice-level outward return), and launched a late-fee amnesty to close the gap. Also reminds that ITC is time-barred after the September return following the financial year. Shows the two returns lenders read can diverge and are on separate clocks.

Quotes:
- "the number of taxpayers who have filed FORM GSTR-3B is substantially higher than the number of taxpayers who have furnished FORM GSTR-1" — PIB, Ministry of Finance, 10 Sep 2018
- "a one-time scheme to waive off late fee payable for delayed furnishing of FORM GSTR-1" — PIB, Ministry of Finance
- "The taxpayers are thus, advised to furnish their returns on time to ensure that input tax credit does not become time barred." — PIB, Ministry of Finance
