# Dratsch et al. 2023, Automation bias in mammography (Radiology)

URL: https://www.rsna.org/news/2023/may/ai-bias-may-impair-accuracy
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: RSNA news release on Radiology paper (doi 10.1148/radiol.222176; journal host blocked). 27 radiologists, 50 mammograms, BI-RADS w/ purported AI advice; 12 of 40 test cases carried wrong AI category. Wrong advice hurt every experience level. Inexperienced ~80% to <20%; experienced 82% to 45.5%. Reading experiment, not live screening.

Quotes:
- "in the prospective experiment, 27 radiologists read 50 mammograms." — RSNA release
- "when the purported ai suggested the wrong category, their accuracy fell to less than 20%." — RSNA release, inexperienced radiologists (from almost 80% with correct advice)
- "experienced radiologists-those with more than 15 years of experience on average-saw their accuracy fall from 82% to 45.5% when the purported ai suggested the incorrect category." — RSNA release
- "it was surprising to find that even highly experienced radiologists were adversely impacted by the ai system's judgments, albeit to a lesser extent than their less seasoned counterparts." — Thomas Dratsch, lead author
