# Vasarhelyi et al 2012, Continuous auditing by internal auditors: a micro analysis (abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/ijoais/v13y2012i3p267-281.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Intl J Accounting Information Systems 13(3), 2012. Rutgers CarLab + KPMG program. Interviews: 22 internal audit managers + 16 staff at 9 leading internal audit orgs. Maturity model: all 9 between traditional and emerging stage; none reached continuous audit stage. Adoption evidence ~20 yrs after CPAS. Internal audit only, small n.

Quotes:
- "we have conducted interviews with 22 internal audit managers and 16 internal audit staff members at 9 leading internal audit organizations to examine the status of technology adoption" — Vasarhelyi et al, abstract
- "according to our audit maturity model, all of the companies were classified between the" — Vasarhelyi et al, abstract (maturity model result; none reached the continuous audit stage)
- "not having yet reached the" — Vasarhelyi et al, abstract (continues: "continuous audit" stage; i.e. none of the 9 reached it)
- "we found that several companies in our study were already involved in some form of continuous auditing or control monitoring while others are attempting to adopt more advanced audit technologies." — Vasarhelyi et al, abstract
- "whose aim was to obtain a 360-degree view of the state of the art of ca, encompassing its use by both external and internal auditors." — authors' footnote on CarLab and KPMG research program
