# PCAOB AS 1105 Audit Evidence (as amended): company-produced information, paragraphs .10 and .10A

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS1105
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: AS1105 .10: auditor using company-produced info must test accuracy+completeness or test controls over them (ITGC, app controls), and judge precision/detail. .10A: external info given by company in electronic form: understand source+process, then test for modification or test controls. Electronic info more reliable when company controls effective.

Quotes:
- "When using information produced by the company as audit evidence, the auditor should evaluate whether the information is sufficient and appropriate for purposes of the audit" — PCAOB AS 1105 Audit Evidence (as amended)
- "Test the accuracy and completeness of the information, or test the controls over the accuracy and completeness of that information" — PCAOB AS 1105 Audit Evidence (as amended)
- "Evaluate whether the information is sufficiently precise and detailed for purposes of the audit" — PCAOB AS 1105 Audit Evidence (as amended)
- "Information produced by the company and information that the company received from one or more external sources in electronic form are more reliable when the company’s controls over that information" — PCAOB AS 1105 Audit Evidence (as amended)
- "Obtaining an understanding of (i) the source from which the company received the information" — PCAOB AS 1105 Audit Evidence (as amended)
- "If the auditor uses an audit procedure for more than one purpose, the auditor should achieve each objective of the procedure" — PCAOB AS 1105 Audit Evidence (as amended)
