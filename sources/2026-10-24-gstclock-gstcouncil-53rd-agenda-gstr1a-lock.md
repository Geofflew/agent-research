# GST Council 53rd meeting agenda: GSTR-1A and locking GSTR-3B liability

URL: https://gstcouncil.gov.in/node/5061
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Official agenda (Item 3(ix), GSTN proposal) introducing optional Form GSTR-1A so that GSTR-3B liability can be auto-populated from GSTR-1 and locked. Explains why: editable 3B let filers pay liability different from GSTR-1, triggering DRC-01B. GSTR-1A is once per period, no late fee, usable until the 3B is filed. Same document quotes the s.39 proviso on the three-year limit for furnishing returns. HTML page, 477-page agenda.

Quotes:
- "the auto populated figures can be edited by the taxpayer" — GST Council agenda, Item 3(ix), para 2 (the pre-lock state)
- "tax liability in FORM GSTR-3B shall be locked on the basis of the tax liability" — GST Council agenda, Item 3(ix), para 3
- "In order to minimize manual interference in filing of FORM GSTR 3B" — GST Council agenda, Item 3(ix), para 3
- "This can be filed only once for a return period." — GST Council agenda, on Form GSTR-1A
- "till filing of corresponding FORM GSTR-3B of the same tax period" — GST Council agenda, window for GSTR-1A
- "even after the expiry of the said period of three years from the due date of furnishing the said return" — GST Council agenda, quoting the proviso to CGST Act s.39
