# Nimdzi: the MTPE efficiency gap (2025 survey data)

URL: https://www.nimdzi.com/the-mtpe-efficiency-gap/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Nimdzi (industry analyst) 2025 survey of LSPs. Avg MTPE share of projects 26% (2022) to ~46% (2024). LSPs with >30% MTPE projects: 29.1% to 62.6%. LSPs with MTPE on >=50% of projects: 7.8% to 45.2%. No per-word rate data on page.

Quotes:
- "average MTPE adoption has surged from 26% in 2022 to nearly 46% in 2024." — Nimdzi, MTPE efficiency gap
- "in 2024, 62.6% of LSPs have more than 30% MTPE projects, up from 29.1% in 2022." — Nimdzi, MTPE efficiency gap
- "in 2024, 45.2% did so, compared with the 7.8% figure in 2022." — Nimdzi, share of LSPs using MTPE on at least half their projects
