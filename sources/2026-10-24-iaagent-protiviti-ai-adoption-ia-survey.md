# Harnessing the Future: Protiviti's Research on AI Adoption

URL: https://www.protiviti.com/gl-en/insights-paper/harnessing-future-protivitis-research-ai-adoption
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Reports Protiviti/IIA 12th Annual Global Internal Audit Perspectives on Top Technology Risks Survey (2024). 23% of IT audit functions use AI/ML tools; 39% of IA functions use AI for IA activities; large gap between AI risk perception and IA proficiency. Adoption is IT-audit-focused, not ledger or journal agents; no audit committee reporting data. Consultancy publication, dated 2024 (stale for 2026).

Quotes:
- "nearly one in four IT audit functions" — Protiviti on survey result (23%)
- "using AI for internal audit activities" — Protiviti, list of IA AI activities (39%)
- "Technology audit proficiency rated high" — Protiviti, AI/ML proficiency figure (13%)
- "Organisational preparedness rated high" — Protiviti, AI/ML preparedness figure (17%)
