# ACDIS/AHIMA Practice Brief: A Gold Mine for Best Practices (Dr. Erica Remer, 2022 brief)

URL: https://medlearn.com/acdis-ahima-practice-brief-a-gold-mine-for-best-practices/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Physician-advisor commentary on the 2022 brief. Key points: AI/computer-generated queries are bound by the same non-leading rules as human ones; queries and titles must not carry reimbursement/quality cues (a query titled "sepsis" when undocumented is leading); CC/MCC/HCC tags should not be visible next to diagnoses; do not ask the same clinician repeatedly; physician advisors may not be queried, only treating providers.
