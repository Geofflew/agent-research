Summary: Lender-side practitioner note: compliance certificates may be the only window into how non-GAAP EBITDA adjustments are applied; non-public borrowers increasingly under-deliver detail. Lenders escalate via the agent, then default provisions with a 30-day cure after awareness or notice.
