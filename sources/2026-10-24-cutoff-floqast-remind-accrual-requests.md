# FloQast ReMind now integrates with checklists

URL: https://www.floqast.com/au/blog/floqast-remind-checklist-integration
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: FloQast blog (Nov 2021) on ReMind, an add-on that automates recurring request emails for month-end items such as accruals. FloQast says its own accounting team built it because they sent too many accrual reminders to colleagues. Requests link to close checklist items and overdue ones can be escalated to managers. No deadline, threshold or no-reply consequence beyond escalation is stated.

Quotes:
- "who had to send far too many reminders to people like myself asking for vendor accruals" — FloQast blog, on why ReMind was built
- "elevate the offending item to the managerial level" — FloQast blog, on overdue requests
- "especially collecting monthly accrual estimates from third parties" — Jessica Lee, Senior Accountant at Unified, quoted on the page
- "I can just set it and forget it" — Jessica Lee, quoted on the page
