# PBC List: Why Audit Cost Is Decided by How Fast You Can Find Things (Kognitos)

URL: https://www.kognitos.com/blog/pbc-list/
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: Vendor blog (Sep 2026) describing client-side PBC work: lists issued 30-60 days before fieldwork, answered by the client finance team, work is mostly retrieval and tie-out; outstanding items extend fieldwork and fees. No survey hours given.

Quotes:
- "Outstanding items extend fieldwork directly. Almost all of the client-side work is retrieval and tie-out rather than judgment." — Kognitos
- "Almost none of this is accounting judgment. It is retrieval, formatting, reconciliation to a control total, and coordination, performed under time pressure by the same people who have just finished the close and are being asked for a forecast update." — Kognitos
- "The document has to be located, which frequently means it lives in a different system from the one the requester assumed, owned by someone in a different function." — Kognitos
- "Audit fees are rarely fixed, and stalled PBC responses are the most common reason audit timelines slip." — Kognitos
- "many firms sending a consolidated list 30 to 60 days ahead of the engagement start" — Kognitos
