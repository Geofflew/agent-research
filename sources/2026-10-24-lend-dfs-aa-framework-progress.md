# Department of Financial Services: Account Aggregator framework progress update (as on 31 Mar 2026)

URL: https://financialservices.gov.in/node/4278
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Ministry of Finance page listing GSTN among eligible FIPs and giving AA network scale at 31 Mar 2026: 179 FIPs, 989 FIUs, 2.88bn accounts enabled, 284.6m linked. Not GST-specific; sets the rail scale. Source cited on page is Sahamati.

Quotes:
- "entities may enrol themselves on AA framework as Financial Information Provider (FIP) viz. bank, banking company, non-banking financial company, asset management company, depository, depository participant, insurance company, insurance repository, central recordkeeping agency, Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN)" — DFS
- "179 financial institutions have gone live as FIP. 989 financial institutions have gone live as FIU." — DFS, progress update as on 31.03.2026
- "284.6 million accounts have been linked by users to the AA framework" — DFS, progress update as on 31.03.2026
