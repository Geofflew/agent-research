# Extend: How to build a month-end close process that doesn't rely on chasing people

URL: https://www.paywithextend.com/resource/how-to-build-a-month-end-close-process-that-doesnt-rely-on-chasing-people
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Vendor marketing blog (corporate card/spend software), June 2026, no survey numbers. Asserts, without data, that the close critical path is inputs from outside finance (expenses, POs, bookings, payroll) and that accruals dependent on other teams are the commonest bottleneck. Remedy offered is a hard-cutoff calendar plus real-time spend visibility, not re-planning.

Quotes:
- "Most close delays are caused by data dependencies, not the accounting work itself" — Extend, TL;DR
- "Accruals and estimates that depend on inputs from other teams are the most common bottleneck" — Extend, TL;DR
- "In most month-end closes, the accounting entries that take the longest are the ones waiting on data from outside finance." — Extend, "The real bottleneck"
- "waiting for data from people who don't understand why it matters right now." — Extend, quoting what controllers say
- "Expense reports submitted after the 2nd will be processed in the following month" — Extend, example of a hard cutoff policy
- "For each input, identify who owns it, when finance needs it, and what happens to close if it's late." — Extend, "Map the dependencies first"
