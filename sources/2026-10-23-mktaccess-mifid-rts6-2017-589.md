# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/589 (MiFID II RTS 6) algorithmic trading controls

URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2017/589/data.htm
Type: standard
Read in session: 0004

Summary: EU RTS 6 (2017), UK-onshored copy of EU text on legislation.gov.uk. Art 15 pre-trade controls: price collars, max order value, max order volume, max message limits, execution throttle, auto-disable until re-enabled by designated staff. Art 12 kill function. Art 9 annual self-assessment + validation report, audited by internal audit, senior mgmt approval.

Quotes:
- "after a pre-determined number of repeated executions, the trading system shall be automatically disabled until re-enabled by a designated staff member" — Art 15(3)
- "an investment firm shall be able to cancel immediately, as an emergency measure, any or all of its unexecuted orders submitted to any or all trading venues to which the investment firm is connected" — Art 12(1), kill functionality
- "an investment firm shall annually perform a self-assessment and validation process and on the basis of that process issue a validation report" — Art 9(1)
- "the validation report shall be audited by the firm's internal audit function, where such function exists, and be subject to approval by the investment firm's senior management" — Art 9(3)
