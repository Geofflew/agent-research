# NAIC proposed P&P Manual amendment, SVO discretion over filing-exempt ratings (v6)

URL: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/2023-005.01%20P%26P%20SVO%20Discretion%20v6.pdf
Type: primary
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: NAIC Valuation of Securities Task Force proposal text (2023 draft v6, pre-adoption). Would give SVO discretion to challenge an NAIC Designation derived from a credit rating provider rating it judges not a reasonable assessment of risk, with a 120-day notice window and a three-notch materiality threshold. Contains no effective date and is not the adopted text.

Quotes:
- "This amendment would grant the Securities Valuation Office staff some level of discretion over" — NAIC SVO staff, summary
- "is not a reasonable assessment of risk for regulatory purposes" — NAIC Task Force, Spring 2023 direction to SVO
- "is three (3) or more notches different than the IAO’s assessment" — NAIC draft paragraph 169, materiality threshold
- "the benefit obtained from the use credit rating in state" — P&P Manual Part One para 80, quoted in memo
