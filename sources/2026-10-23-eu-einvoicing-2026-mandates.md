# Belgium FPS Finance e-invoicing portal: B2B mandate from 1 Jan 2026

URL: https://einvoice.belgium.be/en
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Belgian government e-invoicing site. Structured e-invoices compulsory between VAT-liable Belgian firms since 1 Jan 2026. News item: tolerance period first three months of 2026. Exchange is direct between the two parties (Peppol-based). One national instance; Poland KSeF / France not covered here.

Quotes:
- "structured electronic invoices between companies are compulsory since 2026" — FPS Finance einvoice.belgium.be, homepage headline
- "since 1 january 2026, all belgian enterprises liable to vat will have to use structured electronic invoices in their transactions with each other" — FPS Finance einvoice.belgium.be, homepage
- "period of tolerance during the first three months of 2026" — FPS Finance einvoice.belgium.be, news item dated 18/12/2025
