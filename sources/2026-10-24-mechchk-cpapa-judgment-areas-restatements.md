# CPA Practice Advisor: Judgment areas in financial reporting that trigger restatements (Feb 2026)

URL: https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2026/02/24/judgment-areas-in-financial-reporting-that-trigger-restatements/178615/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Practitioner article (Navneet Sharma, KNAV U.S.). Argues restatements originate in judgment calls (estimates, classification, interpretation of complex instruments) rather than in arithmetic. Qualitative, no error-share statistic; supports the point that rule-computed checks leave judgment errors untouched. Not a quantified mechanical-vs-judgment split; none found.

Quotes:
- "the real test of financial statement integrity often lies not in the standards themselves, but in the judgment calls that shape their application." — Sharma, CPA Practice Advisor
- "even insignificant misjudgments can lead to material misstatements." — Sharma, CPA Practice Advisor
- "misjudging those terms has led to multiple restatements." — Sharma, on warrant classification
