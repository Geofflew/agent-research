# PCAOB notice of filing, Release 2024-007: technology-assisted analysis amendments to AS 1105 and AS 2301 (Federal Register, 2 July 2024)

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-07-02/html/2024-14488.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: FR notice of PCAOB final amendments AS1105/AS2301. Board: auditors reluctant TAA due to perceived regulatory uncertainty. Effective FY start on/after 2025-12-15. Flagged items: determine misstatement or ICFR deficiency. Board admits some auditors may drop TAA procedures, others may wrongly skip multi-purpose design. No prescribed procedures for flagged items.

Quotes:
- "others may be reluctant to do so due to perceived regulatory uncertainty" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007 background
- "the amendments will take effect for audits of financial statements for fiscal years beginning on or after December 15, 2025" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007 purpose
- "such analysis has been used to test the details of all items in a population" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007 background
- "test controls (including information technology general controls and automated application controls) over the company's procedures or test the company's procedures" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007, AS 1105.10A summary
- "the auditor is responsible for evaluating whether the information is sufficient and appropriate for purposes of the audit" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007, existing AS 1105 company-produced information
- "the auditor's investigation of the identified items should include determining whether the items individually or in the aggregate indicate (i) misstatements that should be evaluated in accordance with AS 2810 or (ii) deficiencies in the company's internal control over financial reporting" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007, AS 2301.49
- "the auditor should determine whether there is a reasonable possibility that remaining items within the account or disclosure include a misstatement" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007, AS 2301.50
- "Prescribing specific procedures would be impracticable considering the multitude of possible scenarios encountered in practice" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007, on flagged items
- "some auditors may choose not to use audit procedures that involve technology-assisted analysis" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007, unintended consequences
- "Auditors who hold this view may fail to design and perform audit procedures that provide sufficient appropriate audit evidence" — PCAOB, Release 2024-007, unintended consequences
