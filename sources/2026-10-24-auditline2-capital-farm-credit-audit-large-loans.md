# The Importance of High-Quality Financial Reports (Capital Farm Credit, Business of Agriculture series)

URL: https://www.3blmedia.com/news/business-agriculture-importance-high-quality-financial-reports
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Byline by Stephen Book, VP Credit Officer, Capital Farm Credit (Farm Credit System), distributed via 3BL Media. Says audit is only for "very large and very complex" deals, with no dollar figure; CPA-prepared or reviewed statements are alternatives, and lender verifies by testing (tax returns, credit bureau).

Quotes:
- "In addition, for very large and very complex transactions, audited financial statements may be required." — Stephen Book, Capital Farm Credit
- "The best way to assure reliability of financial statements is to have them audited, reviewed or prepared by a Certified Public Accountant (CPA)." — Stephen Book
- "the lender will conduct testing, such as comparing the submitted P&L with tax returns." — Stephen Book
