# Linford & Co: What are bridge (gap) letters and how do they relate to SOC reports

URL: https://linfordco.com/blog/gap-bridge-letter/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: User entity with calendar year-end gets a SOC report covering only part of the year; the gap is bridged by a service-organization management letter (not signed by the service auditor). Letter lists period covered, material changes, CUEC reminder; AICPA sets no requirements for it. Published 2022-02-15.

Quotes:
- "Once the service auditors have issued the SOC report, the service auditors do not know definitively if the internal control environment has materially changed or not." — Linford & Co blog
- "This letter is on the service organization’s letterhead and signed by the service organization, not the service auditor that performed the SOC examination." — Linford & Co blog
- "The AICPA doesn’t actually cover bridge letter requirements in the SOC guidance so there is no guidance on the specific requirements for a bridge letter" — Linford & Co blog
- "most user entities, especially their auditors , want the SOC reports while they are performing their interim internal control testing" — Linford & Co blog
