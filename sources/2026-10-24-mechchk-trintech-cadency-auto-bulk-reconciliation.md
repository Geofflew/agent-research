# Trintech Cadency Help: Auto/Bulk Reconciliation (v11.0.0)

URL: https://cadencyhelp.cadency.trintech.com/v11.0.0/content/certificationadmin/auto_bulk_reconciliation_about.htm
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Product help page. Admin-defined auto/bulk reconciliation reconciles, reviews and approves accounts with no preparer when mechanical conditions hold: zero balance, no change since last rec, ledger equals subledger, net unexplained difference zero, each within a set threshold. Default threshold is zero. Prerequisites: no open items, account active and assigned.

Quotes:
- "reconciling/reviewing/approving accounts that meet specific requirements, such as zero balance or no activity" — Cadency help, Auto/Bulk Reconciliation
- "used to automatically/bulk reconcile accounts that have subledger balances matching the ledger balance" — Cadency help, Ledger to subledger comparison
- "The balances must match in order to be considered for this type of reconciliation." — Cadency help, Accounts with no activity
- "no open items can exist for the account." — Cadency help, Prerequisites for bulk reconciliation
