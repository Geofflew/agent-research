# ATA Science and Technology Division: MTPE at ATA63, evaluating a project

URL: https://ata-divisions.org/S_TD/2023/03/10/mtpe-at-ata63/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Translator-written conference report (ATA division blog, Mar 2023). Advice: charge hourly for MTPE; light vs full PE by client purpose; over-polishing cuts effective hourly rate; refuse jobs below acceptable rate. Shows pay risk shifts to post-editor under per-word MTPE rates. Opinion, not ATA policy; ATA position paper (2018, ata-divisions.org/LTD) says negative impact on human translation undeniable.

Quotes:
- "it is best to charge an hourly rate, as many people do with regular editing." — Brooke A. Cochran, MTPE at ATA63
- "over-polishing, taking more time than the rate covers or making it publication quality when that's not necessary, is not desirable, since this results in a much lower hourly rate for the translator." — Brooke A. Cochran, MTPE at ATA63
- "if the client works with you regularly and will not negotiate/accept a higher rate, then you just don't accept the job." — Brooke A. Cochran, MTPE at ATA63
