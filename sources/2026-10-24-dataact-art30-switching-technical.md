# Data Act Article 30: Technical aspects of switching

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/30/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Text of Art. 30 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 via a mirror. IaaS-type providers must take reasonable measures toward functional equivalence; other providers (e.g. SaaS/ERP) must offer open interfaces to all customers and destination providers free of charge for portability and interoperability, and export exportable data in a structured format when no standards exist. Para 6 carves out new development, IP/trade-secret assets and security. Interfaces are for portability and interoperability; no write-access or functional-API-for-third-party-operation right is stated.

Quotes:
- "shall make open interfaces available to an equal extent to all their customers and the concerned destination providers of data processing services free of charge to facilitate the switching process" — Art. 30(2)
- "to enable the development of software to communicate with the services, for the purposes of data portability and interoperability" — Art. 30(2)
- "shall, at the request of the customer, export all exportable data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format" — Art. 30(5)
- "shall not be required to develop new technologies or services, or disclose or transfer digital assets that are protected by intellectual property rights or that constitute a trade secret" — Art. 30(6)
- "take all reasonable measures in their power to facilitate that the customer, after switching to a service covering the same service type, achieves functional equivalence" — Art. 30(1), infrastructure-only services
