# 17 CFR 240.15c3-5 Risk management controls for brokers or dealers with market access

URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/240.15c3-5
Type: standard
Read in session: 0004

Summary: SEC Market Access Rule, adopted 75 FR 69825, Nov 15 2010. Broker-dealer w/ market access must run pre-set credit/capital limits + erroneous-order rejects. Controls under broker-dealer direct and exclusive control. Annual review + annual CEO certification.

Quotes:
- "reasonably designed to systematically limit the financial exposure of the broker or dealer that could arise as a result of market access" — 17 CFR 240.15c3-5(c)(1)
- "prevent the entry of orders that exceed appropriate pre-set credit or capital thresholds in the aggregate for each customer and the broker or dealer" — 17 CFR 240.15c3-5(c)(1)(i)
- "prevent the entry of erroneous orders, by rejecting orders that exceed appropriate price or size parameters, on an order-by-order basis or over a short period of time, or that indicate duplicative orders" — 17 CFR 240.15c3-5(c)(1)(ii)
- "shall be under the direct and exclusive control of the broker or dealer that is subject to paragraph (b) of this section" — 17 CFR 240.15c3-5(d)
- "shall, on an annual basis, certify that such risk management controls and supervisory procedures comply with paragraphs (b) and (c) of this section" — 17 CFR 240.15c3-5(e)(2), chief executive officer
