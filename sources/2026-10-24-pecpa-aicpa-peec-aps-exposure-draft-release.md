# AICPA news release: PEEC seeks comment on ethics update for alternative practice structures (Dec 19, 2025)

URL: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/news/article/aicpa-seeks-comment-on-ethics-rules-update-for-alternative-practice
Type: primary
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: PEEC voted Dec 19, 2025 to expose "Proposed Revisions Related to Alternative Practice Structures", aimed at private-equity-backed firms. Comments due April 30 (2026). Draft distinguishes "significant influence" from "control" by investors over the nonattest entity, and widens the "network firm" definition. Would take effect one year after adoption.

Quotes:
- "the increased prevalence of private equity investment in the profession calls for updated interpretations" — AICPA news release, Dec 19, 2025
- "distinguish between “significant influence” and “control” by investors over nonattest entities, with guidance on how these terms affect independence assessments" — AICPA news release
- "If approved next year, the proposed independence updates would be effective one year after adoption, although firms could implement them earlier." — AICPA news release
- "attest firms that are closely aligned with a nonattest entity at least partly owned by investors" — AICPA news release (definition of APS)
