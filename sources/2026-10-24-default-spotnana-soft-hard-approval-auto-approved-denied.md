# Spotnana developer docs: soft vs hard approval, AUTO_APPROVED / AUTO_DECLINED at deadline

URL: https://developer.spotnana.com/spotnana/basic_approval_concepts
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Travel-booking platform docs. Every booking approval has a deadline (approvalVoidDeadline). If the approver has not responded: soft approval is auto-approved and processed; hard approval is auto-declined and the booking cancelled; passive approval proceeds immediately. A clean statement of three default-outcome policies per task type.

Quotes:
- "For soft approvals: The approval status is changed to AUTO_APPROVED and the booking is processed on the platform." — Spotnana, Approval deadline section
- "For hard approvals: The approval status is changed to AUTO_DECLINED and the booking is canceled." — Spotnana, Approval deadline section
- "Auto denied: The booking has been denied automatically." — Spotnana, approval statuses
