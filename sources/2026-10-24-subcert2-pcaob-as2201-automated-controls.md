# PCAOB AS 2201: automated vs. person-performed controls

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pcaobus.org/oversight/standards/auditing-standards/details/AS2201
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: PCAOB ICFR audit standard distinguishes controls that rely on an individual from automated ones, and treats the identity of "key personnel who perform the control" as a risk factor. Automated controls are lower risk if IT general controls are effective, so assurance shifts to the ITGCs and change control. Says nothing about who signs for an automated control.
