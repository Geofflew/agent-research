# Bias in the Loop: How Humans Evaluate AI-Generated Suggestions (Beck, Eckman, Kern, Kreuter, HDSR 2026)

URL: https://stepheckman.com/publication/hdsr_2026/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: N=2784 experiment. Reviewers verify AI-extracted values from corporate emissions report tables (data-verification, close analogue). Required typed correction => fewer flags, more wrong accepts. AI-favorable attitude predicts acceptance of errors. Bonus pay and early AI accuracy no effect. Not accountants.

Quotes:
- "when flagging an AI error required the additional step of typing a corrected value, participants made fewer corrections overall and more often accepted incorrect suggestions" — abstract
- "Participants skeptical of AI detected errors more reliably and achieved higher accuracy, while those favorable toward automation more often accepted incorrect AI suggestions." — abstract
- "Neither the bonus payment nor the accuracy of the AI’s early suggestions meaningfully affected performance." — abstract
- "participants were shown tables from corporate greenhouse gas emissions reports and asked to verify whether values extracted by an AI were accurate" — abstract
