# Algorithm appreciation: People prefer algorithmic to human judgment (Logg, Minson, Moore 2019)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jobhdp/v151y2019icp90-103.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: OBHDP 2019, six experiments. Lay people weight advice more if labeled algorithm. Experts/forecasters rely less, hurts accuracy. Not accounting-specific.

Quotes:
- "lay people adhere more to advice when they think it comes from an algorithm than from a person" — abstract
- "algorithm appreciation waned when: people chose between an algorithm’s estimate and their own" — abstract
- "experienced professionals, who make forecasts on a regular basis, relied less on algorithmic advice than lay people did, which hurt their accuracy" — abstract
