# Error detection by industry-specialized teams during sequential audit review (Owhoso, Messier, Lynch 2002), abstract via SUFE mirror

URL: https://academicnewsletter.sufe.edu.cn/info/413531
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: JAR 2002 seeded-error experiment. Seniors catch mechanical errors, managers conceptual. Real teams (sequential review) worse than nominal teams on mechanical errors. Out of specialization, reviewers ineffective on both error types. Mirror page; abstract only.

Quotes:
- "seniors add value to the team by detecting more mechanical errors while managers detect more conceptual errors" — abstract
- "the nominal teams outperform real teams in the detection of mechanical but not conceptual errors" — abstract
- "Out of specialization the auditors are not effective at detecting errors, and real teams perform below the nominal team benchmark in the detection of both mechanical and conceptual errors." — abstract
