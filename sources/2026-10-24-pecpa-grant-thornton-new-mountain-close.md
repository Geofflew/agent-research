# New Mountain Capital and Grant Thornton close growth investment (May 31, 2024)

URL: https://www.grantthornton.com/insights/press-releases/2024/may/new-mountain-capital-and-grant-thornton-close-growth-investment
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: Firm's own press release announcing closing of the deal. Investment is in Grant Thornton Advisors LLC (non-attest); Grant Thornton LLP stays the licensed CPA attest firm. New Mountain has about $50 billion AUM; CDPQ and OA Private Capital took minority stakes. Stated use: technology, people and new service lines. Release is silent on independence safeguards.

Quotes:
- "With the completion of this investment in Grant Thornton Advisors LLC, Grant Thornton operates in an alternative practice structure" — Grant Thornton press release, May 31, 2024
- "Grant Thornton Advisors LLC provides business advisory and non-attest services. Grant Thornton LLP, a licensed CPA firm, provides attest services." — Grant Thornton press release
- "a leading growth-oriented investment firm with approximately $50 billion in assets under management" — Grant Thornton press release (on New Mountain Capital)
- "CDPQ and OA Private Capital made minority investments in Grant Thornton alongside New Mountain Capital." — Grant Thornton press release
