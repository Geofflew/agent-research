# Stanford Fingate: Balance Sheet Account Balance Reconciliation

URL: https://fingate.stanford.edu/managing-funds/balance-sheet-account-balance-reconciliation
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Stanford's central finance guidance. Central Financial Management Services assigns reconciliation frequency per account on risk, materiality and history; default quarterly with some monthly. Reconciliations can be manual or in BlackLine, and the central team only samples completed reconciliations each quarter for compliance.

Quotes:
- "Financial Management Services (FMS) designates the reconciliation frequency for account balances based on multiple factors, including risk, materiality and history." — Stanford Fingate
- "Balance Sheet account balance reconciliations at Stanford can be performed manually or by using BlackLine accounting software." — Stanford Fingate
- "Provides secondary financial control through review of sample reconciliations" — Stanford Fingate, roles table (FMS)
