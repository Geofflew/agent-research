# ECB: Instant Payments Regulation (IPR) requirements and implementation deadlines

URL: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/retail/instant_payments/html/instant_payments_regulation.en.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: ECB summary of the EU Instant Payments Regulation, adopted 13 March 2024. Euro-area PSPs must receive instant euro credit transfers from 9 January 2025 and send them from 9 October 2025, with equal charges and free verification of payee; non-euro states follow in 2027. Enabler for same-day settlement of an early-payment offer; says nothing about invoice pricing.

Quotes:
- "shall also offer the service of sending and receiving instant credit transfers (Article 5a)" — ECB IPR page, main requirements
- "shall not be higher than the charges levied by that PSP in respect of sending and receiving other credit transfers of corresponding type (Article 5b)" — ECB IPR page, equality of charges
- "This service shall be offered free of charge to the payer." — ECB IPR page, verification of payee
- "PSPs offering instant credit transfers shall verify periodically, and at least daily" — ECB IPR page, sanctions screening
