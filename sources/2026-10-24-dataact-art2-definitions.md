# Data Act Article 2: Definitions (exportable data, functional equivalence, switching)

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/2/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Definitions in Regulation (EU) 2023/2854. Exportable data is input and output data including metadata generated by the customer's use, minus IP-protected assets and trade secrets. Functional equivalence is a minimum level of functionality, a materially comparable outcome for the same input, for shared features only. Switching explicitly includes extracting, transforming and uploading data.

Quotes:
- "means the input and output data, including metadata, directly or indirectly generated, or cogenerated, by the customer" — Art. 2(38), exportable data
- "excluding any assets or data protected by intellectual property rights, or constituting a trade secret, of providers of data processing services or third parties" — Art. 2(38)
- "a minimum level of functionality in the environment of a new data processing service of the same service type" — Art. 2(37), functional equivalence
- "delivers a materially comparable outcome in response to the same input for shared features supplied to the customer under the contract" — Art. 2(37)
- "including through extracting, transforming and uploading the data" — Art. 2(34), switching
