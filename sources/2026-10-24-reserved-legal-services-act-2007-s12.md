# Legal Services Act 2007, section 12: reserved legal activities

URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/29/section/12
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: UK statute defining "reserved legal activity" as an exhaustive list of six activities. Precedent for statutory reservation of named activities to authorised persons, defined by activity rather than by profession. Section 12(3) separately defines the wider "legal activity", which includes legal advice, so reservation is the narrower category. The UK has no equivalent list for financial reporting judgments (audit is reserved separately under the Companies Act 2006, not read here). Legal-activity regulation is about who may be authorised; the Act does not address software.
