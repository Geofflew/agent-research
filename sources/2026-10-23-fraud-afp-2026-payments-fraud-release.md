# AFP: Over 75% of US Firms Experienced Payments Fraud in 2025 (2026 Payments Fraud and Control Survey)

URL: https://www.financialprofessionals.org/about/learn-more/press-releases/Details/over-75-percent-of-us-firms-experienced-payments-fraud-in-2025-while-ai-adoption-for-fraud-mitigation-lags
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: AFP/Truist survey, 465 treasury practitioners, Jan 2026, covers 2025. 76% hit by attempted/actual fraud. 74% hit by BEC, big rise vs 2023-24. Checks 58%, ACH debit 30%, wire 25%. Only 17% use AI for fraud. Treasury finds most fraud. Truist: fraud prevention = operating model; verification, disciplined approvals.

Quotes:
- "More than three-quarters of organizations (76%) in the United States experienced attempted or actual payments fraud in 2025." — AFP press release, Apr 14 2026
- "About three in four organizations (74%) were affected by business email compromise (BEC) in 2025." — AFP press release
- "representing a significant increase from 2023 and 2024" — AFP press release
- "Just 17% of organizations leverage AI to combat payments fraud." — AFP press release
- "Paper checks (cited by 58% of survey respondents) were the most targeted in 2025, followed by ACH debits (30%) and wire transfers (25%)." — AFP press release, FAQ
- "72% of organizations using checks plan to continue using them for the foreseeable future" — AFP press release
- "Survey respondents cited treasury as the department most likely to discover attempted fraud (83%) and actual fraud (55%)." — AFP press release
- "Fraud prevention today is an operating model, not a single control" — Chris Ward, Truist, in AFP release
- "strengthening the fundamentals — verification, disciplined approvals and timely detection" — Chris Ward, Truist, in AFP release
- "The 2026 AFP Payments Fraud and Control Survey was conducted in January 2026 among 465 treasury practitioners" — AFP press release, methodology
