# Data Act Article 2: data processing service, connected product, related service

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/2/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Art. 2 definitions (complements the existing dataact-art2-definitions entry). A data processing service is the broad cloud definition (covers SaaS, IaaS, PaaS) and is the object of the switching chapter; connected product and related service define the scope of the Chapter II access rights and are physical-device-centred.

Quotes:
- "a digital service that is provided to a customer and that enables ubiquitous and on-demand network access to a shared pool of configurable, scalable and elastic computing resources" — Art. 2(8)
- "a set of data processing services that share the same primary objective, data processing service model and main functionalities" — Art. 2(9), same service type
- "a digital service, other than an electronic communications service, including software, which is connected with the product" — Art. 2(6), related service
