# GOV.UK: Apply for a coronavirus Bounce Back Loan (withdrawn 1 April 2021)

URL: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-a-coronavirus-bounce-back-loan
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: UK government-guaranteed lending scheme (published 27 April 2020, closed 31 March 2021). Loans of £2,000 up to £50,000 with 100% state guarantee; the borrower self-declares eligibility, no financial statements requested on the page. Clear dated case of a lender-side program accepting self-declaration in place of audited accounts, at a capped size, because the state bears the loss.

Quotes:
- "The maximum loan available is £50,000. The government guarantees 100% of the loan" — GOV.UK guidance, scheme overview
- "The lender will ask you to fill in a short online application form and self-declare that you are eligible." — GOV.UK, How to apply
- "After 12 months the interest rate will be 2.5% a year." — GOV.UK, scheme overview
- "The Bounce Back Loan Scheme closed to new applications and top-up applications on 31 March 2021." — GOV.UK withdrawal notice
