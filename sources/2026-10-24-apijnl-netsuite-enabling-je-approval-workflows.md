# Enabling Workflows for Journal Entry Approval (NetSuite Help)

URL: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/procedure_4791365580.html
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Journal approval routing is an account-level accounting preference (Approval Routing subtab, Journal Entries box). It replaces the older "Require Approvals on Journal Entries" method, and adds Next Approver and Approval Status fields to journal forms. Control is a preference plus form fields; the page does not mention API-created journals.

Quotes:
- "To enable workflows for journal entry approval, you check the Journal Entries box on the Approval Routing subtab of the Accounting Preferences page." — NetSuite Help
- "NetSuite hides this approval method after the Journal Entries approval routing accounting preference is checked." — NetSuite Help (re Require Approvals on Journal Entries)
- "two new lists appear on the Journal, Intercompany Journal, Advanced Intercompany Journal, and Statistical Journal pages: Next Approver and Approval Status." — NetSuite Help
