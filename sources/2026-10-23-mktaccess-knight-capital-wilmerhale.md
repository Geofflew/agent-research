# WilmerHale: Knight Capital settles Rule 15c3-5 violations with SEC, $12 million

URL: https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/knight-capital-settles-rule-15c3-5-violations-with-sec-agrees-to-pay-12-million
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Law-firm alert, 22 Oct 2013, on SEC order of 16 Oct 2013. First enforcement under 15c3-5. 1 Aug 2012: discontinued code in router, 4M+ orders in 45 min, 397M shares, loss over $460M (initial reports said $440M). SEC findings: no pre-submission erroneous-order control, account not linked to firm-wide capital limit, no automated alerts, weak CEO certification.

Quotes:
- "during the first 45 minutes of trading on august 1, 2012, a router that contained discontinued and therefore incorrect code sent over 4 million orders to the market in an effort to fill 212 small retail orders" — WilmerHale summarizing the SEC order
- "the error resulted in knight capital trading more than 397 million shares, acquiring several billion dollars in unwanted positions, and suffering a trading loss of over $460 million" — WilmerHale summarizing the SEC order
- "failing to generate automated alerts, and instead relying on human monitoring of the firm's financial exposure" — WilmerHale on SEC charges under the market access rule
- "failing to satisfy the annual ceo certification requirement, because the certification stated that the firm had in place" — WilmerHale on SEC charges
