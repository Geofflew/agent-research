# FINRA Rule 11892 — Clearly Erroneous Transactions in Exchange-Listed Securities

URL: https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/11892
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: Breaking executed trade = exception. Needs price deviation past quantitative guideline, action generally within 30 min of detection, hard stop next trading day open. Disputes need counterparty notice, appeal path. Trade final by default; ledger reversal has no such limits.

Quotes:
- "Any action taken in connection with this paragraph shall be taken in a timely fashion, generally within thirty (30) minutes of the detection of the erroneous transaction and in no circumstances later than the start of normal market hours on the trading day following the date of the execution(s) under review." — FINRA Rule 11892, regulatory-halt paragraph
- "Absent extraordinary circumstances, the officer shall take action pursuant to this paragraph generally within 30 minutes after becoming aware of the transaction." — FINRA Rule 11892, officer-action paragraph
- "If a FINRA officer acting pursuant to this Rule declares any transaction null and void, each party involved in the transaction shall be notified as soon as practicable by FINRA, and the party aggrieved by the action may appeal such action in accordance with Rule 11894" — FINRA Rule 11892, null-and-void paragraph
