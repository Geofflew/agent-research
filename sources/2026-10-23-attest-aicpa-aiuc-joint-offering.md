# AICPA and AIUC joint SOC 2 plus AIUC-1 AI assurance offering

URL: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/news/article/auditors-meet-urgent-demand-for-ai-assurance-with-new-offering-from-aicpa-and-aiuc
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: AICPA + AIUC launch Oct 2026. One CPA-led engagement = SOC 2 (controls opinion) + AIUC-1 cert (live agent behavior testing). SOC 2 half = controls design/op effectiveness. AIUC half = tested agent behavior. Full launch early 2027.

Quotes:
- "the resulting report includes an opinion on the design and operating effectiveness of controls supporting the system" — AICPA-CIMA news release, on what SOC 2 delivers
- "what sets aiuc-1 apart is live testing of ai agent behavior, not just a review of policies and guardrails" — Rajiv Dattani, AIUC cofounder, in AICPA release
- "agents are tested across hundreds of risk and attack combinations, from hallucinations and data leakage to manipulative requests and adversarial prompt injection" — Dattani, in AICPA release
- "both soc 2 reports and aiuc-1 certifications require annual renewal, in addition to quarterly aiuc-1 technical retesting" — AICPA release
- "once initial engagements have concluded, a full launch of the joint certification is expected to take place in early 2027" — AICPA release
- "trust is negotiated one security questionnaire at a time: a slow, costly exchange that rarely settles the question and hinders ai adoption" — AICPA release
