# AICPA Professional Ethics Division, Nonattest Services Toolkit (April 2023)

Summary: AICPA's own toolkit explaining the ET 1.295 framework with a checklist. Restates the four client agreements required before any nonattest service (assume management responsibilities, designate an overseer with suitable skill/knowledge/experience, evaluate results, accept responsibility) and says a No on any means independence is impaired. Lists bookkeeping, payroll, and other disbursements as 1.295.120.

Quotes:
- "oversee the service by designating an individual, preferably within senior management, who possesses suitable skill, knowledge and experience" — AICPA toolkit, general requirements
- "authorizing, executing, or consummating transactions or otherwise exercising authority on behalf of a client or having the authority to do so" — AICPA toolkit, management responsibility examples
- "bookkeeping, payroll, and other disbursements (1.295.120)" — AICPA toolkit, list of nonattest service types
