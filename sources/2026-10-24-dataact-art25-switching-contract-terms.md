# Data Act Article 25: contractual terms concerning switching

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/25/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Art. 25 requires a written switching contract: 30-day transition, 2-month max notice, at least 30 days of data retrieval, and an alternative transition up to seven months if 30 days is technically unfeasible. Providers may carve out data specific to their internal functioning to protect trade secrets, if this does not delay switching. Obligations run to the customer and third parties it authorises, only during the switching process.

Quotes:
- "not after the mandatory maximum transitional period of 30 calendar days" — Art. 25(2)(a)
- "provide reasonable assistance to the customer and third parties authorised by the customer in the switching process" — Art. 25(2)(a)(i)
- "continue the provision of the functions or services under the contract" — Art. 25(2)(a)(ii), continuity during transition
- "all categories of data and digital assets that can be ported during the switching process, including, at a minimum, all exportable data" — Art. 25(2)(e)
- "categories of data specific to the internal functioning of the provider's data processing service" — Art. 25(2)(f), exempt where trade secrets at risk
- "a minimum period for data retrieval of at least 30 calendar days" — Art. 25(2)(g)
- "which shall not exceed seven months" — Art. 25(4), alternative transition if 30 days unfeasible
