# El Contribuyente: Cancelacion de CFDI, plazos, excepciones

URL: https://www.elcontribuyente.mx/2025/09/cancelacion-de-cfdi-plazos-excepciones-y-como-responder-para-evitar-errores/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Practitioner article citing CFF art. 29-A and RMF rules 2.7.1.34/35: cancellation only within the fiscal year of issue and with receiver acceptance, with enumerated exceptions (nomina, public-general, foreign residents, next business day, financial system).
