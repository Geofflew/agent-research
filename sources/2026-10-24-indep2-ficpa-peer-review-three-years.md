# Peer Review (Florida Institute of CPAs)

URL: https://www.ficpa.org/resources/peer-review
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: State society page that administers the AICPA Peer Review Program. States peer review is an AICPA member requirement and a statutory requirement in 55 licensing jurisdictions, on a three-year cycle. Gives the oversight clock for CPA firms.

Quotes:
- "the AICPA requires its member firms to undergo a peer review every three years" — FICPA peer review page
