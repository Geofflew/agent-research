# Man Versus Machine: Complex Estimates and Auditor Reliance on Artificial Intelligence (Commerford, Dennis, Joe, Ulla 2022)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/joares/v60y2022i1p171-201.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: JAR 2022 experiment. Auditors got contradictory evidence from firm AI vs human specialist on mgmt complex estimate. AI source → smaller proposed adjustments = algorithm aversion, i.e. UNDER-reliance on AI. Stronger when mgmt inputs objective. Firms spending billions.

Quotes:
- "Audit firms are investing billions of dollars to develop artificial intelligence (AI) systems that will help auditors execute challenging tasks" — abstract
- "the tendency to discount computer‐based advice more heavily than human advice, although the advice is identical otherwise" — abstract
- "auditors receiving contradictory evidence from their firm's AI system (instead of a human specialist) propose smaller adjustments to management's complex estimates, particularly when management develops their estimates using relatively objective (vs. subjective) inputs" — abstract
- "Our findings suggest auditor susceptibility to algorithm aversion could prove costly for the profession and financial statements users." — abstract
