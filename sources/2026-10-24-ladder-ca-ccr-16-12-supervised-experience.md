# California Code of Regulations, Title 16, section 12: General Experience Required

URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/16-CCR-12
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: CBA rule for CPA licensure experience. Experience must be supervised and verified by an active licensee who reviews applicant's work routinely. Total required: one year full-time equivalent. Licensee-years requirement for the supervisor is not stated here.

Quotes:
- "experience must be supervised by a person holding a valid, active license or comparable authority to practice public accounting" — 16 CCR 12(a)
- "Supervised experience means that the applicant's supervisor shall have reviewed and evaluated the applicant's qualifying work" — 16 CCR 12(a)
- "Experience shall be verified by the licensee supervising the experience." — 16 CCR 12(a)(1)
- "shall be signed under penalty of perjury" — 16 CCR 12(a)(3)(A)
- "the total experience completed by the applicant is the equivalent of at least one year of full-time employment" — 16 CCR 12(c)
- "170 hours of part-time employment shall be considered equivalent to one month of full-time employment" — 16 CCR 12(c)
