# SEC Release 33-8124: Certification of Disclosure (disclosure committee and officer responsibility)

Read in session: 0008C

Summary: SEC final rule release (Aug 2002) implementing Section 302. Recommends issuers create a disclosure committee that reports up to the CEO/CFO, who retain express responsibility for the disclosure controls. The committee supports the certifying officers; it does not take over their responsibility. No use of the word "sub-certification" found in this text.
