# Federal Reserve Commercial Bank Examination Manual 2040.4 — Loan Portfolio Management internal control questionnaire

URL: https://www.vabankers.org/sites/main/files/file-attachments/federal_reserve_-_commercial_bank_exam_manual_2020.pdf?1594678241
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Fed examiner questionnaire (Nov 2005 edition, hosted by Virginia Bankers Assn). Treats "audited statements above a dollar minimum of commitment size" as a normal bank policy choice, and leaves the dollar figure blank for the bank to fill: the regulator sets no number. Also asks about periodic financials from borrowers not fully secured by marketable collateral.

- "audited financial statements based on the" — CBEM 2040.4 item 10
- "dollar amount of the commitment? (If so," — CBEM 2040.4 item 10
- "state the dollar minimum for requiring" — CBEM 2040.4 item 10
- "of financial statements by all borrowers" — CBEM 2040.4 item 8 (borrowers "whose loans are not fully secured by readily marketable collateral")
