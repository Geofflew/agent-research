# FRC Key Facts and Trends in the Accountancy Profession 2026 (full report, 2025 data)

Type: primary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: FRC 24th edition, published Oct 2026, covering 2025. UK/ROI students rose 0.9% to over 156,000; the known audit-route student pool fell 4.3% to 26,400. No AI mention, no firm headcount by grade and no graduate intake figures in the page text (firm-level data sits in the Excel file). The 2025 data show no collapse in the student pipeline; it supersedes the 2025 edition's "down 0.3%".
