# Correlated Errors in Large Language Models (Kim, Garg, Peng, Garg; ICML 2025, PMLR 267)

URL: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kim25e.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Peer-reviewed version of the arXiv preprint (2506.07962), published in Proceedings of the 42nd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2025), PMLR vol. 267, pp. 30038-30066. Same abstract: 350+ LLMs, errors substantially correlated, larger and more accurate models more correlated across providers, effects on LLM-as-judge and hiring.
