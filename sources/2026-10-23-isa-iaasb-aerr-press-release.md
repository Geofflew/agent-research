# IAASB Proposes Revisions to Core Standards to Enhance Risk-Based Audit Framework and Address Technological Advances

URL: https://www.iaasb.org/news-events/2026-08/iaasb-proposes-revisions-core-standards-enhance-risk-based-audit-framework-and-address-technological
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: IAASB press release 5 Aug 2026. EDs of ISA 330, 500, 520. New definition of audit evidence for digital environment; stronger relevance and reliability evaluation; principles-based over technology-specific. Responses by 15 Dec 2026. No effective date stated.

Quotes:
- "a revised definition of audit evidence to reflect today’s digital environment" — IAASB press release, 5 Aug 2026
- "strengthened requirements for evaluating the relevance and reliability of information used as audit evidence" — IAASB press release, 5 Aug 2026
- "The proposals reflect the IAASB’s conclusion that a principles-based rather than technology-specific approach is the best way to maintain robust and relevant standards in the face of rapid technological evolution." — IAASB press release, 5 Aug 2026
- "the proposals include guidance to help auditors exercise professional judgment about appropriate use of technology" — IAASB press release, 5 Aug 2026
- "Responses are requested by December 15, 2026" — IAASB press release, 5 Aug 2026
