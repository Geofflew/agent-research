# KPMG Germany: AI agents in KPMG Clara AI (Apr 2025)

URL: https://kpmg.com/de/en/media/press-releases/2025/04/kpmg-integrates-ki-agents-in-global-audit-platform-clara-ai.html
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0004

Summary: KPMG press release 30 Apr 2025. Agents in Clara AI for ~95,000 auditors check expenses, find unrecorded liabilities, analyze provisions. Financial Report Analyzer helps complete disclosure checklists. Control-test agents planned. Only condition stated: auditor skepticism and experience not replaceable. Vendor marketing; no error rates, no acceptance criteria.

Quotes:
- "around 95,000 auditors worldwide will benefit from the new generation of intelligent agents that automate routine tasks, make data-based recommendations and draw the audit teams' attention to high-risk issues in a targeted manner." — KPMG Germany press release
- "the AI agents automatically check expenses, identify unrecognized liabilities and analyze provisions" — KPMG Germany press release
- "the professional skepticism and years of experience of our audit teams are still important and cannot be replaced by AI." — Christian Sailer, KPMG Germany head of audit
