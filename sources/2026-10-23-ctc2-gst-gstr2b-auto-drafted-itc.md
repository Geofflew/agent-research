# GST Portal FAQ: Form GSTR-2B (auto-drafted ITC statement)

URL: https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/FAQ_gstr2b.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005A

Summary: India GSTN official FAQ. Buyer ITC statement auto-drafted from suppliers' filed returns. Buyer cannot edit; must reconcile to own books. Page does not mention e-invoice/IRP; link to clearance data is indirect (GSTR-1 side), not shown here.

Quotes:
- "Form GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted ITC statement which will be generated for every registered person on the basis of the information furnished by his/her suppliers in their respective Form GSTR-1/1A & Form GSTR-5 and ITC received through Form GSTR-6." — GST portal FAQ, Q1
- "It is only a read-only static auto-drafted ITC statement which indicates the availability of Input Tax Credit to you against each document filed by your suppliers" — GST portal FAQ, Q3
- "Taxpayers are advised to ensure that the data generated in Form GSTR-2B is reconciled with their own records and books of accounts." — GST portal FAQ, Q5
- "you cannot make any changes or add document to the Form GSTR-2B" — GST portal FAQ
- "form gstr-2b will be generated for each month on the 14th day of the succeeding month" — GST portal FAQ, Q4
- "the cut-off date is 00:00 hours on 12th of the relevant month to 23:59 hours, on 11th of the succeeding month" — GST portal FAQ, Q4 (monthly GSTR-1)
