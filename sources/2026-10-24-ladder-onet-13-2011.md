# O*NET Summary Report: Accountants and Auditors (13-2011.00)

URL: https://www.onetonline.org/link/summary/13-2011.00
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: US Dept of Labor O*NET. Job Zone Four: accountant needs four years college plus "several years" accounting work to be "considered qualified"; zone includes supervising others.

Quotes:
- "an accountant must complete four years of college and work for several years in accounting to be considered qualified." — O*NET, Job Zone Four Related Experience
- "Employees in these occupations usually need several years of work-related experience, on-the-job training, and/or vocational training." — O*NET, Job Zone Four Job Training
- "Many of these occupations involve coordinating, supervising, managing, or training others." — O*NET, Job Zone Four examples
