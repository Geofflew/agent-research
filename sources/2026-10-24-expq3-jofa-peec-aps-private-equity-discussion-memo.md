# AICPA seeks feedback on independence rules related to private equity (Journal of Accountancy)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/news/2025/mar/independence-rules-private-equity/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: JofA (Mar 2025) on PEEC APS task force discussion memo: only licensed CPA firms provide attest; three-step analysis (network firms, covered members, other threats); nonattest entity is a network firm of the attest firm, but PE investor, funds and portfolio companies generally are not. Page shows later items: Aug 2026 PEEC continues APS discussions.

Quotes:
- "only licensed cpa firms can provide attest services, with clearly defined firewalls required for investors taking a piece or outright control of an entity that provides only nonattest services" — Bryan Strickland, Journal of Accountancy, March 10 2025; Lisa Snyder quoted as PEEC task force co-chair
- "driven by the increasing prevalence of private-equity investment in accounting firms" — Bryan Strickland, Journal of Accountancy, March 10 2025; Lisa Snyder quoted as PEEC task force co-chair
- "the nonattest entity would be considered a network firm of the attest firm" — Bryan Strickland, Journal of Accountancy, March 10 2025; Lisa Snyder quoted as PEEC task force co-chair
- "a private-equity investor, its funds, and other portfolio companies would generally not be considered network firms" — Bryan Strickland, Journal of Accountancy, March 10 2025; Lisa Snyder quoted as PEEC task force co-chair
- "network firms are subject to independence requirements for financial statement audit and review clients" — Bryan Strickland, Journal of Accountancy, March 10 2025; Lisa Snyder quoted as PEEC task force co-chair
