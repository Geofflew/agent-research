# Data Act Articles 23 and 24: removing obstacles to switching, and scope

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/23/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Art. 23 (chapter VI) binds all data processing service providers (incl. SaaS) to remove obstacles to switching to the same service type, to on-premises, or to multi-provider use. The duty is about exit: terminating, porting exportable data and digital assets, functional equivalence. Nothing here gives third parties write access to the service's functions while the customer stays. Art. 24 limits the duties to the source provider's own services, contracts and practices.

Quotes:
- "shall not impose and shall remove pre-commercial, commercial, technical, contractual and organisational obstacles" — Art. 23 chapeau
- "to use several providers of data processing services at the same time" — Art. 23 chapeau, multi-provider use is within purpose
- "porting the customer's exportable data and digital assets, to a different provider of data processing services" — Art. 23(c)
- "shall apply only to the services, contracts or commercial practices provided by the source provider of data processing services" — Art. 24
