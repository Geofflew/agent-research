# EY UK 2025 Transparency Report (FY25, to 27 June 2025): graduate and school-leaver intake

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-uk/about-us/documents/ey-uk-2025-transparency-report.pdf
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: EY UK's statutory transparency report. Audit practice took in 764 people in FY25, 532 of them graduates and school leavers plus 232 short-term placements; firm-wide 1,124 graduates. The report gives no prior-year intake and no cut, and frames technology as changing roles while "continuing to hire". Internally inconsistent: 764 is audit's total in one place and its graduate count in another. Contrast press (Telegraph via trade press) of EY cutting 1,800 to 1,600.

Quotes:
- "In FY25, we welcomed 764 talented individuals into the audit practice, of whom 532 were graduates and school leavers, as well as 232 on short term placements." — EY UK 2025 Transparency Report
- "During FY25, we welcomed 1,124 university and secondary school graduates, of whom 764 joined the audit practice." — EY UK 2025 Transparency Report, Talent attraction
- "Whilst technology will continue to impact how we work and the nature of the roles in audit" — EY UK 2025 Transparency Report
- "We are proud to be one of the UK’s largest student recruiters and are continuing to hire across our graduate, school leaver and internship programmes." — EY UK 2025 Transparency Report
- "around 3,500 trainees in our business at any one time" — EY UK 2025 Transparency Report
