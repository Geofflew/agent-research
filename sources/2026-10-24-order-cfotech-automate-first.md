# AI is coming for the month-end close: what CFOs should automate first (CFOtech, 23 Jul 2026)

URL: https://cfotech.com.au/story/ai-is-coming-for-the-month-end-close-what-cfos-should-automate-first
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Trade-press explainer (Annexa, 23 Jul 2026) ranking close tasks by AI-readiness: bank/transaction reconciliation first (repetitive, structured, cheap to review), then exception/accrual surveillance, then flux drafting; autonomous JE posting called out as not ready. Cites Consero 97% adoption vs General Atlantic 17% core-workflow use. Opinion plus second-hand stats; no primary survey by task.
