# Exposure Draft: Standard on Auditing (SA) 500, Audit Evidence, ICAI (ISA 500 converged)

URL: https://taxguru.in/chartered-accountant/exposure-draft-standard-auditing-500-audit-evidence.html
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Hosted copy of the ICAI exposure draft of SA 500, mirroring ISA 500 application para A2. Inquiry may yield important evidence or even evidence of a misstatement, but alone ordinarily does not establish absence of material misstatement or control effectiveness. Exposure draft, not final text.

Quotes:
- "although inquiry may provide important audit evidence, and may even produce evidence of a misstatement, inquiry alone ordinarily does not provide sufficient audit evidence of the absence of a material misstatement at the assertion level, nor of the operating effectiveness of controls." — Exposure Draft: Standard on Auditing (SA) 500
- "reasonable assurance is obtained when the auditor has obtained sufficient appropriate audit evidence to reduce audit risk" — Exposure Draft: Standard on Auditing (SA) 500
