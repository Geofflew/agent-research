# 17 CFR 240.13a-14 - Certification of disclosure in annual and quarterly reports

URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/240.13a-14
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Rule requiring principal executive and financial officers to sign Section 302 certifications; certification cannot be signed by power of attorney. Personal, non-delegable signature.

Quotes:
- "a person required to provide a certification specified in paragraph (a), (b) or (d) of this section may not have the certification signed on his or her behalf pursuant to a power of attorney or other form of confirming authority." — Cornell LII reproduction of eCFR, 17 CFR 240.13a-14(a),(c)
- "each principal executive and principal financial officer of the issuer" — Cornell LII reproduction of eCFR, 17 CFR 240.13a-14(a),(c)
