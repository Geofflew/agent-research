# A Technology Control Testing Automation Case Study (ISACA Journal 2021 vol 5)

URL: https://www.isaca.org/resources/isaca-journal/issues/2021/volume-5/a-technology-control-testing-automation-case-study
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: ISACA Journal case study, US regional bank, ~300 systems. Sampling 25 apps replaced by automated indicators over 100 percent of population plus continuous monitoring; only exceptions evaluated. Argues for full-population monitoring of control attributes, not sampling. Monitors outcomes vs rule attributes, so supports monitoring over human re-performance, and also a record of exceptions (partial support for C3).

Quotes:
- "increase scope coverage to 100 percent of systems, eliminating sampling." — ISACA Journal 2021 vol 5, benefits of automation
- "thus allowing full coverage of the target population rather than using a sampling of 25 applications in the prior state." — ISACA Journal, case study results
