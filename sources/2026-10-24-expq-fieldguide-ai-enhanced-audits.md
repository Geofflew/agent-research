Summary: Audit-software vendor blog on full-population testing. Population testing depends on clean client data in consistent formats; auditors must validate completeness and accuracy across the full dataset. Notes no PCAOB/AICPA standard specific to full-population testing.

- "Rather than selecting samples and extrapolating results, auditors can analyze entire transaction populations to identify exceptions requiring investigation." — Fieldguide
- "they must validate data completeness and accuracy across the full dataset" — Fieldguide
