# Searchlight Minerals Corp. Financial Reporting and Disclosure Controls and Procedures Policy (8-K Ex. 99.1, 2012)

URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1084226/000114420412029870/v313077_ex99-1.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: A listed company's own written procedure, filed as an exhibit. Sub-certifications come from selected corporate and business-unit management and heads of key business or staff units, in support of CEO/CFO 302 certifications, tailored to each signer's area, with a mechanism to resolve disagreements. Ad hoc "as requested by the CEO and CFO" before each 10-Q/10-K; no process/control-owner level and no automated controls.
