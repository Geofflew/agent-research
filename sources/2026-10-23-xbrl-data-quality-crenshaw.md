# Thomson Reuters Tax: SEC Commissioner Crenshaw urges better XBRL tagging (Nov 2021)

URL: https://tax.thomsonreuters.com/news/sec-commissioner-crenshaw-urges-companies-to-improve-xbrl-tagging/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: News report on Crenshaw speech, XBRL Investor Forum 10 Nov 2021. Structured data helps analysts but quality/accuracy gaps. Some found material error rates in tagged revenues, net income, assets; scaling errors. Filers primarily responsible; EDGAR flags e.g. outdated tags; XBRL US validation voluntary. Inline XBRL (June 2018) embeds tags to cut errors. No error-rate number given.

Quotes:
- "some have found material error rates in data tagged in sec filings, including errors in revenues, net income, and asset" — Thomson Reuters Tax reporting Commissioner Crenshaw, Nov 2021
- "some also found impactful scaling errors" — Thomson Reuters Tax reporting Crenshaw
- "in june 2018, the sec upgraded the rule with a requirement for companies to embed xbrl tags directly into the financial statements to reduce error rates and lower costs" — Thomson Reuters Tax
- "the primary responsibility lies with filers, of course, and there are many tools available to aid with the submission of high-quality, accurate data" — Commissioner Crenshaw, quoted by Thomson Reuters Tax
