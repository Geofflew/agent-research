# ABL Advisor: Elevated EBITDA addbacks continue (S&P Global Ratings study)

URL: https://www.abladvisor.com/news/19810/elevated-ebitda-addbacks-continue-sp-global-ratings
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: Trade-press summary of S&P Global Ratings addback study, Nov 2020 (S&P page itself returns 403). 2015-19 LBO/M&A: addbacks 28% of marketing EBITDA, reported EBITDA 27-30% below marketing. Net leverage 2.8-3.3 turns above forecast. Covenant baskets tied to EBITDA.

Quotes:
- "addbacks inflated marketing ebitda by an average of over 28% and accounted for 54% of reported last-12-months ebitda at deal inception" — ABL Advisor summarizing S&P Global Ratings, Nov 25 2020
- "marketing ebitda (including addbacks) generally does not provide a realistic indication of future ebitda and companies also consistently overestimate debt repayment" — ABL Advisor summarizing S&P Global Ratings
- "they also contribute to incremental event risk, as many covenant baskets are tied to ebitda" — ABL Advisor summarizing S&P Global Ratings
- "reported ebitda coming in at 27% below marketing ebitda in 2018 and below 30% in 2019" — ABL Advisor summarizing S&P Global Ratings
- "our data show a stark contrast in projection performance for sponsored and nonsponsored transactions, with nonsponsored deals vastly outperforming the former" — ABL Advisor summarizing S&P Global Ratings
