# ILPA Reporting Template (updated 2025) overview page

URL: https://ilpa.org/industry-guidance/templates-standards-model-documents/reporting-template
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: ILPA's primary page. The 2025-updated template replaces the 2016 one for funds still investing in Q1 2026 or launching from 1 Jan 2026; older funds may stay on 2016. Page does not itself list the changes (linked guides do). Bears on fund-level reporting standardization, not portfolio-company close.

Quotes:
- "is intended to replace the 2016 ILPA Reporting Template on a go-forward basis for Funds still in their investment period during Q1 2026 or for Funds commencing operations on or after January 1, 2026" — ILPA
- "GPs may continue to provide the 2016 Reporting Template, available below, for Funds that are no longer in their investment period as of Q1 2026" — ILPA
