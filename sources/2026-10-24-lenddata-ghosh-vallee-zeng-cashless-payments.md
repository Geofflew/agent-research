# FinTech Lending and Cashless Payments (Ghosh, Vallee, Zeng)

URL: https://rodneywhitecenter.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Zeng_2021_WP-Fintech-lending-and-cashless-payments.pdf
Type: primary
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: Working paper (Aug 2021) using loan applications to Indifi, an Indian FinTech lender to small businesses, with the 2016 demonetization as IV. Higher share of verifiable cashless payments predicts higher approval, lower rate, lower default. Argues the synergy supports data sharing and open banking and a lender model without a balance sheet or relationships.

Quotes:
- "a larger use of cashless payments predicts a higher likelihood of loan approval, a lower interest rate, and lower default" — authors, abstract
- "an interquartile increase in the share of cashless payments corresponds to a likelihood that is 0.087 * 31 = 2.7 percentage points lower to get a loan approved" — authors, section 4 (printed as shown; the sentence is about cashless share though it says 'lower')
- "an interest rate that is lower by 1.556 * 27 = 42 basis points" — authors, Table 2 discussion
- "This synergy supports data sharing and open banking, and more broadly the development of an alternative banking model without a balance sheet or traditional banking relationships." — authors, abstract
- "We thank Indifi for generously providing us access to their data." — authors, acknowledgments
