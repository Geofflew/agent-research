Summary: Press summary of Minnis (2011, JAR): audited firms pay interest rates "nearly three-quarters of a percent" lower. Roughly 75 bp, journalist paraphrase, not the paper table. Also notes audits were not always requested of $10M-revenue firms yet sometimes of $500K firms; audit cost $15-20K for the smallest businesses.
