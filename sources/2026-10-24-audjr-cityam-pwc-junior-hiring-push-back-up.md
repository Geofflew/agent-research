# City AM: PwC expects junior hiring to 'push back up' despite AI fears

URL: https://www.cityam.com/?p=2463701
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: City AM, autumn 2026. PwC UK CEO Amitrano denies AI is reducing graduate hiring; blames economic instability. PwC UK intake about 1,300 "broadly consistent" with prior year (the 2025 cut was 1,500 to 1,300). Firm-side counter-evidence to the AI-cuts premise.

Quotes:
- "I don't see a single statement of 'AI is going to reduce the number of young people we are hiring' as a valid statement at all" — Marco Amitrano, PwC UK CEO
- "the pinch point of the last year or two" — Amitrano, on economic instability and lower investment rather than AI
- "we're investing in AI tools and the education system so that young people are more work ready" — Amitrano
- "PwC's UK hired around 1300 young people this year." — City AM
