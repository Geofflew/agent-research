# Choi and Xie, "Human + AI in Accounting: Early Evidence from the Field", Journal of Accounting Research 64(3), 2026 (abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/joares/v64y2026i3p1333-1373.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Peer-reviewed study of professional accountants (survey n=277; field data from 79 SMEs, 200,000+ transaction records) plus a framed field experiment on transaction classification, scored against correct classifications. AI raises accuracy on average, but following non-consensus AI recommendations raises error risk; accountants intervene when AI confidence is low. Closest found to finance-staff automation-bias evidence; abstract only (full text 403 on Wiley/SSRN/Stanford), so no acceptance-rate-of-wrong-suggestions figure. Not seeded-error review of entries; bookkeeping at SME firms, not enterprise close. A genuine search found no other study of company accountants reviewing AI entries against known answers.
