# Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update), ACDIS/AHIMA

URL: https://www.acdis.org/articles/2019-update-guidelines-achieving-compliant-query-practice
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Joint ACDIS/AHIMA practice brief (full text on acdis.org; superseded by 2022 update, same core rules) governing how providers are queried for documentation. Rules: no reimbursement impact, no steering to a specific answer, patient-specific clinical indicators required, all clinically supported options plus an "other/unable to determine" escape, yes/no only for already-documented diagnoses, no repeat-asking until the desired answer arrives. Applies to anyone whose interaction could alter coded data, regardless of title or technology.

Quotes:
- "never include impact on reimbursement or quality measures" — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "avoid queries that: fail to include clinical indicators that justify the query or justify the choices provided within a multiple-choice format encourage the provider to a specific diagnosis or procedure indicate the impact on reimbursement, payment methodology, or quality metrics" — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "a query must adhere to compliant, non-leading standards, permitting the provider of record to unbiasedly respond with a specific diagnosis or procedure. references to reimbursement must not occur." — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "all clinically supported options should be included as well as additional options that permit the provider to craft their own alternate response. options may include other, unknown, unable to determine, not clinically significant, integral to, or other similar wording." — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "yes/no queries should only be employed to clarify documented diagnoses that need further specification. yes/no queries may not be used in circumstances where only clinical indicators of a condition are present, and the condition/diagnosis has not yet been documented in the health record." — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "it is considered non-compliant to continue asking the same query to the same or multiple providers until a desired response is received." — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "regardless of the credential, role, title, or use of technology, all healthcare professionals (whether or not they are ahima or acdis members) seeking to clarify provider documentation must follow compliant query guidelines." — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "trying to obtain too much information in one query may result in a non-compliant query." — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
- "regardless of the format and technology used, a query should not direct the provider to document a specific response." — Guidelines for Achieving a Compliant Query Practice (2019 update)
