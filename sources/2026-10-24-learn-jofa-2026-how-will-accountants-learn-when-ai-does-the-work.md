# How will accountants learn new skills when AI does the work? (Journal of Accountancy, Mar 2026)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/issues/2026/mar/how-will-accountants-learn-new-skills-when-ai-does-the-work/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: AICPA magazine feature (Hannah Pitstick) quoting AICPA's Carl Mayes, BYU's David Wood and firm leaders. States that automation is taking the repetitive, low-risk work juniors used to learn systems, controls and skepticism from (e.g. vouching), and asks how to train when "the training work" disappears. Mayes: supervising a bot requires understanding the procedure. AICPA "Profession Ready" research on early-career skills due 2027. Professional-body journalism, not a study.

Quotes:
- "automation and artificial intelligence (ai) are taking over much of the repetitive, low-risk work young accountants used to do to learn about systems, controls, and professional skepticism" — source page text
- "how do you train accountants when "the training work" is disappearing?" — source page text
- "for us to supervise the bot, we have to understand the underlying procedure, even if we're never going to do the procedure ourselves" — source page text
