# Sierra blog: outcome-based pricing for AI agents

URL: https://sierra.ai/blog/outcome-based-pricing-for-ai-agents
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Dec 2024. Sierra paid only when agent completes task (resolved convo, saved cancellation, upsell). Escalation usually free. Argues seat vendors conflicted: better AI means fewer seats. Blended with per-conversation for routing.

Quotes:
- "Sierra gets paid only when we complete a task for you." — Elliot Greenwald, Sierra blog, 10 Dec 2024
- "If the conversation is unresolved, in most cases, there's no charge." — Elliot Greenwald, Sierra blog
- "the more effective their AI becomes, the fewer contact center seats their clients need" — Elliot Greenwald, Sierra blog, on legacy CX vendors
- "How much will my seat-based license bill shrink?" — Elliot Greenwald, Sierra blog, suggested buyer question
- "routing or greeter-style interactions may align better with consumption-based pricing, where payment is based on conversation count, regardless of the outcome" — Elliot Greenwald, Sierra blog
