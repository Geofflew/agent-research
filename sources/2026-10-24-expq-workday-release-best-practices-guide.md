# Workday Release Best Practices guide

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://forms.workday.com/content/dam/web/sg/documents/other/release-best-practices-guide-en-sg.pdf
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: Workday vendor guide: twice-yearly feature releases (typically March and September) plus weekly service updates, most features disabled by default. Customers test in a sandbox preview copy during a five-week preparation window, focusing on integrations, key business processes and custom reports; "adoption items" are assigned to functional teams. Says nothing about SOX or auditor evidence; the customer-side review is framed as feature adoption, not control change management.
