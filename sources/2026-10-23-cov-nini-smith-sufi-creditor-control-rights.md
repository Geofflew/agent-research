# Nini, Smith & Sufi, Creditor control rights, corporate governance, and firm value (HLS Forum summary)

URL: https://corpgov.law.harvard.edu/2011/03/11/creditor-control-rights-corporate-governance-and-firm-value
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Authors summarise SEC-filing evidence 1996-2008: 10-20 percent of firms in any year report a financial covenant violation. Violations are followed by amended agreements with stronger restrictions, lower capex and leverage, and higher CEO turnover; violators are not near payment default.

Quotes:
- "between 10 percent and 20 percent of firms report being in violation of a financial covenant in a credit agreement" — Nini, page text
- "violations are followed immediately with a decline in acquisitions and capital expenditures" — Nini, page text
- "amended credit agreements that contain stronger restrictions on firm decision-making" — Nini, page text
- "violators are not on the verge of bankruptcy or payment default" — Nini, page text
