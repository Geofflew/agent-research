# Pay.UK: Confirmation of Payee reaches two billion checks

URL: https://newseventsinsights.wearepay.uk/media-centre/press-releases/confirmation-of-payee-reaches-two-billion-checks-helping-protect-uk-consumers-against-fraud/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: Pay.UK 18 Mar 2024: CoP 2bn checks, name vs receiving account; mismatch alerts payer w/ risk warning, payer can still proceed. Claims "instrumental" in cutting fraud but gives no CoP-specific effect size; only APP fraud -1% H1 2023 industry-wide. Operator self-report.

Quotes:
- "works by prompting a banking customer to verify that the name they input matches the name on the account receiving the payment. If the names do not match, the banking customer is alerted and informed of the associated risks of proceeding with a payment when a match cannot be confirmed." — Pay.UK
- "APP fraud, a type of fraud specifically targeted by CoP, has also seen a 1% fall compared to the same period the previous year." — Pay.UK
- "The service has been instrumental in reducing fraud and misdirected payments." — Pay.UK
- "the Payment System Regulator’s mandate for over 350 organisations to join CoP in 2024" — Pay.UK
