# CPA Practice Advisor: Faster financial period closes are extremely valuable, say CFOs (CohnReznick survey)

URL: https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2014/03/05/faster-financial-period-closes-are-extremely-valuable-say-cfos/14506/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: 2014 report of a CohnReznick survey of CFOs and financial managers. More than 70 percent of respondents named five factors keeping the close from being shorter; the first listed is waiting for information from other people. Old (2014) and the page gives the factors as a list, not a ranking with separate percentages.

Quotes:
- "According to more than 70 percent of the survey respondents, five factors stood between their wish for a shorter financial close: Waiting for information from other people" — CPA Practice Advisor on CohnReznick survey
- "Lacking visibility into where things stand in the close process" — second factor in the same list
- "picking up an additional day or two is very important because it creates high-value analytical time" — John Macrae, Principal, CohnReznick
