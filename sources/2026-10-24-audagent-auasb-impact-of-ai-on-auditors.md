# Impact of AI on Auditors (AUASB, July 2025)

URL: https://auasb.gov.au/publications/impact-of-ai-on-auditors/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Australian standard setter's webpage on auditors' own AI audit tools and on audited entities' AI. Endorses AI tools for independently assessing completeness and accuracy of entity information, advises running the tool in parallel with other procedures in year one, and warns of automation bias and that re-performance of changing AI logic may not be possible. Tells auditors to test entity AI output like their own tool output.

Quotes:
- "Consideration should be given to using a tool in parallel with other procedures in the first year" — AUASB, Impact of AI on Auditors
- "AI can create challenges that go beyond 'black boxes' because the logic used can change and code reviews and reperformance may not be possible." — AUASB
- "Auditors should exercise caution not to over rely on the information generated using technology (automation bias)." — AUASB, on audited entities' AI
- "The auditor may be unable to challenge information and judgements underlying the entity's financial/sustainability report if the data used and logic applied by the AI tool cannot be explained" — AUASB
