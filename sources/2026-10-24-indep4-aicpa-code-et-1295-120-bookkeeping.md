# AICPA Code, ET 1.295.120 Bookkeeping, Payroll, and Other Disbursements

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pub.aicpa.org/codeofconduct/ethicsresources/et-cod.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Official AICPA Code (2026 edition). A firm may record client-classified transactions, post client-coded entries and propose journal entries management reviews, but determining or changing entries or account codings without client approval, authorizing transactions, or preparing source documents impairs independence with no safeguard. This is the line any close agent run by an attest firm must stay on the right side of.

Quotes:
- "record transactions to an attest client’s general ledger when management has determined or approved the account classifications for the transaction." — ET 1.295.120 .02a
- "post transactions coded by the attest client to the attest client’s general ledger." — ET 1.295.120 .02b
- "Prior to the member posting these journal entries or changes, the member should be satisfied that management has reviewed the entries and understands the nature of the proposed entries" — ET 1.295.120 .02e
- "prepare a reconciliation (for example, bank and accounts receivable) that identifies reconciling items for the client’s evaluation." — ET 1.295.120 .02i
- "determines or changes journal entries, any account coding or classification of transactions, or any other accounting records without first obtaining the attest client’s" — ET 1.295.120 .03a
- "authorizes or approves transactions." — ET 1.295.120 .03b
- "makes changes to source documents without the attest client’s approval." — ET 1.295.120 .03d
- "approves vendor invoices for payment." — ET 1.295.120 .03h
- "pose such a significant self-review threat that no safeguards can eliminate or reduce the threats" — ET 1.295.010 .17 (preparing source documents cross-reference)
