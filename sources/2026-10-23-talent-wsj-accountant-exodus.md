# WSJ via Fox Business: Why so many accountants are quitting

URL: https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/why-accountants-quitting
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: WSJ Dec 2022 piece (Lindsay Ellis) syndicated on Fox Business. 300k+ US accountants/auditors left in 2 yrs, -17%, per BLS. Exits across ages 25-34 and 45-54, not just retirees. Pipeline cannot refill; KPMG, PwC raising pay.

Quotes:
- "More than 300,000 U.S. accountants and auditors have left their jobs in the past two years, a 17% decline, and the dwindling number of college students coming into the field can't fill the gap." — Lindsay Ellis, WSJ via Fox Business
- "Young professionals in the 25- to 34-year-old range and midcareer professionals between the ages of 45 and 54 also departed in high numbers starting in 2019, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics." — Lindsay Ellis, WSJ via Fox Business
- "Recruiters who have been luring experienced accountants into new roles say they are often moving into jobs in finance and technology." — Lindsay Ellis, WSJ via Fox Business
- "The huge gap between companies that need accountants and trained professionals has led to salary bumps and more temporary workers joining the sector." — Lindsay Ellis, WSJ via Fox Business
- "The firm, which needs to recruit 4,200 entry-level associates for fall 2023" — Lindsay Ellis, WSJ via Fox Business, on PwC
