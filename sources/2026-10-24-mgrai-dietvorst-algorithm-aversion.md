# Algorithm Aversion: People Erroneously Avoid Algorithms after Seeing Them Err (Dietvorst, Simmons, Massey 2015)

URL: https://repository.upenn.edu/fnce_papers/58
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: JEP:General 2015, 5 studies. After seeing algorithm err, people drop it for worse human forecaster, even when algorithm outperformed.

Quotes:
- "people more quickly lose confidence in algorithmic than human forecasters after seeing them make the same mistake" — abstract
- "Participants who saw the algorithm perform were less confident in it, and less likely to choose it over an inferior human forecaster" — abstract
- "This was true even among those who saw the algorithm outperform the human." — abstract
