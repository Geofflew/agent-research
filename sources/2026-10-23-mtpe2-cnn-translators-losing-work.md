# CNN: translators losing jobs and income to AI (Jan 2026)

URL: https://www.cnn.com/2026/01/23/tech/translation-language-jobs-ai-automation-intl/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: CNN Business, 23 Jan 2026. Irish-language EU translator lost ~70% income. SoA 2024: over 1/3 translators lost work, 43% income down. Oxford (Frey, Llanos-Paredes) US 2010-23: ~28,000 translator jobs not added due to MT. Contradicts C9 flat-headcount premise; evidence is slower growth + survey, not absolute headcount fall.

Quotes:
- "he says he lost about 70% of his income when the eu translation work dried up." — CNN, Timothy McKeon, Irish-language translator for EU institutions
- "and 43% of translators said their income had dropped because of the technology." — CNN, on UK Society of Authors 2024 survey
- "our best baseline estimate is that roughly 28,000 more jobs for translators would've been added in the absence of machine translation," — Carl Frey, Oxford, to CNN
- "machine translation has reduced the amount of work available to human translators and interpreters, and depressed their earnings." — CNN article description
