# AASB 134 Interim Financial Reporting, recognition and measurement (AASB; IAS 34 equivalent)

URL: https://standards.aasb.gov.au/node/837
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Australian adoption of IAS 34. Says interim reports generally need greater use of estimation methods than annual reports, and measurement must still ensure reliable information. IFRS-side counterpart to ASC 270.

Quotes:
- "While measurements in both annual and interim financial reports are often based on reasonable estimates, the preparation of interim financial reports generally will require a greater use of estimation methods than annual financial reports." — AASB 134 para 41
- "The measurement procedures to be followed in an interim financial report shall be designed to ensure that the resulting information is reliable" — AASB 134 para 41
