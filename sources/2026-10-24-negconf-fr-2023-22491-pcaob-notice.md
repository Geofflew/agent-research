# Federal Register: PCAOB Notice of Filing, The Auditor's Use of Confirmation (88 FR 71684, 17 Oct 2023)

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-10-17/pdf/2023-22491.pdf
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: SEC notice reproducing the PCAOB rationale for new AS 2310. Under existing AS 2310 negative confirmations could be sole evidence if criteria were met; the new standard bars sole use, allowing them only to supplement other substantive procedures. Reason given: electronic correspondence volume makes recipients less likely to consider the request. Novel admissions: PCAOB staff found few if any large firms use them as sole procedure, yet some large-firm methodologies still allowed it; the Board says intermediaries and email are now commonplace.

Quotes:
- "With a negative confirmation request, the auditor requests a confirmation response only if the confirming party disagrees with the information provided in the request." — PCAOB rationale as quoted in the notice
- "A confirming party might not respond to a negative confirmation request because it did not receive or open the request" — notice, on why silence is ambiguous
- "negative confirmation requests may not be used as the sole substantive audit procedure under the new standard" — notice, economic analysis
- "the increasing likelihood that a recipient of a negative confirmation request would not consider the request" — notice, reason for the limit
- "few, if any, GNFs use negative confirmation requests as the sole substantive procedure in practice" — notice, Board's oversight observation
- "some GNF methodologies still allow the use of negative confirmation requests as the sole substantive procedure under certain conditions" — notice, PCAOB staff methodology review
- "negative confirmation requests have typically been used to obtain audit evidence related to the completeness of deposit liabilities" — notice, current practice
- "numerous financial institutions in the United States, and an increasing number of international banks, mandate the use of an intermediary" — notice, on electronic confirmation platforms
- "Both GNFs and NAFs within the scope of the PCAOB staff" — notice, staff review of third-party tools automating confirmation procedures
