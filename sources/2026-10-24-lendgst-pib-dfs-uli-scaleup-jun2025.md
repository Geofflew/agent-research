# PIB: DFS meeting to scale up the Unified Lending Interface (23 Jun 2025)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2139039
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: DFS Secretary and RBI Deputy Governor T. Rabi Sankar call for government datasets to be integrated into ULI, framed as a lending DPI comparable to UPI. Does not mention GST data by name; shows the policy intent that lenders pull trusted government data instead of borrower-prepared documents.

Quotes:
- "ULI is envisioned as a Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) in the lending space" — PIB, DFS Secretary, 23 Jun 2025
- "rich, trusted and high-value datasets available with Central Government Ministries/Departments and State governments" — PIB, DFS Secretary
- "ULI can surpass the transformative impact of UPI" — PIB, Deputy Governor T. Rabi Sankar
- "Digitally integrate high-value datasets available with the Central Govt. Ministries and State Governments with ULI" — PIB, meeting resolve
