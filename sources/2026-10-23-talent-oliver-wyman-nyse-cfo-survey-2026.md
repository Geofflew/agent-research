# Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026: finance workforce

URL: https://www.oliverwymanforum.com/agenda-cfo/playbook-cfo-strategy-growth-cost-ai.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Survey N=494 CFOs. 91% expect flat (61%) or lower (30%) finance headcount in 3 yrs. 64% expect shift away from junior roles; pyramid to diamond. Only 8% deployed AI/agents at scale. AI = freeze/shrink + upgrade mix, not fill seats.

Quotes:
- "91% of CFOs expect to either hold their finance workforce size relatively flat (61%) or decrease it by more than 10% (30%), with only 9% expecting an increase of more than 10%." — Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026
- "CFOs envision leaner finance functions and a shift away from junior roles, but not a one-directional shift in operating model." — Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026
- "The most common expected mix shift is toward midlevel roles (41%), followed by a shift toward senior roles (23%)." — Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026
- "Only 13% expect a shift toward more junior roles." — Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026
- "the traditional finance pyramid is starting to flatten into more of a middle-heavy diamond." — Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026
- "Many organizations are planning to freeze or reduce headcount overall" — Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026
- "8% have deployed AI-assisted tools or autonomous agents at scale" — Oliver Wyman Forum x NYSE CFO Survey 2026
