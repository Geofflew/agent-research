# Data Act Articles 29 and 31: switching charges and custom-built exemption

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/29/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Art. 29 abolishes switching charges from 12 January 2027; reduced, cost-linked charges allowed until then. Art. 31 exempts custom-built services not offered at broad commercial scale from Art. 23(d), 29 and 30(1),(3), and test versions from the whole chapter. Art. 29(5) obliges providers to disclose services where switching is highly complex or impossible without significant interference in service architecture. The rules price exit; they set no duty to expose functions to outside agents.

Quotes:
- "From 12 January 2027, providers of data processing services shall not impose any switching charges on the customer for the switching process." — Art. 29(1)
- "from 11 January 2024 to 12 January 2027" — Art. 29(2), reduced charges window
- "shall not exceed the costs incurred by the provider of data processing services that are directly linked to the switching process" — Art. 29(3)
- "highly complex or costly switching or for which it is impossible to switch without significant interference" — Art. 29(5)
