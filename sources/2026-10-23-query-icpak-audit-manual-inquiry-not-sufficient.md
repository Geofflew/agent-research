# Audit Manual 12: Audit Evidence (ISA 500-based), ICPAK

URL: https://audit.icpak.com/manual/guide/audit-manual/12.html
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Professional-body audit manual restating the ISA 500 audit-procedure list. Inquiry (asking knowledgeable persons) is a procedure but alone does not suffice for misstatement detection or control testing; responses must be evaluated and corroborated, and management-intent inquiries rest on track record. Used because pcaobus.org returns 403 to scripted fetches.

Quotes:
- "inquiry alone ordinarily does not provide sufficient audit evidence to detect a material misstatement at the assertion level. moreover, inquiry alone is not sufficient to test the operating effectiveness of controls." — Audit Manual 12: Audit Evidence (ISA 500-based)
- "evaluating responses to inquiries is an integral part of the inquiry process." — Audit Manual 12: Audit Evidence (ISA 500-based)
- "alternatively, responses might provide information that differs significantly from other information that the auditor has obtained" — Audit Manual 12: Audit Evidence (ISA 500-based)
