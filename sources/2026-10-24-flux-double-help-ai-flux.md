# Double help center: AI Flux Analysis

URL: https://help.doublehq.com/en/articles/15024367-ai-flux-analysis
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Help article (Aug 2026). Dollar and/or percent thresholds (amount, percent, OR, AND) decide which lines get an AI description; AI reviews underlying transactions. Descriptions fully editable, pushed to footnotes or comments, bulk Mark as reviewed. Stale descriptions flagged orange when transactions change. Reviewer-model choice offered.

- "Double's AI reviews the underlying transactions and produces a description for you." — Double help center, intro
- "AI-generated descriptions are fully editable." — Double help center, Editing
