# Comply with the newly effective 'Hosting Services' interpretation (JofA, Jul 2019)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/news/2019/jul/hosting-services-independence-ethics-interpretation-201921655/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Post-effective-date JofA guidance with AICPA FAQ content. Test for hosting: could the client switch providers without the member's held data. Portals must be cleansed; own backups required; a third-party GL on the firm's server is hosting if client books are incomplete without it. Sharing vs storing is the line.

Quotes:
- "a new ethics interpretation took effect on July 1" — JofA, Jul 2019
- "CPAs should not perform any activities for their clients that are management's responsibility." — JofA, Jul 2019
- "If the client could not make such a change without relying on data, records, and documents held by the member, the member is likely to be providing hosting services." — JofA, Jul 2019
- "having a third party's general ledger on a server owned or leased by the member would result in the provision of hosting services if the client's books and records are not complete without the client having to contact the member or having access to the member's server" — JofA, Jul 2019, summarizing AICPA FAQs
- "But when a member stores a client's documentation rather than sharing it, the member is likely to be providing hosting services and independence may be impaired." — JofA, Jul 2019
- "If a member's copy of those files and data is relied upon for business continuity or disaster recovery for an attest client, the member would be providing hosting services." — JofA, Jul 2019
- "Craig explained that it's OK for a member to keep a copy of the client records, as long as the client also has a copy of those records." — JofA, Jul 2019 (AICPA Professional Ethics team)
- "caution should be taken when an attest client repeatedly requests copies of data or records" — JofA, Jul 2019
- "is not meant to discourage cloud- or technology-based means of exchanging files and documents with clients" — JofA, Jul 2019
