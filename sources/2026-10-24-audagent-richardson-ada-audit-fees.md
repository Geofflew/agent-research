# Audit data analytics implemented by internal audit and subsequent external audit fees (Richardson, PolyU seminar abstract, Mar 2024)

URL: https://www.polyu.edu.hk/af/news-and-events/event/2024/03/20240328-prof-vernon-j-richardson
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Abstract of an archival study (seminar abstract, not confirmed peer-reviewed) of client internal audit functions implementing audit data analytics. Client-side analytics are associated with lower later external audit fees and delays, strongest for diagnostic analytics. This is evidence on client analytics lowering fees, not on external auditors re-performing the population themselves.

Quotes:
- "the internal audit implementation of ADA is negatively associated with subsequent external audit fees and audit delays" — Vernon Richardson, seminar abstract
- "ADA technology focused on diagnostic analytics are negatively associated with subsequent audit fees as well as audit lags" — Richardson
- "lower material weaknesses, subsequent restatements, and discretionary accruals following ADA implementation by the internal audit function" — Richardson
- "there exists a void of evidence regarding the impact of this evolving technology within the accounting literature" — Richardson
