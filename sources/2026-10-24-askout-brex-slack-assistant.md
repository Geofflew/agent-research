# Brex help: Brex and Slack

URL: https://brex.com/support/brex-and-slack
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Brex messages employees in Slack about expenses missing receipts or memos per company policy; the employee replies via an Add button. Brex Assistant in Slack answers questions and updates expense info but is employee-initiated, and Brex disclaims accuracy.

Quotes:
- "Users will receive Slack messages about expenses missing receipts or memos per your company’s policy." — Brex Help Center
- "receive automatic reminders about outstanding tasks/transactions missing information" — Brex Help Center, Overview
- "Assistant messages will start with a diamond symbol to indicate an AI-generated response." — Brex Help Center
- "Brex Assistant may make mistakes, so it’s important to double-check any information that it generates." — Brex Help Center
- "Employees are scoped to their own data" — Brex Help Center, Brex in Slackbot via MCP
