# Great Models Think Alike and this Undermines AI Oversight (Goel et al., 2025)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04313
Type: primary
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: Proposes CAPA, a chance-adjusted similarity metric based on overlap in model mistakes. LLM-as-judge scores favor models similar to the judge, and weak-to-strong gains depend on complementary knowledge between supervisor and student. Finds model mistakes are becoming more similar as capabilities increase, so oversight by another model carries correlated-failure risk.

Quotes:
- "LLM-as-a-judge scores favor models similar to the judge, generalizing recent self-preference results" — Goel et al., arXiv abstract
- "model mistakes are becoming more similar with increasing capabilities, pointing to risks from correlated failures" — Goel et al., arXiv abstract
- "Our work underscores the importance of reporting and correcting for model similarity, especially in the emerging paradigm of AI oversight." — Goel et al., arXiv abstract
