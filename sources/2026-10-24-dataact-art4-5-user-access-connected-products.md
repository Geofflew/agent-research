# Data Act Articles 4 and 5: user access and sharing of product data

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/5/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Art. 5 (and Art. 4) of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 give a user of a connected product or related service the right to have product data and related service data made available to the user or a chosen third party. Data holders are the manufacturers/providers of connected products. The right is a read/share right over generated data; it creates no right to post or create records in the service. A cloud ERP is not a connected product.

Quotes:
- "upon request by a user, or by a party acting on behalf of a user, the data holder shall make available readily available data" — Art. 5(1)
- "in a comprehensive, structured, commonly used and machine-readable format" — Art. 5(1)
