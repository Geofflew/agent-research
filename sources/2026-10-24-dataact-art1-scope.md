# Data Act Article 1: Subject matter and scope

URL: https://data-act-law.eu/article/1/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Art. 1 of Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (mirror text). Chapter II data-access rights apply to data on the performance, use and environment of connected products and related services (IoT), not to SaaS business records. Chapter VI (switching) applies to data and services processed by providers of data processing services. Nothing in the scope lists third-party write or action access to a service.

Quotes:
- "the making available of product data and related service data to the user of the connected product or related service" — Art. 1(1)(a)
- "facilitating switching between data processing services" — Art. 1(1)(d)
- "chapter ii applies to data, with the exception of content, concerning the performance, use and environment of connected products and related services" — Art. 1(2)(a)
- "chapter vi applies to any data and services processed by providers of data processing services" — Art. 1(2)(e)
