# SSARS AR-C section 80, Compilation Engagements (reporting when not independent)

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://irp.cdn-website.com/897bf1f7/files/uploaded/ar-c-00080.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Full text of AICPA AR-C section 80 as reproduced in a PDF hosted by a CPA firm site. Paragraphs .22-.23 let a non-independent accountant compile but require the lack of independence to be stated in a final paragraph of the report. Covers premise 2. Reproduction host is not the AICPA; text is the standard's.

Quotes:
- "When the accountant is not independent with respect to the entity, the accountant should indicate the accountant's lack of independence in a final paragraph of the accountant's compilation report." — AR-C 80.22, Reporting when the accountant is not independent
- "If the accountant elects to disclose a description about the reasons the accountant's independence is impaired, the accountant should include all such reasons in the description." — AR-C 80.23
