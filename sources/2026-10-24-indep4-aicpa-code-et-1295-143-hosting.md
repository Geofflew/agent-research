# AICPA Code, ET 1.295.143 Hosting Services

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pub.aicpa.org/codeofconduct/ethicsresources/et-cod.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Hosting (sole host of a client information system, sole custody of records, or security/backup of client data) means the firm maintains the client's internal control over its data, so independence is impaired with no safeguard. Carve-outs: separate software instances, client-contracted third-party cloud the firm merely accesses, portals, and software licensed to the client that performs no activity which would impair independence if the firm did it directly (relevant to firm-supplied AI tools).

- "Providing electronic security or back-up services for an attest client’s data or records" — ET 1.295.143 .01c
- "When a member provides hosting services, the member is maintaining the attest client’s" — ET 1.295.143 .02
- "Accordingly, the management participation threat" — ET 1.295.143 .02

- "The attest client enters into an agreement with a third-party service provider to maintain its software in a cloud-based solution and grants the member access to the software so that the member can perform the bookkeeping service" — ET 1.295.143 .04c.ii
