# Belgium e-invoice (FPS BOSA/Finance) FAQ: specific questions on e-invoicing

Summary: Official Belgian FAQ (updated 06.05.2026) on receiving Peppol invoices: Peppol does not detect duplicates, so the accounting system must; wrongly addressed invoices are rejected by Peppol Invoice Response code RE; missing reverse-charge detail is not a reason to reject but to contact the supplier. No matching-rate numbers.

Quotes:
- "Peppol doesn't know whether an invoice is a duplicate of the original invoice or not." — FPS Finance e-invoice FAQ Q4
- "Ideally, duplicate detection should be included in the accounting system." — FPS Finance e-invoice FAQ Q4
- "You should reject the invoice." — FPS Finance e-invoice FAQ Q28
- "You can reject the invoice via Peppol by sending an Invoice Response (IR) with status code ‘RE’ (Rejected)" — FPS Finance e-invoice FAQ Q28
- "You should not reject the invoice, but you can obviously contact your service provider to point out that this information is missing." — FPS Finance e-invoice FAQ Q27
