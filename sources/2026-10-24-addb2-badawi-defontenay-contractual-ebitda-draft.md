Summary: Full-text working paper draft (via r.jina.ai mirror of Columbia Law PDF). Contractual EBITDA definitions, not accruals, are the negotiated margin: more add-backs go with tighter slack AND fewer violations, so add-backs are read as removing noisy accruals, not loosening. Researchers using standardized (GAAP-like) definitions overstate violations; a rare passage tying definitions to violation incidence. Counter-evidence to the add-backs-loosen-covenants claim.
