# SAP extends support deadline for getting off legacy software (The Register, 28 Jan 2025)

URL: https://theregister.com/2025/01/28/sap_extends_support_deadline/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: SAP spokesperson and CEO statements on ECC dates: mainstream maintenance end 2027, extended 2030, private-edition transition option 2031-2033; Gartner migration share (Sept 2024) shows most ECC customers had not licensed S/4HANA.

Quotes:
- "this is not a prolongation of the mainstream maintenance deadlines communicated in 2020, namely the end of 2027 for SAP Business Suite 7 and the end of 2030 for extended maintenance" — SAP spokesperson, quoted by The Register
- "The option will be offered for the period from 2031 until the end of 2033" — SAP spokesperson, quoted by The Register
- "only 37 percent of ECC customers had licensed S/4HANA, compared with 34 percent in the same quarter last year" — Gartner figure (Sept 2024) as reported by The Register
- "little evidence that migrations to SAP S/4HANA were taking place at the rate needed to meet SAP's target" — The Register summarising Gartner
- "very few large customers [which would] not making the [deadline] because, to transform and consolidate ERP and business processes in over 100 countries is sometimes not that easy" — SAP CEO Christian Klein quoted on analyst call, The Register (the bracketed text is the Register's)
