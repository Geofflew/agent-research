# Sub-certifications are not guarantees for SOX 302 (Compliance Week, 2006)

URL: https://complianceweek.com/sub-certifications-are-not-guarantees-for-sox-302/6695.article
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Paywalled trade-press teaser (7 Mar 2006). States plainly that sub-certifications from lower-level employees are not required by the Act; only CEO and CFO certify under 302. Companies build them anyway for culture of compliance and to let officers "breathe easier".

Quotes:
- "from lower-level employees are not required by sarbanes; only chief executive and financial officers must certify results, as prescribed by the law's section 302" — Compliance Week (Martinek), 7 Mar 2006
- "a large number of companies are crafting sub-certification processes anyway" — Compliance Week (Martinek), 7 Mar 2006
- "the safest way to do that: back up those attestations all the way down the line" — Compliance Week (Martinek), 7 Mar 2006
