# Regulation (EU) 2023/2854 (Data Act), Articles 23, 25 and 29 on switching

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32023R2854
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Data Act obliges cloud (data processing) providers to let customers port "exportable data" and switch providers: 30-day transition, notice max two months, no switching charges from 12 Jan 2027. It guarantees export of data, not write access for third-party agents or action APIs; exportable data excludes provider trade secrets. Contrast with PSD2 Art. 66. Read via r.jina.ai.

Quotes:
- "porting the customer’s exportable data and digital assets, to a different provider of data processing services" — Data Act Art. 23(c)
- "which shall not exceed two months" — Data Act Art. 25(2)(d), max notice period
- "From 12 January 2027, providers of data processing services shall not impose any switching charges on the customer for the switching process." — Data Act Art. 29(1)
- "excluding any assets or data protected by intellectual property rights, or constituting a trade secret" — Data Act Art. 2(38), exportable data definition
