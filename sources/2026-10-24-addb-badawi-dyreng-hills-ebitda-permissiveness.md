# Badawi, de Fontenay, Dyreng, Hills, Contractual Complexity in Debt Agreements: The Case of EBITDA (seminar abstract)

URL: https://law-economic-studies.law.columbia.edu/content/fall-2021-blue-sky-talks
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Columbia seminar listing carrying the paper abstract. Counter-evidence: permissive EBITDA definitions (more adjustments) go with tighter covenants and fewer violations; authors read this as better informativeness, not looser covenants. Opposes the Fed add-backs paper.

Quotes:
- "We document significant variation in the contractual definition of EBITDA across syndicated loans and develop a permissiveness score based on the number of adjustments included in EBITDA definitions." — seminar abstract
- "We show that permissiveness is associated with tighter covenants but fewer violations." — seminar abstract
- "Market responses to covenant violations are more negative when permissiveness is higher." — seminar abstract
- "Our findings suggest that permissiveness in EBITDA definitions enhances the informativeness of covenant realizations by refining EBITDA to better reflect the borrower’s true financial condition." — seminar abstract
