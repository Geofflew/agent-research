# Amazon Bedrock model lifecycle (hosted models, Active / Legacy / EOL)

URL: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-lifecycle.html
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: For models launched on Bedrock on or after 7 Sep 2026, each model card states an EOL-no-sooner-than date and a Legacy notice period of 6 months or 45 days (most get 6 months). In Legacy, new customers cannot adopt and idle existing customers may lose access after 15 days. After EOL requests fail and migration is not automatic. Contrast with self-held open weights.

Quotes:
- "There are two Legacy periods: 6 months and 45 days." — Bedrock model lifecycle page
- "Most models have a 6-month Legacy period." — Bedrock model lifecycle page
- "existing customers may lose access after 15 days of inactivity" — Bedrock model lifecycle page, Legacy definition
- "migration will not happen automatically" — Bedrock model lifecycle page, EOL definition
- "Model lifecycle dates are specific to Amazon Bedrock and may differ from dates published by model providers" — Bedrock model lifecycle page, note
