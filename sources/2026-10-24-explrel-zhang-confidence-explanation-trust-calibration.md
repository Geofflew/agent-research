# Zhang, Liao, Bellamy, Effect of Confidence and Explanation on Accuracy and Trust Calibration in AI-Assisted Decision Making (FAT* 2020)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02114
Type: primary
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Two human experiments where human and AI accuracy are comparable. A confidence score calibrated trust, but calibrated trust alone did not improve joint accuracy. The authors flag problems with local explanations in this setting. Abstract only checked; wording on explanations is hedged, not a measured acceptance effect.

Quotes:
- "confidence score can help calibrate people's trust in an AI model, but trust calibration alone is not sufficient to improve AI-assisted decision making" — Zhang, Liao, Bellamy, abstract
- "We also highlight the problems in using local explanation for AI-assisted decision making scenarios" — Zhang, Liao, Bellamy, abstract
- "humans and AI have comparable performance alone" — Zhang, Liao, Bellamy, abstract
