# Independence interpretation on hosting services gets new effective date (JofA, Aug 2018)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/news/2018/aug/hosting-services-independence-effective-date-201819533/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: PEEC extended effective date of Hosting Services (ET 1.295.143) from Sept 1, 2018 to July 1, 2019, to let members work with vendors and clients.

Quotes:
- "PEEC voted last week to move the effective date to July 1, 2019; the ethics interpretation originally was scheduled to take effect on Sept. 1, 2018." — JofA, Aug 2018
- "The additional time is being provided to allow AICPA members to work with their third-party vendors and clients to implement any necessary changes resulting from the new interpretation." — JofA, Aug 2018
