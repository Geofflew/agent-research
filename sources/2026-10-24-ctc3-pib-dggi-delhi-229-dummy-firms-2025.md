# PIB: DGGI Delhi uncovers 229 dummy firms, Rs 645 crore ITC (Nov 2025)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2189791&reg=48&lang=2
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: DGGI release Nov 2025. 229 non-existent firms issued invoices with no supply, Rs 645 cr ineligible ITC. Syndicate controlled registrations, returns and banking, so returns and bank trail both look real. Recent, shows fraud persists after e-invoicing.

Quotes:
- "engaged in issuance of invoices without any actual supply of goods or services, resulting in the fraudulent passing of ineligible ITC amounting to Rs. 645 crore approx" — PIB DGGI Delhi release, 13 Nov 2025
- "managing GST registrations and returns and records of dummy firms, managing banking transactions, and facilitating the circulation of illicit funds through multiple layers" — PIB DGGI Delhi release
- "162 mobile phones, probably used to get OTPs for GST/banking purposes" — PIB DGGI Delhi release
