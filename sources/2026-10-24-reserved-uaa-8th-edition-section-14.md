# Uniform Accountancy Act, Eighth Edition (Jan 2018): attest reservation and Section 14(a)

URL: https://nasba.org/app/uploads/2018/02/Uniform-Accountancy-Act-%E2%80%93-Eighth-Edition-%E2%80%93-January-2018.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: NASBA/AICPA model act. Reserves the issuance of reports on financial statements, and attest and compilation services, to licensees and individuals with practice privileges. Expressly leaves preparation of financial statements and all other accounting work open to anyone. The reservation attaches to the signed report and to persons, not to the underlying estimates or judgments; the text does not mention software or AI (2018 edition, predates generative AI). Whether an AI agent could "issue a report" is not addressed.

Quotes:
- "(a)    Only licensees and individuals who have practice privileges under Section 23 of this" — UAA Section 14(a), Unlawful Acts
- "the preparation of financial statements without the issuance of reports thereon. Non-" — UAA Section 14(a)
- "is the cornerstone prohibition of the UAA, reserving the performance" — UAA Section 14(a) comment
- "Act reserves the issuance of reports in standard form on audited, reviewed and compiled financial" — UAA introduction
- "may perform these services and must do so only through firms registered with a state board." — UAA introduction
- "licensed or not, may offer and perform any other kind of accounting service, including tax services," — UAA key features, point 1
- "statements and other attest information to licensees who have demonstrated qualifications to perform" — UAA introduction
