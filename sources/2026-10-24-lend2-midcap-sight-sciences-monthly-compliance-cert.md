# MidCap / Sight Sciences term loan, First Amendment (monthly compliance certificate), 2021

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1531177/000095017021004130/sght-ex10_2.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Private-credit lender (MidCap Financial) term-loan amendment, SEC Exhibit 10.2 via r.jina.ai mirror. Restated s.4.1(i): within 30 days after each month end, a compliance certificate signed by a responsible officer with calculations showing cash and compliance with the financial covenants. The same amendment softens monthly reporting to apply only when the agent requests it, i.e. the monthly package is negotiable. Includes form compliance certificate.

Quotes:
- "within thirty (30) days after the last day of each month, together with the monthly financial statements described in (a) above" — s.4.1(i) as restated
- "a duly completed compliance certificate signed by a responsible officer setting forth calculations showing monthly cash and cash equivalents of borrowers" — s.4.1(i) as restated
- "compliance with the financial covenants set forth in this agreement" — s.4.1(i) as restated
- "as soon as available, but no later than thirty (30) days after the last day of each month," — original s.4.1(a) language, deleted by the amendment
- "within five (5) business days of agent's written request, if any, but no earlier than thirty (30) days after the last day of each month" — replacement s.4.1(a), monthly statements now on request
