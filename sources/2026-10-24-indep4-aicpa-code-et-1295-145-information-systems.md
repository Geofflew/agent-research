# AICPA Code, ET 1.295.145 Information Systems Services (FIS, tools, post-implementation)

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pub.aicpa.org/codeofconduct/ethicsresources/et-cod.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Designing or developing a client's financial information system impairs independence. FIS is defined to include a calculating tool unless the client evaluates inputs and understands results. Operating the client's software applications or monitoring/maintaining its systems on an ongoing basis is management responsibility. Text predates agents but maps directly: an agent the firm builds or runs for an attest client's close is an FIS question.

Quotes:
- "A financial information system (FIS) is a system that aggregates source data underlying the financial statements or generates information that is significant to either the financial statements or financial processes as a whole." — ET 1.295.145 .03a
- "An FIS includes a tool that calculates results unless" — ET 1.295.145 .03a
- "the attest client evaluates and accepts responsibility for the input and assumptions" — ET 1.295.145 .03a.ii
- "When a member designs or develops an attest client’s FIS," — ET 1.295.145 .05
- "Operates the attest client's network, such as managing the attest client’s systems or software applications" — ET 1.295.145 .21a
- "Has responsibility for maintaining the security of the attest client’s networks and systems" — ET 1.295.145 .21f
- "the attest client has not outsourced a function, process, or activity to the member, which would result in the member assuming a management responsibility" — ET 1.295.145 .04
