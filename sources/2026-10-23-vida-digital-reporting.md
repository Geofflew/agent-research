# European Commission: VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) package page

URL: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: ViDA adopted 11 Mar 2025, in force 14 Apr 2025. Member States may mandate e-invoicing under conditions from entry into force. Digital reporting for cross-border B2B from 1 Jul 2030. By 1 Jan 2035 domestic real-time reporting regimes must align with EU model/standards.

Quotes:
- "digital reporting requirements will affect cross-border b2b transactions from 1 july 2030" — European Commission, DG TAXUD ViDA page
- "by 1 january 2035, member states with a domestic digital real-time transaction reporting obligation must align their systems with the eu model and standards" — European Commission, DG TAXUD ViDA page
- "upon entry into force (14 april 2025), member states are able to introduce mandatory e-invoicing under specific conditions" — European Commission, DG TAXUD ViDA page
