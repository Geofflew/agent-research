# Learning the "Craft" of Auditing: A Dynamic View of Auditors' On-the-Job Learning (Westermann, Bedard & Earley 2015)

URL: https://faculty.providence.edu/en/publications/learning-the-craft-of-auditing-a-dynamic-view-of-auditors-on-the--2
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Contemporary Accounting Research 32(3), 864-896. Interviews with 30 Big 4 audit partners. Auditors mainly acquire technical knowledge on the job through engagement-team interaction; partners worry IT-driven distraction and reduced interpersonal contact, plus regulatory/economic pressure, erode that learning. Abstract only (Providence College faculty page); no numbers beyond n=30.
