# Roberts & Sufi (2009), Control rights and capital structure (abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v64y2009i4p1657-1695.html
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Journal of Finance abstract. After a covenant violation, creditors use acceleration and termination rights to raise rates and cut credit; borrowers rarely switch lenders. Shows what a lender does once a metric crosses the line.

Quotes:
- "net debt issuing activity experiences a sharp and persistent decline following debt covenant violations" — Roberts & Sufi, page text
- "creditors use their acceleration and termination rights to increase interest rates and reduce the availability of credit" — Roberts & Sufi, page text
- "borrowers rarely switch lenders following a violation" — Roberts & Sufi, page text
