# PIB: 29,273 bogus firms, Rs 44,015 crore suspected ITC evasion (Jan 2024)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1993970
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: Ministry of Finance PIB release. Fake-invoice drive since May 2023 found 29,273 bogus firms, Rs 44,015 cr ITC. Fake invoices pass through filed returns and feed buyers GSTR-2B. Govt itself relies on GSTR-1/3B/2B gap intimations plus analytics, not on returns data alone.

Quotes:
- "a total of 29,273 bogus firms involved in suspected Input Tax Credit (ITC) evasion of Rs. 44,015 crore have been detected" — PIB Ministry of Finance, 7 Jan 2024
- "carrying out a focused drive on the issue of non-existent / bogus registrations and issuance of fake invoices without any underlying supply of goods and services" — PIB Ministry of Finance, 7 Jan 2024
- "system generated intimation for reconciliation of the gap in tax liability in GSTR-1 & GSTR- 3B returns and of the gap between ITC available as per GSTR-2B & ITC availed in GSTR-3B returns" — PIB, measures against evasion
