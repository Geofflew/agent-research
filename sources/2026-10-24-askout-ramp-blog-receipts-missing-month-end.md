# Ramp: what happens if receipts are missing at month-end

URL: https://ramp.com/blog/receipt-compliance/receipts-missing-at-month-end
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Vendor marketing/answers page describing the shipped escalation ladder for missing receipts: automated employee reminders, then manager escalation, then finance visibility; items missing at close stay flagged and late receipts sync to the ERP afterward. No AI agent is named; this is rule-based.

Quotes:
- "Employees get automated email and in-app reminders with a link to upload receipts. These reminders continue until the receipt is submitted." — Ramp, How it works on Ramp
- "If receipts are still missing after the reminder period, the task can be escalated to the employee’s manager." — Ramp
- "Admins can bulk-reassign outstanding receipt tasks to managers so that follow-up does not fall entirely on finance teams." — Ramp, related questions
- "If a receipt is added after books are closed, Ramp updates the transaction and syncs the new documentation to your ERP." — Ramp
- "Each line shows the transaction details, the employee responsible, when reminders were sent, and whether it has been escalated to a manager." — Ramp
