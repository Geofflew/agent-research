# Badertscher, Kim, Kinney, Owens (JAE 2023): assurance level, CPA fees, cost of debt in US private firms

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jaecon/v75y2023i2s016541012200074x.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Peer-reviewed study of US private firms choosing compilation, review or audit. Fees more than double at each step, so audit is more than 4x compilation. Reporting-quality proxies do not differ on average between review and audit, but cost of debt is lower for audits than reviews. Only the abstract is open; fee levels are behind the paywall. Notre Dame press coverage (mendoza.nd.edu/?p=31274, not logged) adds that banks place greater value on audits.

Quotes:
- "We find that CPA fees more than double for each increment in assurance." — abstract, JAE 75(2) 2023
- "but are statistically indistinguishable on average between reviews and audits" — abstract, on financial reporting quality proxies
- "Cost of debt is significantly lower for reviews than for compilations and significantly lower for audits than for reviews." — abstract
- "Many U.S. private firms choose either a financial statement compilation or review rather than the higher assurance provided by an audit" — abstract
