# Ohio Revised Code 4701.06: CPA certificate requirements (HB 238)

URL: https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-4701.06
Type: standard
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Ohio statute, effective Jan 1, 2026 (HB 238). Baseline is one year of board-satisfactory experience in a public accounting firm, government, business or academia; baccalaureate-only candidates need two years. Requirements fixed by the date a candidate first sits the exam. Statute does not say supervised; detail left to board.

Quotes:
- "acquisition of one year of experience satisfactory to the board in any of the following: (i) A public accounting firm; (ii) Government; (iii) Business; (iv) Academia." — ORC 4701.06(A)(2)(b)
- "The experience requirement for a candidate who satisfies the educational requirements under division (A)(2)(a) of this section by obtaining a baccalaureate degree is two years of the experience described in division (A)(2)(b) of this section." — ORC 4701.06(B)
- "shall be those in effect on the date on which the candidate first sits for the examination" — ORC 4701.06(E)
- "Effective: January 1, 2026" — ORC 4701.06 page header
