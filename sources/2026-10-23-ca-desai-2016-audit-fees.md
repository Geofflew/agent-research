# Desai, Jacob, Tripathy 2016, Impact of Continuous Auditing on Earnings Quality and Audit Fees (working paper abstract)

URL: https://www.iima.ac.in/hindi/index.php/node/16113
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: IIMA working paper, 4 Apr 2016. Archival test: firms with CA-based internal audit vs periodic audit (PA). No significant difference in discretionary accruals. CA firms pay significantly lower external audit fees than PA firms. Evidence external audit adjusts to client CA; fee effect, not replacement. Working paper abstract only; no sample size or magnitude.

Quotes:
- "external auditors tend to rely more and spend relatively lower audit effort on the ca based ia functions compared to traditional periodic audit" — Desai, Jacob, Tripathy, abstract
- "the results indicate that there is no significant difference in the level of discretionary accruals between companies employing ca versus pa." — Desai, Jacob, Tripathy, abstract
- "we find that, the companies employing ca do pay significantly lower audit fees than companies employing pa." — Desai, Jacob, Tripathy, abstract
