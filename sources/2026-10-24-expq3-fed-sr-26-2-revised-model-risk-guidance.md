# SR 26-2 Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management (Fed/OCC/FDIC, April 2026)

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602a1.pdf
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: April 2026 interagency guidance revising SR 11-7: effective challenge needs expertise, independence, standing; vendor models must be validated, benchmarking to other models is an accepted assessment; generative and agentic AI are expressly out of scope.

Quotes:
- "effective challenge is performed by individuals with the appropriate expertise to conduct a critical and objective challenge, sufficient independence to maintain objectivity" — Federal Reserve SR 26-2 attachment, April 17 2026
- "benchmarking to other models" — Federal Reserve SR 26-2 attachment, April 17 2026
- "an important element of model risk management is the validation of vendor products, either by internal or outside parties" — Federal Reserve SR 26-2 attachment, April 17 2026
- "generative ai and agentic ai models are novel and rapidly evolving. as such, they are not within the scope of this guidance" — Federal Reserve SR 26-2 attachment, April 17 2026
- "banking organizations may not receive from the vendor the underlying code, data, or methodology" — Federal Reserve SR 26-2 attachment, April 17 2026
