# PCAOB Issues Staff Audit Practice Alert in Light of Deficiencies Observed in Audits of Internal Control (Oct 24, 2013; Deloitte DART copy)

URL: https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/ov-resource/9114e890-3f32-11e6-95db-cb8a64de759c.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: PCAOB press release reproduced by Deloitte. SAPA 11 covers testing mgmt review controls. 2010 inspections: 15% of 309 integrated audits lacked sufficient evidence for ICFR opinion. Deficiencies persisted in later years.

Quotes:
- "in 15 percent of the 309 integrated audit engagements inspected, firms failed to obtain sufficient audit evidence to support their opinions on the effectiveness of internal control due to one or more deficiencies" — PCAOB release
- "Inspections in subsequent years have continued to identify similarly high levels of deficiencies in audits of internal control at other registered firms." — PCAOB release
- "Testing management review controls" — SAPA 11 topic list
