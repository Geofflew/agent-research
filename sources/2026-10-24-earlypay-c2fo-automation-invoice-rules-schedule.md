# C2FO Early Pay help: Automation (invoice rules and offer schedules)

URL: http://help.c2fo.com/en/articles/15350917-automation
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: C2FO help page (June 5, 2026). New Automation feature lets suppliers set rules (days paid early, due date, amount, invoice ID) and calendar schedules so participation happens "on your behalf". It automates which invoices enter the offer and when, not the rate; the rate stays supplier-set. Feature may not be enabled for every account. Schedules follow US-Central market processing hours.

Quotes:
- "automations can participate on your behalf based on your rules" — C2FO help, Automation
- "Offer schedules follow Market Processing Hours in US-Central Time." — C2FO help, Tips
- "If you do not see Automation, it may not be enabled for your account." — C2FO help, Where to find it
