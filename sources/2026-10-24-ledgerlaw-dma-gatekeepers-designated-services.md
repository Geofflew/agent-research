# European Commission: DMA designated gatekeepers and core platform services

URL: https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/gatekeepers_en
Type: primary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Commission list of the 23 designated core platform services across Alphabet, Amazon, Apple, Booking, ByteDance, Meta and Microsoft. Microsoft is designated only for LinkedIn and Windows PC OS; no ERP, accounting, finance or business-software service (and no cloud computing service, e.g. Azure or Dynamics) appears. So DMA Art. 6(7)/6(10) access duties reach no ledger vendor. Booking is the only B2B-ish intermediary; SAP, Oracle, Intuit, Sage, Workday, NetSuite are absent.
