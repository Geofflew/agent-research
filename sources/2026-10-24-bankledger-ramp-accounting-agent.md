# Ramp launches Accounting Agent

URL: https://ramp.com/blog/accounting-agent-launch
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0002F

Summary: 12 Feb 2026. Card/spend platform codes every line item at swipe, auto-posts low-risk items to ERP, accrues unready spend and reverses next month, reconciles to ERP in real time. Ramp Plus only. Numbers vendor-reported from internal data (98% accuracy on ready-to-sync, 3.5x more auto-coded, 70% fewer corrections). Ledger stays in external ERP; Ramp sits in front. Against thesis: spend platform syncs to ERP, does not replace it; human sets permissions.
