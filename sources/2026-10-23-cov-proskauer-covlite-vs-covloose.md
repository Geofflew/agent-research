# Proskauer: private credit leverage covenants, cov-lite vs cov-loose, auto resets

URL: https://www.proskauer.com/alert/private-credit-deep-dives-leverage-covenants-and-auto-resets
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: Law firm private credit group. Cov-lite = no maintenance covenant for term lenders. Private credit once required leverage maintenance covenant; blurred now. True mid-market mostly still insists. RCF lenders get springing covenant, tested only if drawn.

Quotes:
- "that distinction has become less clear-cut and, whilst some private credit institutions continue to insist upon the inclusion of a maintenance covenant (including the significant majority of such institutions within the true mid-market), others have become increasingly comfortable in telling sponsors that they can live without one" — Proskauer private credit group, London, intro
- "leverage would only be tested on an incurrence basis (whereby compliance with a maximum leverage ratio may be required in order to take certain positive actions; for example, incurring incremental debt or paying distributions to equity-holders)" — Proskauer, cov-lite vs cov-loose section
- "there will be no maintenance covenant at all for the benefit of the term loan lenders" — Proskauer, cov-lite vs cov-loose section
- "leverage covenant (meaning that it only applies to the extent the revolving facility is actually drawn to a certain extent, commonly 40%)" — Proskauer, cov-lite vs cov-loose section
