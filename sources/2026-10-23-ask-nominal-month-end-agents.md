# Nominal: How AI agents automate month-end close

URL: https://www.nominal.so/blog/how-ai-agents-automate-month-end-close
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Vendor blog. A trigger agent matches an incoming invoice to an accrual and drafts the reversal entry, then assigns it as a task to a preparer and reviewer who approve, override or dismiss. Matching tolerance is set by the customer. Describes the preparer's work as reviewing agent-made tasks; marketing, no customer data.

Quotes:
- "That recommendation is then assigned to the appropriate preparer and reviewer as a task" — Nominal blog, accrual reversals
- "your team simply receives a task with the recommended output and decides whether to approve, override, or dismiss it" — Nominal blog
- "The matching and drafting happened automatically, throughout the month" — Nominal blog
- "You can instruct the system to accept matches within a 10% variance of the bill amount" — Nominal blog, matching thresholds
