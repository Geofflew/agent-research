# Researchers find quarterly reporting leads to corporate myopia (Accounting Today on Kraft, Vashishtha, Venkatachalam)

URL: https://accountingtoday.com/news/researchers-find-quarterly-financial-reporting-leads-to-corporate-myopia
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Press summary of Accounting Review paper. Firms forced to report more often cut capex 1.5-1.9% of assets vs ~9% avg capex. Counter to Fu et al: frequency has real-investment cost. Supports EU/UK dropping quarterly mandate.

Quotes:
- "they reduced their annual capital investments by around 1.5 to 1.9 percent of their total assets" — article on mandated increase in reporting frequency
- "the average annual capital investments of those companies totaled approximately 9 percent of assets, so those reductions were hefty cuts" — article
- "supports the recent decision by the eu and the uk to abandon requiring quarterly reporting for listed companies" — authors, quoted in article
