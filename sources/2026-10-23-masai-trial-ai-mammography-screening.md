# MASAI trial: AI-supported mammography screening, safety analysis (Lancet Oncology 2023)

URL: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=EXT_ID:37541274&resultType=core&format=xml
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Europe PMC record of Lang et al, Lancet Oncol 2023 abstract (thelancet.com blocked). Sweden, 4 sites, 80,033 women randomised 1:1 AI-supported vs standard double reading. Cancer detection 6.1 vs 5.1 per 1000; reading workload -44.3%. Interim safety analysis; interval-cancer endpoint came later (Lancet 2026, Europe PMC EXT_ID 41620232: interval rate 1.55 vs 1.76 per 1000, sens 80.5% vs 73.8%; LDH 2025 EXT_ID 39904652: detection +29%, workload -44.2%, 105,934 women).

Quotes:
- "80 033 women were randomly assigned to ai-supported screening (n=40 003) or double reading without ai (n=40 030)." — Lang et al, Findings
- "cancer detection rates were 6·1 (95% ci 5·4-6·9) per 1000 screened participants in the intervention group, above the lowest acceptable limit for safety, and 5·1 (4·4-5·8) per 1000 in the control group" — Lang et al, Findings
- "the screen-reading workload was reduced by 44·3% using ai." — Lang et al, Findings
- "ai-supported mammography screening resulted in a similar cancer detection rate compared with standard double reading, with a substantially lower screen-reading workload, indicating that the use of ai in mammography screening is safe." — Lang et al, Interpretation
