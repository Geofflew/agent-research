# PIB: DGGI Meerut busts 246 fake entities, ITC Rs 557 crore (Jul 2023)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1943338&reg=3&lang=2
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: DGGI press release. 246 shell firms issued invoices to 1,500+ buyer firms in 27 states. Fake invoices sat in authority data as filed returns. Bank accounts opened with bank official involvement. Authority record carried fraudulent counterparties; found via data mining plus human intelligence.

Quotes:
- "These 2 syndicates have issued invoices having taxable turnover of Rs. 3,142 crore involving ITC of Rs. 557 crore through 246 fake firms to more than 1,500 beneficiary firms" — PIB DGGI Meerut release, 27 Jul 2023
- "based on extensive data mining coupled with human intelligence" — PIB DGGI Meerut release, how syndicates were found
- "Initial probe has also indicated the involvement of bank officials in opening of the bank accounts in the name of fake firms" — PIB DGGI Meerut release
