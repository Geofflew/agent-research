# Insurers launch cover for AI chatbot errors (FT via Armilla)

URL: https://www.armilla.ai/resources/insurers-launch-cover-for-losses-caused-by-ai-chatbot-errors
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: FT repost on Armilla site, May 2025. Lloyd's/Chaucer-backed cover pays on degradation vs baseline, not single mistake. Armilla assesses model first. Standard tech E&O has tiny AI sublimits. Limits now 25m. Chaucer selective, refuses flaky models.

Quotes:
- "a mistake by an ai tool would not on its own be enough to trigger a payout under armilla's policy" — Financial Times article republished by Armilla (Armilla CEO, Lockton brokers, Chaucer)
- "the cover would kick in if the insurer judged that the ai had performed below initial expectations" — Financial Times article republished by Armilla (Armilla CEO, Lockton brokers, Chaucer)
- "armilla's insurance could pay out if a chatbot gave clients or employees correct information only 85 per cent of the time, after initially doing so in 95 per cent of cases" — Financial Times article republished by Armilla (Armilla CEO, Lockton brokers, Chaucer)
- "we assess the ai model, get comfortable with its probability of degradation, and then compensate if the models degrade" — Financial Times article republished by Armilla (Armilla CEO, Lockton brokers, Chaucer)
- "a general policy that covers up to $5mn in losses might stipulate a $25,000 sublimit for ai-related liabilities" — Financial Times article republished by Armilla (Armilla CEO, Lockton brokers, Chaucer)
- "we will be selective, like any other insurance company" — Financial Times article republished by Armilla (Armilla CEO, Lockton brokers, Chaucer)
- "limits have since increased to $25m (january 2026)" — Financial Times article republished by Armilla (Armilla CEO, Lockton brokers, Chaucer)
