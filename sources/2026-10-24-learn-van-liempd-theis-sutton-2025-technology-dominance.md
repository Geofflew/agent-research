# The Risk of Technology Dominance in Using Digital Decision Aids in Assurance Engagements (van Liempd, Theis & Sutton 2025)

URL: https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/publications/the-risk-of-technology-dominance-in-using-digital-decision-aids-i-4/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007A

Summary: Accounting Horizons 39(3), 175-194. Survey of 725 Danish auditors. Reliance on digital decision aids is higher among inexperienced auditors; senior auditors voice deskilling concerns and are less positive about junior auditors' skill development under DDA use. Evidence is perceptual (survey), not measured skill loss. Abstract only.

Quotes:
- "surveying 725 Danish auditors, we sought to determine the level of reliance practicing auditors have on DDAs" — source page text
