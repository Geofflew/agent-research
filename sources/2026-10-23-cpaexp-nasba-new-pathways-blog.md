# NASBA: New CPA Licensure Pathways and CPA Mobility (Dec 2025)

URL: https://nasba.org/blog/2025/12/23/new-cpa-licensure-pathways-and-cpa-mobility/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: NASBA staff blog summarizing the 2025 UAA update. Adds a third model pathway (bachelor's, 120 hours, two years of professional experience). Mobility moves from state-based to individual-based. Adoption is state by state; no experience content definition given on this page.

Quotes:
- "In 2025, NASBA and the AICPA significantly updated the Uniform Accountancy Act (UAA) to add a third pathway to licensure" — NASBA blog, Dec. 23, 2025
- "New 120-Hour Pathway: Bachelor's degree in accounting (120 semester hours) + two (2) years of professional experience + passing the CPA Exam." — NASBA blog
- "individual jurisdictions must formally enact legislation to adopt the new models and pursue formal rule changes. Implementation dates vary." — NASBA blog
- "A CPA's mobility is now determined by their individual qualifications (education, exam and experience) rather than whether their home state has" — NASBA blog
