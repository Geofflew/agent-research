# ISA (NZ) 500 Audit Evidence (external information source, A35-A44)

URL: https://standards.xrb.govt.nz/standards-navigator/isa-nz-500
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: NZ XRB adoption of ISA 500 (Revised). Governmental orgs are external information sources but auditor must still consider relevance and reliability. Independent source can be unreliable. Credible authority may need only light corroboration. No sufficient basis means scope limitation.

Quotes:
- "External information sources may include pricing services, governmental organisations, central banks or recognised stock exchanges." — ISA (NZ) 500 para A1
- "Even when information to be used as audit evidence is obtained from sources external to the entity, circumstances may exist that could affect its reliability." — ISA (NZ) 500 para A35
- "Performing a comparison of information obtained from the external information source with information obtained from an alternative independent information source." — ISA (NZ) 500 para A42
- "When the auditor does not have a sufficient basis with which to consider the relevance and reliability of information from an external information source" — ISA (NZ) 500 para A44, followed by scope limitation
- "when the information is from a credible authoritative source" — ISA (NZ) 500 A-material, single-provider case
