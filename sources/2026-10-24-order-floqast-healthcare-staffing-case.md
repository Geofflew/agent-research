# FloQast case study: healthcare staffing leader saves 976 hours and halves close cycle

URL: https://www.floqast.com/stories/healthcare-staffing-leader-saves-976-hours-and-halves-close-cycle-with-floqast-ai
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Vendor case study, undated. First agents built were accrual data-prep (dimension audits, time-record splits) and commission audits, not reconciliation matching or flux; chosen via a per-activity time-mapping exercise. 20 to 10 business days; accrual prep 16 to 4 hrs/month; commission audits 32 to 16 hrs/week. Four agents built in a four-week sprint. Next target: payroll/benefits reclassifications.
