# Digital Markets Act Article 2: core platform service definition (closed list)

URL: https://www.eu-digital-markets-act.com/Digital_Markets_Act_Article_2.html
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Art. 2(2) of Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 lists the ten categories of core platform service that can be designated. ERP, accounting and general business software (SaaS) are not categories; the nearest is "cloud computing services", and no cloud service is on the current designation list. A ledger vendor cannot be a gatekeeper for its ledger unless the legislator adds a category (the Commission review is the route).

Quotes:
- "‘core platform service’ means any of the following: (a) online intermediation services; (b) online search engines; (c) online social networking services; (d) video-sharing platform services;" — DMA Art. 2(2)
- "(f) operating systems; (g) web browsers; (h) virtual assistants; (i) cloud computing services; (j) online advertising services" — DMA Art. 2(2)
- "‘business user’ means any natural or legal person acting in a commercial or professional capacity using core platform services" — DMA Art. 2(21)
