# CMS CERT: Medicare FFS improper payment rate FY2025 6.55%

URL: https://www.cms.gov/cert
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: CMS external audit of claims. Stratified random sample of FFS claims, reviewed vs coverage, coding, payment rules. FY2025 rate 6.55%, $28.83B. Part B 8.44%. Sampling = after-the-fact, not per-claim; provider bears error.

Quotes:
- "reviews a statistically valid stratified random sample of Medicare FFS claims to determine if they were paid properly under Medicare coverage, coding, and payment rules" — CMS CERT page, method
- "estimated improper payment rate is 6.55 percent, representing $28.83 billion in improper payments" — CMS CERT page
- "The reporting period for this improper payment rate is July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2024." — CMS CERT page, lag
- "Part B Providers 8.44% $9.62 B" — CMS CERT page, table
