# Krishnan & Yang 2009, Recent trends in audit report and earnings announcement lags (Accounting Horizons, abstract)

URL: https://scholarworks.sogang.ac.kr/item/95d6480c-11d3-47b3-895f-a6ddbeb50209
Type: primary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Longitudinal 2001-2006 sample around SEC accelerated filing. Both audit and earnings announcement lags rose even under the new deadlines. Firms increasingly announced earnings before the audit report date, especially 2004-2006 (SOX 404). Accrual quality was not worse for long-lag firms except a mild 2004 effect: a null/mixed counterpoint on accrual quality.

Quotes:
- "the likelihood that companies announced earnings prior to the audit report date increased considerably over the period 20012006, but particularly during 2004-2006 when Section 404 of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act of 2002 (SOX) was in effect" — Krishnan & Yang abstract
- "companies are less likely to wait for completion of their audits to announce earnings" — abstract
- "long audit report lags (or 10-K filings lags) were not associated with lower quality of earnings or accruals (except for a mild effect in 2004)" — abstract
