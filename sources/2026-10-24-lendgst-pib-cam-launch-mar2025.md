# PIB: Finance Minister launches New Credit Assessment Model for MSMEs (6 Mar 2025)

URL: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2108812
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Ministry of Finance release on the launch of the PSB credit assessment model scoring MSMEs' digital footprints (GST via API, AA bank statements, ITR, bureaus), replacing reliance on external assessment. Says it will also cover MSMEs without a formal accounting system and improves on assessment based only on asset or turnover criteria. Lists CGTMSE-covered loans as needing no physical collateral.

Quotes:
- "Public sector banks (PSBs) will build their in-house capability to assess MSMEs for credit, instead of relying on external assessment." — PIB, Ministry of Finance, 6 Mar 2025
- "API fetch of GST data through service providers, Bank Statement Analysis using account aggregator, ITR upload and verification" — PIB, list of digital footprints the model may use
- "expected to be a significant improvement over the traditional assessment of credit eligibility based only on asset or turnover criteria" — PIB, 6 Mar 2025
- "That will also cover MSMEs without a formal accounting system." — PIB, 6 Mar 2025
- "Credit decision based on objective data/ Transactional behaviour and credit history" — PIB, listed benefit to MSMEs
