# Correlated Errors in Large Language Models (Kim, Garg, Peng, Garg, 2025)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07962
Type: primary
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: Evaluates 350+ LLMs on two leaderboards and a resume-screening task. Errors are substantially correlated: on one leaderboard dataset, models agree 60% of the time when both err. Shared architecture and provider drive correlation, but larger, more accurate models are highly correlated even across architectures and providers. Shows effects on LLM-as-judge evaluation and hiring (algorithmic monoculture).

Quotes:
- "on one leaderboard dataset, models agree 60% of the time when both models err" — Kim et al., arXiv abstract
