# Journal of Accountancy: PEEC to continue discussions on alternative practice structures (Aug 10, 2026)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/news/2026/aug/peec-to-continue-discussions-on-alternative-practice-structures/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0001F

Summary: AICPA's own magazine reports that after public comments on the Dec 2025 exposure draft, PEEC is revising its APS approach: further changes to network-firm definition and a new interpretation prohibiting attest-client investment in firms. A second exposure draft is under discussion for an October 2026 interim meeting, so no final rule exists as of this date.

Quotes:
- "adding a new interpretation prohibiting attest client investment in firms" — Neil Amato, Journal of Accountancy, Aug 10, 2026
- "PEEC agreed to continue discussions at an interim October meeting on issuing a second exposure draft" — Neil Amato, Journal of Accountancy
- "Public comments on the original exposure draft, published in December 2025, spurred PEEC to consider additional changes." — Neil Amato, Journal of Accountancy
