# FDA De Novo authorization of IDx-DR (Apr 11 2018), first autonomous AI diagnostic screening decision

URL: https://clpmag.com/lab-essentials/information-technology/fda-clears-ai-based-device-detect-diabetes-related-eye-problems/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Trade-press reprint of FDA press release (fda.gov page 404/403 to scorer fetcher; ucm604357 tried). IDx-DR first marketed device giving screening decision w/o clinician reading image. Study 900 pts, 10 primary care sites. Sens 87.4%, spec 89.5%. De Novo pathway. Page also carries vendor (IDx) founder statement.

Quotes:
- "idx-dr is the first device authorized for marketing that provides a screening decision without the need for a clinician to also interpret the image or results" — FDA press release, reprinted
- "fda evaluated data from a clinical study of retinal images obtained from 900 patients with diabetes at 10 primary care sites." — FDA press release, reprinted
- "idx-dr was able to correctly identify the presence of more than mild diabetic retinopathy 87.4% of the time" — FDA press release, reprinted (sensitivity)
- "was able to correctly identify those patients who did not have more than mild diabetic retinopathy 89.5% of the time" — FDA press release, reprinted (specificity)
