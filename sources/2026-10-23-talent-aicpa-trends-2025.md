# AICPA 2025 Trends report: accounting graduates and CPA exam candidates

URL: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/news/article/accounting-firms-report-strong-hiring-outlook-aicpa-report-finds
Type: primary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: AICPA press release on 2025 Trends. Accounting bach+master grads 55,152 in 2023-24, -6.6%. New CPA candidates 42,626 (2023) to 28,082 (2024) after exam change. Firm hiring survey low response.

Quotes:
- "Graduates who earned either a bachelor's or master's degree in accounting fell to 55,152 in the 2023-24 academic year, a drop of 6.6% compared to the previous year." — AICPA, Trends report press release
- "Those who earned master's degrees in either accounting or taxation declined approximately 15% to 14,335 degrees." — AICPA, Trends report press release
- "42,626 new candidates entered the exam pipeline, the highest tally since 2016." — AICPA, Trends report press release
- "That compares to 28,082 new candidates entering the pipeline in 2024." — AICPA, Trends report press release
- "showed growth with 16,448 new candidates entering the exam pipeline through the first six months of the year" — AICPA, Trends report press release
- "Only 18% anticipated lower hiring rates." — AICPA, firm hiring survey in Trends report
