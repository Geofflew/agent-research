# The SEC Proposes Optional Semiannual Reporting: Form 10-S (Cooley GovernanceBeat)

URL: https://governancebeat.cooley.com/the-sec-proposes-optional-semiannual-reporting-form-10-s/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Cooley list of key proposal terms. 10-S same disclosure as 10-Q for six months, GAAP, auditor reviewed not audited. Deadlines 40/45 days like 10-Q.

Quotes:
- "with financial statements prepared under GAAP and reviewed (not audited) by an auditor" — Cooley, item on Form 10-S content
- "Semiannual reports on Form 10-S would be due 40 or 45 days after the end of a semiannual period, consistent with current Form 10-Q deadlines based on filer status" — Cooley, item 4 Filing Deadlines
