# Maintain vendor bank account information (Dynamics 365 Finance)

URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/accounts-payable/maintain-vendor-bank-info
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Vendors self-enter bank accounts via vendor collaboration. AP clerk reviews changes and runs pre-note validation, then marks reviewed. Staff can also enter bank info on vendor's behalf.

Quotes:
- "The accounts payable clerk can view the changes and run the account information through the pre-note process to validate it." — Microsoft Learn, vendor collaboration-generated bank changes
- "You can also enter bank account information on a vendor's behalf." — Microsoft Learn, maintain vendor bank info
- "Bank information that vendors enter on the page shows Vendor as the source." — Microsoft Learn, maintain vendor bank info
