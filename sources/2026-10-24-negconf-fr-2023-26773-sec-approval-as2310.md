# Federal Register: SEC Order Approving PCAOB AS 2310, The Auditor's Use of Confirmation (88 FR 84836, 6 Dec 2023)

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-12-06/pdf/2023-26773.pdf
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: SEC Release 34-99060 approving new AS 2310 and rescinding the old one. States that negative confirmations may provide sufficient evidence only when combined with other substantive procedures (AS 2310.12 and .13). Effective for fiscal years ending on or after June 15, 2025; applies to EGC audits. Three comment letters received (Deloitte, PwC, one individual).

Quotes:
- "the use of negative confirmation requests may provide sufficient appropriate audit evidence only when combined with other substantive procedures" — SEC order, section II.A
- "Reflecting the relatively insignificant amount of audit evidence obtained when using negative confirmation requests." — SEC order, bullet heading
- "The Amendments will be effective for all audits of financial statements for fiscal years ending on or after June 15, 2025." — SEC order, II.B
- "describing principles-based requirements for all methods of confirmation, including paper-based and electronic means of communications" — SEC order, section II
- "We received three (3) comment letters" — SEC order, section I
