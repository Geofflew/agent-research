# Deloitte DART, COSO Releases Publication on Internal Controls Related to Generative AI (Heads Up, 3 Apr 2026)

URL: https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/publications/deloitte/heads-up/2026/coso-internal-controls-generative-ai
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Big Four summary of COSO's Feb 2026 GenAI internal-control guidance (the COSO text itself not retrieved). Frames GenAI as probabilistic with variable outcomes needing continuous monitoring, and lists prompt/configuration governance and change control, model/version logging, and drift monitoring as control building blocks, with vendor changes as a root cause. Does not say a model version change is an ITGC program change in so many words, and not a PCAOB or IAASB text.

Quotes:
- "moving from deterministic, rule-based technologies to probabilistic models with inherently variable outcomes, and from static, point-in-time assurance to continuous monitoring of model performance and risk" — Deloitte DART summary of COSO guidance
- ""set-and-forget" does not work" — Deloitte DART summary of COSO guidance
- "root causes of deficiencies (e.g., prompt design, retrieval issues, vendor changes)" — Deloitte DART summary of COSO guidance
- "Prompt/configuration governance and change control" — Deloitte DART summary of COSO guidance
- "Logging/traceability (model/version, prompts, key inputs/outputs, approvals)" — Deloitte DART summary of COSO guidance
- "Monitoring controls for drift, anomalies, and unauthorized use" — Deloitte DART summary of COSO guidance
