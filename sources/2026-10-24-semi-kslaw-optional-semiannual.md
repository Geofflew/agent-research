# SEC Proposes Amendments to Allow Optional Semiannual Reporting (King & Spalding)

URL: https://www.kslaw.com/news-and-insights/sec-proposes-amendments-to-allow-optional-semiannual-reporting
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Law-firm alert on 5 May 2026 SEC proposal. 10-S deadlines mirror 10-Q, auditor review not audit, 60-day comment after FR publication. Warns cost savings may be modest: debt covenants, voluntary quarterly disclosure, internal processes still needed.

Quotes:
- "Financial statements would be required to be reviewed (not audited) by an auditor, and prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP." — K&S summary of Form 10-S
- "consistent with current Form 10-Q deadlines based on filer status, which would not change" — K&S on 40/45-day deadline
- "The second semiannual period would be subsumed in the annual period presented in the annual report on Form 10-K." — K&S
- "The public comment period will remain open for 60 days following publication of the proposing release in the Federal Register." — K&S next steps
- "Some issuers are subject to debt covenants that require quarterly reporting regardless" — K&S practical considerations
- "may be more modest than anticipated as companies would still need internal processes to generate quarterly financial information" — K&S on cost savings
