# GLEIF: Introducing the verifiable LEI (vLEI)

URL: https://www.gleif.org/en/organizational-identity/introducing-the-verifiable-lei-vlei
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: GLEIF page on vLEI. vLEI = verifiable credential for legal entity + persons acting for it in specific roles. ISO 17442-3 (2024) standardised vLEI as digital credentials. Trust chain back to GLEIF root and LEI record. Use cases: signing regulatory filings, authorising payments. Identity/role credential, not about financial figures.

Quotes:
- "the vlei provides instant, automated identity verification of legal entities and those acting on the organization's behalf" — GLEIF, Introducing the vLEI
- "as a verifiable credential, it represents the next generation of identity management and the natural evolution of identity credentials" — GLEIF, Introducing the vLEI
- "verifiable credentials issued to specific persons in specific roles" — GLEIF, Introducing the vLEI
- "all vleis are openly traceable, through a cryptographically protected chain of credentials, back to their identity data source - their lei record in the global lei index" — GLEIF, Introducing the vLEI
