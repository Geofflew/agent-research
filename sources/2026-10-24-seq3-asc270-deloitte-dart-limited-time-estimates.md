# ASC 270 Interim Reporting (Deloitte DART codification presentation)

URL: https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/codification/presentation/asc270
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Codification text of ASC 270 reproduced on DART. States that limited time to develop complete information means many costs are estimated in interim periods, and that later refinement of those estimates can distort later interim results. Direct standard text for estimate-now, true-up-later.

Quotes:
- "In view of the limited time available to develop complete information, many costs and expenses are estimated in interim periods." — ASC 270 as reproduced on DART
- "Subsequent refinement or correction of these estimates may distort the results of operations of later interim periods." — same paragraph
- "it may not be practical to perform extensive reviews of individual inventory items, costs on individual long-term contracts and precise income tax calculations for each interim period" — same paragraph
