# BD Emerson: Transition services agreement guide

URL: https://www.bdemerson.com/article/transition-services-agreement-guide
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Advisory-firm guide (dated Aug 6, 2026). TSAs routinely include finance and accounting incl. month-end close support, so at carve-outs/exits the seller's shared finance function keeps closing the books for the divested business for roughly 6 to 24 months. Practitioner view, not survey data.

Quotes:
- "typically for 6 to 24 months at a price set in the agreement" — BD Emerson, TSA definition
- "month-end close support" — BD Emerson, list of finance and accounting TSA scope (full: "Finance and accounting covers accounts payable and receivable processing, general ledger access, treasury and banking connectivity, tax filings and compliance support, and month-end close support.")
- "A common structure is a 12-month base term with extension rights in 3 or 6 month increments out to 24 months" — BD Emerson
- "run 18 to 36 months and need the extension rights from day one" — BD Emerson, on deeply entangled separations with a single ERP instance
- "so a 12-month TSA is signed against an 18-month IT program" — BD Emerson, on TSAs overrunning
