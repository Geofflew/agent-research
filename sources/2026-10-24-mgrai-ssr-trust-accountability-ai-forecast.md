# Trust, Accountability, and Reliance on Algorithmic Advice (AEA RCT registry 17756)

URL: https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/17756
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Preregistered forecasting experiment 2026-27. Manipulates trust info and who is accountable for AI errors (participant vs analytics team); later splits liability, auditability, contestability. DV weight on advice. Design only, no results.

Quotes:
- "who is responsible for errors generated by the AI tool (the participant vs. an external Analytics Team responsible for monitoring and correcting the AI)" — experimental design
- "liability (who bears blame), auditability (whether performance is monitored), and contestability (whether users can challenge AI outputs)" — experimental design
- "to identify which specific governance feature drives reliance" — experimental design
