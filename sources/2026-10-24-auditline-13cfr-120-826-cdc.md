# 13 CFR 120.826: CDC annual audited or reviewed financial statements

URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/13/120.826
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: Federal regulation drawing a review/audit line in the SBA 504 program. It applies to the Certified Development Company, not the borrower. A CDC with a 504 portfolio of $30M or more needs an audit; smaller ones may submit CPA-reviewed statements, and SBA can demand an audit if the CDC is materially noncompliant. A regulator itself treats a review as sufficient below a size line.

Quotes:
- "Each CDC with a 504 loan portfolio balance of $30 million or more (as calculated by SBA) must have its financial statements audited annually by a certified public accountant" — 120.826(c)
- "For CDCs with a 504 portfolio balance of less than $30 million (as calculated by SBA), the CDC's annual financial statements submitted to SBA must be reviewed by an independent CPA in accordance with GAAP" — 120.826(c)
