# Setting Up Roles and Permissions (NetSuite Payment Automation)

URL: https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_1006011432.html
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: NetSuite Payment Automation SuiteApp splits payment clerk from Vendor Master Clerk (edits vendor bank record) and Vendor Master Approver. Doc says assign clerk to one vetted employee.

Quotes:
- "This role is assigned to an employee who is allowed to create and edit vendor files." — Oracle NetSuite Help, Vendor Master Clerk
- "Preferably this role must be assigned only to one employee to ensure that all vendors are valid and vetted." — Oracle NetSuite Help, Vendor Master Clerk
- "Preferably this user should be different from Vendor Master Clerk." — Oracle NetSuite Help, Vendor Master Approver
- "The AP Payment Clerk role lets you pay supplier invoices." — Oracle NetSuite Help, AP Payment Clerk
