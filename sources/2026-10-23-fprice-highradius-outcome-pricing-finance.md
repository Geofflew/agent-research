# HighRadius: 5 real-world outcome-based pricing examples in finance

Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005A

Summary: HighRadius (AP/AR/close vendor) blog, 16 Mar 2026. Says outcome pricing in finance is emerging: cost per invoice, touchless rate, auto-cash match. Examples are hypothetical 'may' structures, no named customer contract or published rate. Vendor marketing; evidence of intent, not of live pricing.

Quotes:
- "tie pricing to metrics such as invoice processing efficiency, automation rates, cash application accuracy, or improvements in days sales outstanding (dso)" — HighRadius blog
- "outcome-based pricing models are already beginning to reshape how finance automation solutions are evaluated" — HighRadius blog
- "an outcome-based pricing model can tie vendor compensation to the number of payments automatically matched and posted by the system" — HighRadius blog, cash application example
- "vendors may receive incentives as automation milestones are achieved or when a defined percentage of invoices are processed without manual intervention" — HighRadius blog, touchless AP example
- "in practice, the most effective models rely on mutually agreed success criteria" — HighRadius blog
