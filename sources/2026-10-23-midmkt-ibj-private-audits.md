# Private-company audits make a comeback, but not for all (IBJ)

URL: https://www.ibj.com/articles/14735-accounting-private-company-audits-make-a-comeback-but-not-for-all
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Indiana Business Journal. Banks historically took compilation/review, hesitant to require audit over cost. Review costs 40-60% of audit. Old article (post-2008 credit tightening). Admission: bank underwriters see review value as limited.

Quotes:
- "Many banks and lenders have historically relied on either a compilation or review of their borrowers" — IBJ
- "they are beginning to understand that these CPA services often have only limited value" — IBJ, on compilations and reviews
- "The cost of a review ranges from 40 percent to 60 percent of the cost of an audit" — IBJ
