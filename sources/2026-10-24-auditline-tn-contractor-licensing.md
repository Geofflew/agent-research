# Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors: financial statement requirements

URL: https://www.tn.gov/commerce/regboards/contractors/license/get/contractor.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: State licensing board sets a hard line by requested monetary limit: reviewed statements up to $3M, audited above. The limit is 10x the lesser of working capital or net worth, so an unlimited limit needs only $300,000 of each, meaning audits are required of fairly small firms. From 1 July 2026 a surety bond of 50% of the limit may replace either statement.

Quotes:
- "A “Reviewed” financial statement is required for limits of $3,000,000 or less." — Guidelines section
- "An “Audited” financial statement is required for limits exceeding $3,000,000." — Guidelines section
- "Monetary limits are calculated as 10 times the lesser of working capital or net worth, plus required experience." — monetary limit section
- "To obtain an unlimited monetary limit, an applicant must show $300,000 in both working capital and net worth, plus required experience." — monetary limit section
- "The surety bond must be in an amount equal to at least 50% of the monetary limit requested." — surety bond option (from 1 July 2026)
