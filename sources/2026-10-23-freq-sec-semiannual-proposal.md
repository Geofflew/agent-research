# SEC proposes optional semiannual reporting (Accounting Today)

URL: https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/sec-proposes-semiannual-reporting-option
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: SEC proposed rule 5 May 2026. Opt-in Form 10-S replaces 3 10-Qs. Trump push. Atkins lists cost of capital as factor firm weighs. Proposal only, no final rule found as of 2026-10-23.

Quotes:
- "companies and corporations should no longer be forced to 'report' on a quarterly basis" — Trump Truth Social post, quoted in article
- "this will save money, and allow managers to focus on properly running their companies" — Trump, quoted in article
- "instead of three quarterly reports and one annual report" — article on what semiannual filers file
- "the potential effects on its cost of capital" — Atkins, factors a company might weigh in choosing cadence
- "this flexibility might reduce some of the burdens of being a public company and potentially influence a company's decision to become or remain public" — Atkins statement
- "there will be a 60-day comment period on the proposal" — article
