# PO Accruals in Ramp (Ramp Help Center, alpha)

URL: https://support.ramp.com/po-accruals-in-ramp
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: Alpha PO accruals: Ramp builds a straight-line schedule per PO line from start/end dates and shows scheduled, billed and amount-to-accrue per line; the accountant includes or excludes. No receipt confirmation or requester question; the estimate is date-schedule-driven, not receipt-driven. Business owner is absent from the flow.
