# COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI (GenAI), February 2026 (PDF via text mirror)

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf
Type: standard
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: COSO's own 32-page GenAI paper (mirror header: published 23 Feb 2026; copyright 2026 COSO; authors from ASU, Duisburg-Essen, BYU, EY, Meta). Principle 3 text says each GenAI tool should have a "named owner" with authority and accountability, and the agent example (reconciliation auto-posting) requires multi-party approval for threshold changes plus post-change sampling. Reliance on AI in an ICFR control requires documented prompt, configuration and model version, and a control owner who re-performs and documents review in the non-reliance case. It does not mention SOX 302 sub-certification; the named owner is of the tool or capability, not of an agent as a checklist signer.
