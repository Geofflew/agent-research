# Ethics: independence and information systems services (JofA, Dec 2019)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/issues/2019/dec/ethics-independence-information-systems-services/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Deloitte independence director explains PEEC's revised ET 1.295.145 (issued June 2019). FIS vs non-FIS; member designing or developing an FIS cannot be safeguarded; COTS implementation limited to configuration where client decides; ongoing monitoring/support/maintenance of a system = management responsibility even with client oversight.

Quotes:
- "threats to a member's independence would not be at an acceptable level and could not be reduced to an acceptable level if the member designs or develops an FIS for an attest client" — Dan O'Daly, JofA, Dec 2019
- "an FIS aggregates source data underlying the financial statements or generates information that is significant to either the financial statements in particular or financial processes as a whole" — Dan O'Daly, JofA, Dec 2019
- "threats to a member's independence cannot be brought to acceptable levels for services that involve a client outsourcing to the member an ongoing function or process, such as systems monitoring, support, or maintenance, as such services represent the member assuming a management responsibility, even if an individual with suitable skill, knowledge, and/or experience from client management is overseeing the services" — Dan O'Daly, JofA, Dec 2019
- "An example is being engaged by a client to analyze the client's network and provide observations or recommendations for the client's consideration." — Dan O'Daly, JofA, Dec 2019 (permissible discrete service)
- "as long as the client makes all decisions with respect to selecting those options" — Dan O'Daly, JofA, Dec 2019 (COTS configuration)
- "takes effect Jan. 1, 2021, and allows for early implementation" — Dan O'Daly, JofA, Dec 2019
- "Designing an information system, or a single program, means determining how it will function, process data, and produce results" — Dan O'Daly, JofA, Dec 2019
