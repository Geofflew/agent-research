# Da Silva Moore v. Publicis Groupe (S.D.N.Y. Feb 24 2012), first court approval of computer-assisted review

URL: https://cloudnine.com/ediscovery-case-law-computer-assisted-review-approved-by-judge-peck-in-new-york-case/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: eDiscovery Daily blog quoting Judge Peck opinion at length (opinion host justia 403, courtlistener 403, casetext gone). First court approval of CAR. Not mandate; no vendor endorsed; counsel must design process w/ QC testing. Parties agreed to protocol: 7 iterative seeding rounds, more if unstable. Goal = higher recall + precision than alt method at proportionate cost.

Quotes:
- "this opinion appears to be the first in which a court has approved of the use of computer-assisted review." — Judge Peck, conclusion, as quoted by eDiscovery Daily
- "counsel must design an appropriate process, including use of available technology, with appropriate quality control testing, to review and produce relevant esi while adhering to rule 1 and rule 26(b)(2)(c) proportionality." — Judge Peck, conclusion
- "what the bar should take away from this opinion is that computer-assisted review is an available tool and should be seriously considered for use in large-data-volume cases where it may save the producing party (or both parties) significant amounts of legal fees in document review." — Judge Peck, conclusion
- "goal is for the review method to result in higher recall and higher precision than another review method" — Judge Peck, analysis section
