# Dynamics 365 Finance pricing page (agents require Copilot Credits)

URL: https://www.microsoft.com/dynamics-365/products/finance/pricing
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: Seat licence ($210/$300 per user/month) plus Copilot Credits to run prebuilt and custom agents; Premium includes 1,000 credits per user/month, extra via pay-as-you-go billed in arrears on consumption or prepaid commit units. Unit is consumption of credits, not accepted tasks; page states no refund or acceptance condition.

Quotes:
- "1,000 Copilot Credits are included with each Dynamics 365 Finance Premium license." — vendor source, www.microsoft.com
- "allowing you the flexibility to only pay for the Copilot Credits you consume at the end of your monthly billing period." — vendor source, www.microsoft.com
- "Copilot Credits (included in Finance Premium and available for purchase separately) are required to run prebuilt agents and custom agents built in Microsoft Copilot Studio." — vendor source, www.microsoft.com
- "save up to 20% with an upfront purchase of Copilot Credit commit units" — vendor source, www.microsoft.com
