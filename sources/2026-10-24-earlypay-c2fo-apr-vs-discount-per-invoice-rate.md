# C2FO Early Pay help: APR vs. Discount (rate per invoice, fixed/variable divisions set by buyer)

URL: http://help.c2fo.com/en/articles/15363461-apr-vs-discount
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0003F

Summary: C2FO help page (June 3, 2026). A supplier offer can be an annualized APR applied to each invoice by its days paid early, so cost is computed per invoice, or a flat discount. Some divisions use Fixed or Variable rates set by the market/customer, not the supplier. Pricing is per invoice arithmetically, but the offer is a standing rate chosen by a human supplier.

Quotes:
- "An annualized rate applied to each invoice based on its specific" — C2FO help, definition of APR
- "A 2% discount on a $10,000 invoice always costs $200." — C2FO help, flat Discount example
- "These divisions use rates set directly by the market, not your manual APR or Discount entry." — C2FO help, Fixed and Variable Rate divisions
