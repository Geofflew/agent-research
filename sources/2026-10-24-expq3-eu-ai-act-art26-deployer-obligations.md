# EU AI Act Article 26: Obligations of deployers of high-risk AI systems

URL: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/26/
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008C

Summary: Article 26 requires deployers of high-risk AI systems to use them per instructions, assign human oversight to competent staff, monitor operation, keep automatically generated logs, and report serious incidents to the provider. Named person: whoever is assigned oversight must have competence, training and authority. Logs kept at least six months.

Quotes:
- "deployers shall assign human oversight to natural persons who have the necessary competence, training and authority, as well as the necessary support" — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 26 paragraphs 1, 2, 5, 6 (as shown on artificialintelligenceact.eu)
- "deployers shall monitor the operation of the high-risk ai system" — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 26 paragraphs 1, 2, 5, 6 (as shown on artificialintelligenceact.eu)
- "at least six months, unless provided otherwise in applicable union or national law" — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 26 paragraphs 1, 2, 5, 6 (as shown on artificialintelligenceact.eu)
- "they shall, without undue delay, inform the provider" — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 26 paragraphs 1, 2, 5, 6 (as shown on artificialintelligenceact.eu)
- "shall suspend the use of that system" — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 26 paragraphs 1, 2, 5, 6 (as shown on artificialintelligenceact.eu)
- "deployers of high-risk ai systems shall take appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure they use such systems in accordance with the instructions for use" — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 26 paragraphs 1, 2, 5, 6 (as shown on artificialintelligenceact.eu)
