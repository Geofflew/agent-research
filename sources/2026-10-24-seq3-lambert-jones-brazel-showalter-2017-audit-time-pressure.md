# Lambert, Jones, Brazel & Showalter 2017, Audit time pressure and earnings quality: accelerated filings (AOS 58, IDEAS abstract)

URL: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/aosoci/v58y2017icp50-66.html
Type: primary
Read in session: 0009B

Summary: Tests SEC rules 33-8128 and 33-8644 (10-K accelerations). Earnings quality fell only where the rule actually cut days from year-end to audit report; stronger for smaller accelerated filers and the first cut. Survey of 32 retired audit partners: time pressure hurts receipt/evaluation of complex valuations and resolution of audit adjustments. Direct primary support for "faster deadline degrades estimate-heavy quality"; proxy is earnings quality, not true-ups per se.

Quotes:
- "we find that the 10-K accelerations reduced audit quality only when it actually reduced the number of days from year-end to audit report date" — Lambert et al. abstract
- "this effect was more acute for smaller, accelerated filers and during the initial deadline change (relative to the second)" — abstract
- "Survey results underscore the challenges time pressure imposes on receiving and evaluating complex valuations (such as for derivatives, pensions, and goodwill) and resolving audit adjustments." — abstract, survey of thirty-two retired audit partners
