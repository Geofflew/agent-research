# PCAOB AS 2201 Appendix B: Benchmarking of Automated Controls

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2007-06-12/html/E7-11311.htm
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: (0008A rehosted from r.jina.ai pcaobus mirror to govinfo FR notice of AS No. 5, 2007; B28-B33 text identical)  Primary std text B28-B33. Entirely automated app controls low human-failure risk, so baseline test can be reused. Needs ITGCs effective + still tested, auditor verifies control unchanged; evidence amount varies w/ program-change control strength. "May conclude", not must. Baseline reestablished after period, length depends on circumstances, no fixed years. Limits: depends on tables/params, risk factors, business-assumption change breaks control.
