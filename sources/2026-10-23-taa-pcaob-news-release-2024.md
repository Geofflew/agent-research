# PCAOB news release: technology-assisted analysis amendments to AS 1105 and AS 2301

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pcaobus.org/news-events/news-releases/news-release-detail/pcaob-updates-its-standards-to-clarify-auditor-responsibilities-when-using-technology-assisted-analysis
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: PCAOB adopts AS1105/AS2301 amendments Jun 2024. Aim: more TAA, less auditor reluctance. Auditor may run TAA on all items in population, must evaluate info reliability incl ITGC and app controls. Investigate flagged items for misstatement or control deficiency. Effective FY starting on/after 2025-12-15.

Quotes:
- "auditors who use technology-assisted analysis in the audit may issue an opinion without obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence" — PCAOB news release
- "some auditors’ reluctance, which the PCAOB has observed, to use technology-assisted analysis at all under existing standards" — PCAOB news release
- "perform, after identifying such risks, substantive procedures on all items within a population" — PCAOB news release
- "their testing should include, where applicable, controls over the company’s information technology general controls and automated application controls related to such information" — PCAOB news release
- "the auditor’s investigation of such items should include determining whether the identified items individually or in the aggregate indicate misstatements or control deficiencies" — PCAOB news release
- "if an auditor uses an audit procedure for more than one purpose, the auditor should achieve each objective of the procedure" — PCAOB news release
- "will take effect for audits of financial statements for fiscal years beginning on or after December 15, 2025" — PCAOB news release
