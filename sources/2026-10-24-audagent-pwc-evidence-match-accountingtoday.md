# PwC expects end-to-end AI audit automation within 2026 (Accounting Today, 28 Oct 2025)

URL: https://accountingtoday.com/news/pwc-expects-end-to-end-ai-audit-automation-within-2026
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Trade-press report of a PwC US briefing (pwc.com pages block fetches). PwC says a tool exists or is coming for every audit step; Evidence Match is an agent-led module matching client documents to evidence in cash, receivables and payables, and controls-testing automation is planned. The tools match and tie out client-supplied documents; the article describes no independent re-preparation of client journal entries or reconciliations.

Quotes:
- "complete end-to-end integration of AI in the audit cycle is about one year away" — Accounting Today summarizing PwC's estimate, 28 Oct 2025
- "Evidence Match is an agent-led audit module that automatically extracts matching and validating evidence against documents." — Accounting Today, describing Kyle Maryanski of PwC US
- "support testing in high-volume areas such as cash, accounts receivable and accounts payable" — Accounting Today, on Evidence Match
- "The client may hand you a napkin!" — Kyle Maryanski, PwC US assurance partner
- "automated controls testing that will operate similarly to their current software for evidence matching" — Accounting Today, on Shawn Panson's plans
