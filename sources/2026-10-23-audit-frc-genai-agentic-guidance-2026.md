# FRC: generative and agentic AI guidance for audit firms (Mar 2026)

URL: https://www.frc.org.uk/docs/9256/html/
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: UK FRC guidance 30 Mar 2026, first from any audit regulator on genAI/agentic AI. Non-binding. Four mitigation categories: system design, certification, staff education/governance, human-in-loop review. Confidence in output must fit use; if relied on as audit evidence, evaluate quantity and quality of evidence, not just reliability. AI output need not match persuasiveness of replaced procedure if not overstated. HTML is AI-converted from PDF, not human-verified (FRC warning).

Quotes:
- "where the output is to be relied upon as audit evidence, the auditor's evaluation of their confidence in the quality of an output consists of their evaluation of the quantity and quality of evidence obtained, not just its reliability." — FRC, Generative and agentic AI guidance
- "it is not necessary that the outputs of an AI-enabled procedure exactly match the persuasiveness of those of a procedure it replaces, so long as the methodology does not overstate their persuasiveness, and the overall approach meets auditing standards." — FRC, Generative and agentic AI guidance
- "the inherent limitations of LLMs mean there will always be some risk that their outputs contain deficiencies." — FRC, Generative and agentic AI guidance, human in the loop mitigation
- "putting the tool through a robust certification process" — FRC, Generative and agentic AI guidance, mitigations of deficient output
