# The IIA, Internal Auditor: COSO Issues GenAI Guidance (Jake Lamb, 11 Mar 2026)

URL: https://internalauditor.theiia.org/en/articles/2026/march/coso-issues-genai-guidance/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: IIA magazine news item on the COSO GenAI paper; confirms it applies the five COSO components rather than a new governance structure and groups GenAI use cases into eight capability categories. Names authors and intended readers (controllers, internal and external auditors). Says nothing about named owners or agent sign-off.

Quotes:
- "The report applies COSO's Internal Control-Integrated Framework to AI systems, offering control mapping, risk assessment tools, and governance guidance across the AI lifecycle." — Jake Lamb, The IIA Internal Auditor, 11 Mar 2026
- "GenAI introduces new cyber risks, including prompt manipulation, opaque reasoning, model drift, and frequent system changes." — Jake Lamb, The IIA Internal Auditor, 11 Mar 2026
- "Rather than proposing a new governance structure, the publication applies the COSO framework's five internal control components" — Jake Lamb, The IIA Internal Auditor, 11 Mar 2026
- "It is intended for management teams, risk and compliance professionals, controllers, IT and information security leaders, internal auditors, external auditors, and board oversight committees." — Jake Lamb, The IIA Internal Auditor, 11 Mar 2026
