# Vasarhelyi and Halper, The Continuous Audit of Online Systems (abstract, AT&T Bell Labs CPAS)

URL: https://www.youscribe.loungeup.com/catalogue/documents/ressources-professionnelles/gestion-et-management/the-continuous-audit-of-online-systems-515415
Type: primary
Read in session: 0004

Summary: Origin of continuous audit. Authors' own abstract of manuscript (submitted Aug 1989, revised Jun 1990; pub Auditing: JPT 1991). CPAS built at AT&T Bell Labs for Internal Audit; implements continuous process audit methodology (CPAM); target = large paperless database systems. Contrasts with traditional audit model. Third-party mirror, abstract only; internal-audit tool, not external audit.
