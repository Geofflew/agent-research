# Bird & Bird: Proposed Open Finance framework (FIDA), initial thoughts

URL: https://twobirds.com/en/insights/2023/global/proposed-open-finance-framework-fida-bird-and-birds-initial-thoughts
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Law-firm analysis of the Commission's June 2023 FIDA proposal (COM(2023) 360). FIDA is customer-permissioned sharing of data held by financial institutions (loans, savings, investments, pensions, non-life insurance, creditworthiness data); in scope as data holders are financial entities only, so an ERP/accounting vendor is neither data holder nor covered data. It is a data-sharing regime (customer data to data users), with no payment-initiation-style write right. Proposal status; the adopted text was not verified (eur-lex blocked).

Quotes:
- "FIDA provides a legal framework for sharing customer data between various entities active in the financial services sector." — Bird & Bird
- "In both cases the data holder makes customer data available upon request from a customer submitted by electronic means." — Bird & Bird
- "mortgage credit agreements, loans and accounts, except payment accounts" — Bird & Bird, summarising FIDA Art. 2(1)(a)
- "non-life insurance products in accordance with Directive Solvency II, with the exception of sickness and health insurance products" — Bird & Bird, summarising Art. 2(1)(e)
