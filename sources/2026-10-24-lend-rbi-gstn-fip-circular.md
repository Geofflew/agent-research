# RBI circular: GSTN included as Financial Information Provider under the Account Aggregator framework (23 Nov 2022)

URL: https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_NBFCNotificationView.aspx?Id=12412
Type: standard
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: RBI/2022-23/140 amends the 2016 NBFC-AA Master Direction so GSTN is an FIP; GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are the shared financial information; Department of Revenue regulates GSTN for this purpose. Stated purpose is cash flow-based MSME lending. This is the regulatory basis for lenders pulling tax-return data with taxpayer consent.

Quotes:
- "With a view to facilitate cash flow-based lending to MSMEs, it has been decided to include Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN) as a Financial Information Provider (FIP) under the Account Aggregator (AA) framework." — RBI circular RBI/2022-23/140, para 2
- "Department of Revenue shall be the regulator of GSTN for this specific purpose and Goods and Services Tax (GST) returns, viz. Form GSTR-1 and Form GSTR-3B, shall be the Financial Information." — RBI circular, para 2
