# Prepito: Substantive analytical procedures (expectation, threshold, investigating differences)

URL: https://www.prepi.to/en/articles/substantive-analytical-procedures
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0009C

Summary: Exam-prep article summarizing AU-C 520, ISA 520 and quoting AS 2305.21. Threshold (acceptable difference) is set before comparing, driven by materiality, no standard prints a percentage. Management's explanation must be quantified and corroborated by evidence not from the explainer; unexplained remainder is measured against the threshold. PCAOB site blocked, so this quotes AS 2305.21 secondhand.

Quotes:
- "No standard prints a percentage, and it is fixed before the comparison, since a threshold chosen afterwards can always be made to fit." — Prepito
- "Quantify the explanation: a reason that covers a third of the gap leaves two thirds unexplained." — Prepito
- "Corroborate it with evidence that does not come from the person who gave it." — Prepito
- "Measure the remainder against the threshold set at the start." — Prepito
- "The answer alone is not evidence" — Prepito, decision table
