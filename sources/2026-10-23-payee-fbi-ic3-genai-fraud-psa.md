# FBI PSA I-120324: Criminals Use Generative AI to Facilitate Financial Fraud

URL: https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2024/PSA241203
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006A

Summary: FBI warns genAI audio/video used for impersonation: real-time video chats w/ fake executives, voice clones used to access bank accounts. Advice: hang up, call number found independently (callback still recommended). Callback advice stands but channel trust eroding.

Quotes:
- "Criminals generate videos for real time video chats with alleged company executives, law enforcement, or other authority figures." — FBI PSA I-120324
- "Criminals obtain access to bank accounts using AI-generated audio clips of individuals and impersonating them." — FBI PSA I-120324
- "Verify the identity of the person calling you by hanging up the phone, researching the contact of the bank or organization purporting to call you, and call the phone number directly." — FBI PSA I-120324
- "Since it can be difficult to identify when content is AI-generated" — FBI PSA I-120324
