# City AM: How AI is forcing the Big Four to rethink graduate training

URL: https://www.cityam.com/?p=2459320
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: City AM, 23 Sep 2026. Asked KPMG, Deloitte, EY, PwC how graduate training is changing. Firms say AI removed first-year manual tasks so trainees do judgement work earlier; KPMG front-loads exams and pairs grads with experienced staff before client work. Answers are about soft skills; none describes how audit judgement is learned without doing the tasks.

Quotes:
- "because AI now handles those tasks, the Big Four giants have trainees take on complex, judgement-based work much earlier than previous cohorts" — City AM
- "Graduates will become more involved in reviewing and exercising judgement rather than simply producing outputs" — Marc Burrows, chief people officer, KPMG UK
- "KPMG's rejigged programme now has grads take front-loaded exams" — City AM
- "Before starting client engagements, graduates also learn by working alongside experienced staff and partners." — City AM
