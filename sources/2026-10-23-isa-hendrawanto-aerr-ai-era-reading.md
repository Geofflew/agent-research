# Audit Evidence in the AI Era: Reading Proposed ISA 330, 500 and 520

URL: https://hendrawanto.com/en/artikel/bukti-audit-era-ai-rancangan-isa-330-500-520/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: Practitioner (Indonesian audit partner) reading of the Aug 2026 IAASB EDs, 13 Oct 2026. Gives five reliability attributes, automation-bias point, controls-only guardrails, and a 100%-population analytics example. Effective-date claim (18 months after final approval) is the author's statement, not verified in IAASB text.

Quotes:
- "It proposes allowing at least 18 months for implementation after approval of the final standards." — Hendrawanto, status section
- "Testing 100% of the population does not automatically provide 100% assurance." — Hendrawanto, analytics example
- "an undue level of trust in information in digital form or generated by automated systems, especially when a tool is complex, uses numerous inputs or is not transparent" — Hendrawanto on proposed ISA 500 automation bias
- "Accuracy and completeness may be particularly important for information produced by the entity" — Hendrawanto, reliability attributes
- "A controls-only response may be considered only when inherent risk is not at the upper end of the spectrum, the risk is not significant and the controls are expected to be sufficiently effective to reduce the risk of material misstatement to an acceptably low level." — Hendrawanto on proposed ISA 330
- "whether all data were extracted from the source system" — Hendrawanto, analytics example
