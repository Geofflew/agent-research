# Intercom Fin AI Agent outcomes help article

URL: https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8205718-fin-ai-agent-outcomes
Type: vendor
Read in session: 0005B

Summary: Fin priced $0.99 per outcome, not per seat. Resolution = customer confirms OR leaves without asking more (assumed). One charge per conversation max. Qualification outcome $9.99. Vendor doc, Sep 2026.

Quotes:
- "A resolution is a type of outcome that is counted when, following Fin's last answer in a conversation, the customer either confirms the answer was satisfactory (confirmed resolution), or exits the conversation without requesting further assistance (assumed resolution)" — Intercom help center, Resolution definition
- "No further help is requested after the last" — Intercom help center, outcome price table (resolution row, $0.99)
- "A resolution is only counted when Fin provides an actual answer to a customer's query." — Intercom help center, Resolution definition
- "You can also set usage reminders and hard limits to ensure you're happy with how many resolutions Fin is able to provide." — Intercom help center, outcome pricing
