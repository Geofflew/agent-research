# Digital Markets Act Article 6: gatekeeper obligations (interoperability, business-user data access)

URL: https://www.eu-digital-markets-act.com/Digital_Markets_Act_Article_6.html
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008A

Summary: Text of Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 Art. 6 via a mirror (eur-lex blocks scripted fetch). Every Art. 6 duty binds only a designated gatekeeper and only for core platform services listed in its designation decision (6(1)). 6(7) is interoperability with OS/virtual-assistant features; 6(10) gives business users and authorised third parties continuous real-time access to data generated by use of the CPS: a read/data right, not a right to post transactions. Together with the designation list, no ERP vendor is bound.

Quotes:
- "The Gatekeeper shall comply with all obligations set out in this Article with respect to each of its core platform services listed in the designation decision pursuant to Article 3(9)." — DMA Art. 6(1)
- "effective interoperability with, and access for the purposes of interoperability to, the same hardware and software features accessed or controlled via the operating system or virtual assistant" — DMA Art. 6(7)
- "The gatekeeper shall provide business users and third parties authorised by a business user, at their request, free of charge, with effective, high-quality, continuous and real-time access to, and use of, aggregated and non-aggregated data" — DMA Art. 6(10)
