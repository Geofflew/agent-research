# Directive (EU) 2015/2366 (PSD2), Article 66

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32015L2366
Type: standard
Read in session: 0008B

Summary: Article 66 forces the bank (account servicing PSP) to serve third-party payment initiation providers: it must treat their payment orders without discrimination, and access cannot depend on a contract between the two. This is the legal compulsion for outside write access in banking that no ERP faces. Direct eur-lex fetch returned empty (202); read via r.jina.ai.

Quotes:
- "treat payment orders transmitted through the services of a payment initiation service provider without any discrimination other than for objective reasons" — PSD2 Art. 66(4)(c)
- "The provision of payment initiation services shall not be dependent on the existence of a contractual relationship between the payment initiation service providers and the account servicing payment service providers for that purpose." — PSD2 Art. 66(5)
- "Member States shall ensure that a payer has the right to make use of a payment initiation service provider" — PSD2 Art. 66(1)
- "not modify the amount, the payee or any other feature of the transaction." — PSD2 Art. 66(3)(h), duty on the initiation provider
