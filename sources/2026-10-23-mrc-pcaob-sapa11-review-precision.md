# PCAOB Staff Audit Practice Alert No. 11 (2013): management review controls and precision

URL: https://r.jina.ai/https://pcaobus.org/Standards/QandA/10-24-2013_SAPA_11.pdf
Type: standard

Summary: PCAOB staff text. Auditor must test MRC design + operation: precision, investigation criteria, reviewer competence/authority.
Sign-off alone = little evidence. Auditor re-performs reviewer analysis. Reviewer seat is itself audited control.
