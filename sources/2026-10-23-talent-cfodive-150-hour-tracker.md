# CFO Dive: CPA licensure 150-hour rule alternative paths tracker

Summary: CFO Dive running tracker. 'Bachelor's plus two' path: 120 hrs + 2 yrs exp + exam, backed by AICPA/NASBA UAA. 44 states/territories eased rules by Jun 2026; handful left (MI, MA, ND, WY, FL). Entry-level jobs shrinking in parallel.

Quotes:
- "For decades the standard licensure pathway required candidates to complete 150 hours of college credit, one year of professional experience and also pass the CPA exam." — CFO Dive, CPA licensure tracker
- "requires candidates to complete both a bachelor's degree which typically requires 120 credit hours and two years of professional experience, as well as pass the CPA exam" — CFO Dive, CPA licensure tracker
- "brings to 44 the total number of states and territories, including Puerto Rico, that have eased CPA certification rules since early last year" — CFO Dive, Rhode Island entry, 26 Jun 2026
- "Just a handful of states - Michigan, Massachusetts, North Dakota, Wyoming and Florida - have yet to formalize the licensing reforms that have swept the nation" — CFO Dive, Missouri entry, 14 Jul 2026
- "There are fewer entry-level positions as technology transforms finance teams" — CFO Dive, CPA licensure tracker
