# California Board of Accountancy: Examination and Licensure Requirements (AB 1175)

URL: https://www.dca.ca.gov/cba/outreach/faculty-resource/examination-and-licensure-requirements.shtml
Type: primary
Read in session: 0007C

Summary: CBA faculty resource on AB 1175 pathway (bachelor's with accounting concentration, no 150-unit rule, two years experience), effective Jan 1, 2027 with legacy route valid through 2028. Experience is general accounting services supervised by an actively licensed CPA; internships and VITA count; a master's can cut a year.

Quotes:
- "The legacy requirements call for one year of general accounting experience supervised by an actively licensed CPA, while the new requirements increase that to two years." — CBA faculty resource
- "there will be no change to the requirement of a minimum of 500 hours of attest work supervised by an actively licensed CPA with attest authority" — CBA faculty resource
- "the two-year experience requirement can be reduced by one year for earning a master's degree in an accounting-related subject" — CBA faculty resource
- "All experience performing general accounting services, as long as it was performed under the supervision of an actively licensed CPA, counts towards the experience requirement." — CBA faculty resource
- "a new licensure pathway set to take effect on January 1, 2027" — CBA faculty resource, AB 1175
