# Test your knowledge of professional ethics (Journal of Accountancy, 2010)

URL: https://www.journalofaccountancy.com/issues/2010/oct/20103166/
Type: secondary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: JofA Q&A on Interpretation 101-3 (predecessor of ET 1.295). Includes a review engagement where the CPA books entries for a small client: not impaired if client identifies the expense type and CPA only books it; bank rec allowed if client reviews and approves. Pre-AI, pre-codification renumbering.

Quotes:
- "A CPA performs a review engagement for a small company that has limited staff for its accounting and finance functions." — JofA 2010 Q5
- "if the client sufficiently identifies the type of expense and the CPA books the entry to the general ledger account based on the expense identified by the client, independence has not been impaired." — JofA 2010 answer 5
- "The skill, knowledge and/or experience needed will vary depending on the nature of the nonattest service." — JofA 2010 answer 1
