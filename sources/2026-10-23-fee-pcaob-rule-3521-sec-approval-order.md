# SEC order approving PCAOB Rule 3521 (contingent fees), 2006

URL: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2006-04-25/html/E6-6125.htm
Type: primary
Read in session: 0006B

Summary: SEC order approving PCAOB ethics and independence rules incl. Rule 3521. Auditors are not independent if they take contingent fees from audit clients, directly or indirectly. PCAOB tightened the SEC definition, removing the tax-results exception because it had been misinterpreted. pcaobus.org returned 403 to urllib; this Federal Register copy describes the rule.

Quotes:
- "proposed rule 3521 would treat registered public accounting firms as not independent if they enter into contingent fee arrangements, directly or indirectly, with audit clients" — SEC, Release 34-53677, Rule 3521 section
- "the pcaob found this provision had been misinterpreted and could permit fees that jeopardized the independence of auditors" — SEC order, on removing the tax-results exception
- "prohibit contingent fee arrangements for any services a registered public accounting firm provides to its audit clients, in order to maintain its independence" — SEC order, introduction
