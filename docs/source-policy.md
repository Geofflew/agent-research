# Source policy

Source entries describe public documents and retain their URLs and attribution. They are included as research notes so readers can inspect the premises behind an argument. Third-party excerpts remain attributable to their original authors; inclusion here does not grant ownership of the underlying material.

Use short excerpts that serve a specific premise. Keep the original wording, distinguish quoted text from summaries, and record the document type honestly. Prefer a primary study, filing, standard, or product documentation over a summary when the original is available.

Do not add company records, customer examples from private work, transcripts, support exports, account data, credentials, private documents, or authenticated links. Use invented examples explicitly labeled illustrative when a public source does not supply the needed example.

Sources are a dated research snapshot. Revisit the original document before relying on a current product feature, date, legal interpretation, or financial assertion.
