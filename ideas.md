# Candidate ideas

The detailed unresolved arguments live in `threads/`. This file is a starting point for new experiments, not a record of measured outcomes.

- Compare rule approval with item approval: what evidence does each leave behind?
- Treat blocked work as a product experience: who answers, by when, and with what context?
- Separate adoption from trust: can a platform measure errors independently of ordinary acceptance?
- Explore competing boundaries between ledger features, external skills, and governance layers.

For each idea, name a source that could falsify it and explain what changes in someone's day.
