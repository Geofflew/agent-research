## C1. Because the shipped close agents hold the exception, the proposed treatment, the approval, and the evidence as records but not the policy or the procedure, the reviewer's screen will shift from approving an agent's answer to checking the agent's citation of a versioned policy clause, and the product that holds those clauses will hold the boundary between ERP and close platform.

## C2. Agent capability will reach the close by two routes, as ERP features for work inside the ledger and as close-platform skills over a protocol layer for work that crosses the ledger, banks, billing, and spend, and because the first ERP protocol servers scope an outside agent by role and record type rather than amount or period, the close platform has to carry the posting limits and the controller sets the agent up as an ERP role.

Confidence: retired

## C3. Headless agent work in the close will arrive as item-level approval turning into class-level rules inside today's level 1 products, which skips verification and stops the draft-versus-final record an error rate needs, so the reviewer's screen after class approval must keep serving a per-class sample for re-performance, and the vendors that do this will be the ones auditors can rely on.

## C4. The preparer seat on routine close tasks moves to the agent first and the former preparer becomes a first-level reviewer, but unlike the second mammography reader that seat cannot be removed on a validation study alone, because it is a review control the external auditor tests and close errors surface as restatements long after the period rather than as interval cancers, so the seat stays until auditors accept an agent as the control's performer, and the person's work becomes setting investigation thresholds, handling exceptions, and sampled re-performance.

Confidence: retired

## C5. The buyer of close agents moves from the controller to the CFO when close platforms stop pricing by entity, module, and seat and start pricing per accepted task, which at least one close platform will do by end of 2028, and the unit that holds will be a task refunded when the reviewer rejects it or the period reopens, so the invoice becomes the error-rate report the audit committee asks for.

Confidence: retired

## C6. Agent write authority to the ledger will arrive first for amount-capped, reversible postings, because the controls that exist outside finance bound identity, audience, amount, and expiry but not decision class, and the one documented remedy for an agent incident, environment separation, has no equivalent for a ledger.

## C7. The externally verifiable record that lenders and auditors need to check a company's numbers without the company will be assembled from mandated invoice clearance and structured payment messages that outside parties already hold, not from a filing schema or a vendor schema, so the close platform's external-facing job becomes reconciling the ledger to records the tax authority and the banks hold rather than publishing its own.

The mechanism runs in five steps. First, clearance mandates send each invoice to the tax authority as structured data: Italy through SdI, Belgium since 1 January 2026, and EU cross-border B2B under ViDA from 1 July 2030. Second, holding both sides, the authority builds the books itself: Italy drafts VAT registers for about two million operators, India generates a read-only purchase-credit statement from suppliers' filings, and Chile built the purchase and sales register and removed the companies' obligation to keep their own. Third, payments became ISO 20022 only on 22 November 2025, and Fed remittance carries up to 9,000 characters naming invoices settled. Fourth, the invoice reference joins the legs, so an outsider can rebuild revenue and cash, though not without the company's consent: seven years into Italian clearance the tax authority lets a business delegate consultation of its electronic invoices only to intermediaries such as its accountant, and Italian credit scoring from those records works by the firm sharing them itself. Fifth, the company's job is the residual between its ledger and those records, which India already tells taxpayers to reconcile.

This disagrees with the design of India's purchase-credit statement, which treats suppliers' filings as the record the buyer reconciles to, because the record carries fiction both sides file: a single drive found 29,273 bogus firms behind suspected credit evasion of Rs. 44,015 crore, and one syndicate's 246 fake firms invoiced more than 1,500 buyers through bank accounts opened in their names. The residual between ledger and register catches divergence, not a transaction both sides recorded, so the outside record replaces invoice sampling for completeness but not for occurrence, and ISA 500 still holds that information from external sources may not be reliable. Payee accounts are the exception, as C40 shows: where a register ties bank accounts to each VAT payer's tax number, the outside record proves which account belongs to the vendor, which no invoice both sides file can fake.

Consequence: in India and Italy today, the payables and receivables preparer stops ticking invoices to the subledger and starts each close from the agent's list of differences between the ledger and the authority's register, such as GSTR-2B, which the taxpayer cannot edit, and works only that residual. The auditor asks for the residual list and tests it instead of sampling invoices, and the close vendor builds the authority feed as a reconciliation source. Signposts: a lender or auditor in Italy or India testing revenue against tax-authority data before ViDA's 1 July 2030 start confirms; a vendor schema adopted by two or more ERP or close vendors before then counts against.

## C10. ERP and close platforms will give agent authority the control shape of the market access rules, pre-set limits, rejection before entry, and automatic disable until a named person re-enables, only at the points where a party outside the company records the agent's act, which are payment release, period lock, and in clearance countries invoice issuance, because unlike an executed trade a journal in an open period is reversed by the company itself, so for ordinary journals the kill switch is the period status the controller already holds; and where the outside record runs a deadline with a default, as buyers' acceptance of invoices does in Chile, Colombia, and Mexico, an agent that stops for a reviewer has already acted, so there the limit is a deadline before which the agent must escalate, not a stop.

SEC Rule 15c3-5 blocks orders over pre-set thresholds and duplicative orders, and MiFID II RTS 6 disables a system after repeated executions until designated staff re-enable it, because of losses like Knight Capital's $460 million in 45 minutes. First, those rules act before entry because an executed trade is final: FINRA lets an officer break one only on price thresholds, generally within 30 minutes. Second, a journal in an open period has no counterparty and is reversed line by line, dated in an open period if the original has closed, so a looped agent posting costs a reversal, not a loss. Third, the ledger already has a pre-set gate: setting periods On hold stops further posting, and a period can be restricted to one user group during close, which excludes the agent. Fourth, two steps stay final as a trade is: an accepted payment order can be cancelled only if the receiving bank agrees, and a Permanently closed period cannot be reopened. Clearance adds a third: a French e-invoice issued through an approved platform cannot be modified and is corrected only by a new invoice the customer and the authority both see (C50), and in Italy, where every business invoice has cleared since 2019, an invoice sent and not rejected is considered issued and no longer modifiable, while billing software offers both a draft a person updates before sending and automatic sending, which the vendor itself warns can transmit outdated information. Clearance also puts clocks on the buyer: in Chile an invoice not claimed against within 8 days of the tax authority's receipt is irrevocably accepted, and for credit invoices the acknowledgement is given automatically; in Colombia an invoice not rejected within three business days is accepted irrevocably under the Commercial Code; in Mexico a cancellation request unanswered for three business days is accepted (C55).

This disagrees with the SEC's Knight finding as applied to the close, that human monitoring fails at machine speed: until the lock a person can find and reverse the agent's entries, so the scarce control is the lock, not the speed limit. For journal amounts a gate also exists: Dynamics 365 journal workflows send entries above a set total debit amount to an approver, so the agent submits as its own principal into that workflow rather than holding direct posting, while C23 denies it payee details and payment release outright. That gate holds only where the agent's journal enters the workflow, and the first ERP agent connections scope an outside agent only by role and record type: NetSuite's AI Connector Service lets an outside AI client create and update records under a NetSuite role, and NetSuite does not route for approval transactions created through CSV import, script deployment, web service, or RESTlet, so there a role allowed to post journals posts any amount to any open period and the amount gate moves to the close platform, which checks amount, account, and period before each ERP call. Consequence: with the agent set up as its own non-administrator ERP role, the controller stops reading its journals one by one; on the day before lock they work down the list of its open-period entries, reverse the ones that looped or misposted, and take its posting group out of the period, while on payment days the treasury analyst re-enables a disabled agent only after reading which release limit tripped, and in Chile the payables lead has the agent escalate any invoice still unreviewed on day five of eight. Signpost: the first ERP limit for an agent principal will attach to payment release or period status before journal amount, by end of 2029.

## C11. Close agents will deliver the full-population transaction testing that continuous auditing promised since 1989 and never reached, but it will arrive as the controller's monitoring inside the close platform rather than as internal audit's tool, and its payoff will be a lower external audit budget granted only to companies with a clean prior-year control opinion, not the replacement of periodic audit.

Continuous auditing was built at AT&T Bell Labs for internal audit in 1989; by 2012 none of nine leading internal audit functions had reached the continuous stage, and in 2021 only 39% of internal auditors said they were implementing or using it; in 2025 internal audit teams used generative AI in just 6% of fieldwork, so the new tool is again reaching audit last. The chain from there runs in four steps. First, the stated barriers were the improvisational nature of audit work against the rigidity of automated programs, and the tool belonging to audit while continuous monitoring belongs to management. Second, a close agent removes the first barrier because it handles the irregular item a fixed script could not, and the second because it runs inside management's own process. Third, testing every transaction then becomes a by-product of preparing the reconciliation, owned by the controller. Fourth, the external auditor can use that only as reduced year-end effort, because, as below, reliance in the one experiment required a clean prior-year control report as well.

This disagrees with the PCAOB's reading of its 2024 technology-assisted analysis amendments as answering auditors who may be reluctant due to perceived regulatory uncertainty. The amendments, effective for fiscal years beginning on or after December 15, 2025, accept analysis that tests the details of all items in a population, but AS 1105 still requires the auditor to test the accuracy and completeness of company-produced information or the controls over it, and AS 2301 requires investigating each flagged item to decide whether, individually or in the aggregate, it indicates a misstatement or a control deficiency. An agent's full-population monitoring is company-produced information, so every exception it raises becomes the auditor's work before any budget falls; in the one experiment, 87 auditors cut the budget only when a functioning continuous module met a clean prior-year control report, and firms with continuous audit pay lower fees with no difference in accruals quality, so the gain is a fee, not a different audit.

Consequence: the controller becomes the buyer and clears the agent's flagged exceptions each week before fieldwork, since the auditor must investigate every flagged item it uses, the auditor's year-end work moves from sampling transactions to testing the controls over the agent and its exception log, and the controller should expect the first cycle to add testing. Signpost: for a calendar-year company the first audit under the amendments covers 2026, so the clean control report arrives with it and a fee reduction attributed to agent monitoring cannot appear before the 2027 audit, disclosed in the 2028 proxy, and C42 narrows it further: a model retired between the two audits, as gpt-4o is on 14 January 2027, changes the control and moves the reduction out a year.

## C17. Close agents will shrink finance teams from the bottom by not hiring juniors rather than by cutting existing staff, turning the pyramid into a diamond during a shortage, and by about 2032 the binding constraint on agent adoption will be the supply of mid-level reviewers able to judge exceptions, because the routine preparation that trained them is the work agents take first.

The agents arrive into a shortage. More than 300,000 US accountants and auditors left their jobs in two years, a 17% decline, accounting graduates fell 6.6% to 55,152 in 2023-24, and material weaknesses citing inadequate accounting resources rose 14% in fiscal 2025. Yet 91% of CFOs expect flat or smaller finance teams, the most common planned shift is toward mid-level roles, and only 13% expect more junior roles, which is a diamond rather than a pyramid. Auditors primarily acquire technical knowledge on the job, through the interactions among engagement team members, and the routine tickmarking and reconciliation work through which juniors did so is what AI is absorbing.

## C19. The faster close that agents make possible will not be bought for public reporting, because public reporting is moving to less frequent filing and the median close already finishes weeks inside the filing deadline, so the value of agent speed will be captured in the lender's monthly package and in cost per close, and vendors selling days-to-close will hit a ceiling.

The chain runs in four steps. First, the public deadline does not bind: the median organization closes a month in 6.4 calendar days and the top quarter in 4.8, while the SEC's May 2026 proposal gives the semiannual Form 10-S the same 40- and 45-day deadlines as the 10-Q. Second, nothing outside rewards finishing earlier inside that window: the one test of a one-week shorter filing lag found no effect on valuation, and the UK saw no change in investment after dropping quarterly reporting. Third, the regulator is cutting frequency rather than raising it, treating cost of capital as a factor each firm weighs when choosing its cadence, so a continuous close has no public filing to feed. Fourth, the one clock that does bind is the lender's: filed credit agreements require monthly statements within thirty days after month end and quarterly statements within forty-five days, certified by a responsible officer, with a compliance certificate alongside, though one private-credit lender later softened monthly delivery to on request; some issuers' debt covenants require quarterly reporting whatever cadence the SEC allows, so companies still need quarterly internal processes.

It also fixes what C28 and C45 price: if days saved are worth nothing outside, the unit a buyer pays for is accepted work and a reliable lender package, not speed. ## C23. The hard limit on agent write authority in payables and the close will be adversarial input rather than error rate, and agent roles will exclude payee bank details and payment release, carved out of the inherited human role where the ERP's identity model inherits, as Workday's and SAP's do, and left out of a purpose-built agent permission set where it does not, as Business Central's does, because the agent reads the same vendor emails and invoices that carry payment fraud, prompt injection has no reliable prevention, and the ERP identity models give agents the permissions of human roles in which keeping bank changes from the payer is only a setting.

Payment fraud is the common case: 76% of US organizations had attempted or actual payments fraud in 2025, 74% were hit by business email compromise, and the FBI counts $55.5 billion in exposed BEC losses. Text planted in a retrieved email or file can control which APIs a language-model agent calls; EchoLeak showed one crafted email defeating Microsoft's injection classifier in Copilot with no click, and OWASP says it is unclear whether fool-proof prevention exists. An injected invoice is built to look routine, so it scores high, and an error rate measured on honest data says nothing about an adversary.

The ERPs then hand the agent the exposed right. Workday says every agent inherits your policies, rules, security groups, and permissions, SAP says every agent action carries your identity and access permissions, and NetSuite's connector runs under a specific role. Keeping bank details from the payer is configuration: NetSuite separates the AP Payment Clerk from the Vendor Master Clerk, and Dynamics 365 holds a new bank account until an approver approves it, but that setting can be set to No. A clerk holds the right safely only because of a step outside the system, the callback to a number already on file, while the agent is the one reading the email that requested the change, so the same permission is safe for one principal and unsafe for the other, and the only fix inside an inheritance model is to subtract fields and actions from the template on day one. Business Central took the other design: its Payables Agent runs under its own user with a system permission set restricted to the objects, fields, and actions needed for processing vendor invoices, the agent itself doesn't provide any capabilities for vendor approvals, a vendor it creates is set to Blocked, and it does not post the invoice.

This disagrees with the inheritance design that Workday and SAP present as the guardrail, because inheritance assumes the person and the agent see the same inputs, and with OWASP's remedy of human approval for high-impact actions, since a reviewer shown the agent's proposed change anchors on it. Consequence: the administrator builds each agent role as its human template minus bank fields and payment release; when the agent finds a new bank account on an invoice it does not carry the number into the ERP but sends the vendor to the vendor portal, where Dynamics 365 marks vendor-entered bank details as vendor-sourced; and the AP clerk works that queue, runs the pre-note, calls the number on file, the one job the agent never does, and logs the call, since the crime policy pays a social engineering loss only on a recorded callback. ## C26. Unreviewed close work will arrive by two routes sorted by task class, a frozen rule that a model drafts and the auditor benchmarks, and a threshold set in dollars against materiality as well as in confidence, checked by a sample drawn from above it, while an informal preference is not sufficient control evidence without a baseline test or an outcome sample, and every switch of a class to auto-post or change of a threshold is a configuration change that restarts the evidence.

The research distinguishes frozen rules from model-judged thresholds. Vendor documentation provides one example of the first route: FloQast generates a script from a matching rule's wording, tags each match with its rule, and reapplies matches when the rule is edited; its journal auto-post rules use a field, operator, and value. The SEC's management guidance rates judgment-heavy controls riskier and ties reliance on automated controls to effective IT general controls, so a frozen rule over structured fields, which fits matching and recurring accruals, is relied on after one baseline test: AS 2201 Appendix B lets the auditor carry it forward without repeating the prior year's tests while program change controls are effective, and counts its tables and parameters, such as a matching tolerance, as part of the control.
