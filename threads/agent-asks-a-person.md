What we know so far: No shipped close agent found in session 0007C sends a question to anyone. Microsoft's Dynamics 365 reconciliation agent recommends an action for each exception and the user accepts it or picks another; Oracle's EPM reconciliation assistant only answers a user who opens a chat; Rillet's flux agent writes the variance explanation itself instead of asking the account owner; Nominal's agent creates a task for the preparer and reviewer, which is the nearest thing to an outbound request, and that comes from a vendor blog. Today the question to operations is a controller's email about an unclear receipt, where replies take a day and some never arrive. Medical documentation already regulates machine-generated questions to clinicians: they must be non-leading, carry the indicators that justify them, offer an "other / unable to determine" option, and not be repeated until the desired answer comes, and those rules bind AI-generated queries too.

Tried in 0007C (iter 48, reverted): C9 sharpen with question design — agent asks owner for receipt/fact, not to confirm its number; CDI non-leading rules (indicators, all options + unable to determine, no repeat); reply releases item only with document attached (inquiry alone insufficient). Novelty rose 1->2 but evidence and specificity fell because the mammography premise was cut in the same edit. Retry: keep C9's existing premises, add only the question design; or put it in a claim about accruals.

What it would take to become a claim: one shipped close product (release note or help doc) that sends a request to a named business owner and records the answer, or a dated roadmap item saying it will; plus evidence on what share of close exceptions wait on information outside finance rather than on judgment.

Open sub-questions:
- If an agent asks a budget owner to confirm an accrual, does the owner anchor on the agent's proposed amount the way a reviewer does, and should the question be non-leading like a clinical query?
- Who owns the answer as evidence: does the auditor treat a business owner's reply to an agent as inquiry, which alone is not sufficient evidence?
- When in the close does the agent ask: continuously as invoices land, or batched before a cutoff?
