# Could the external auditor rely on an agent that internal audit runs over the ledger for the audit committee, instead of running its own?

Status: live
Opened: session 0003F
Yield: 1.82 (0009C C11; n and r rose, r untargeted)
Touches: C46, C49, C11, C33; scenarios/2036-audit-committee-runs-its-own-ledger-agent
Sources: [iaagent-isa610-revised-2013], [iaagent-iia-global-internal-audit-standards], [iaagent-protiviti-ai-adoption-ia-survey], [iaagent-iia-on-the-frontlines-genai-year], [iaagent-polyu-ada-internal-audit-seminar]

What we know so far: ISA 610 lets the external auditor use internal audit's work only if the function's organizational status supports objectivity (judged by whether it reports to those charged with governance), it is competent, and it applies a systematic and disciplined approach including quality control; the auditor must use less of it as judgment and risk rise, and may not use direct assistance for significant judgments [iaagent-isa610-revised-2013]. The IIA's Global Internal Audit Standards have the chief audit executive report functionally to the board and say the function must strive to have technology, including software that tests an entire population [iaagent-iia-global-internal-audit-standards]. One archival study (abstract only) associates internal audit data analytics with lower later external audit fees and delays [iaagent-polyu-ada-internal-audit-seminar]. Adoption is modest: 39% of internal audit functions use AI for any activity and 23% of IT audit functions use AI or ML tools in 2024 [iaagent-protiviti-ai-adoption-ia-survey]; 41% of audit teams use generative AI, 6% in fieldwork [iaagent-iia-on-the-frontlines-genai-year].

Counter-case: ISA 610 evaluates the function, not a tool; if the function fails the tests its procedures count as internal controls the auditor tests rather than evidence it uses, and journal-entry and management-override testing is the high-risk, high-judgment area where reliance is smallest. So the auditor would at most cut its own population run for low-risk classes, not drop it.

What it would take to become a claim: an audit firm methodology or regulator inspection note on using internal audit's analytics or agents under ISA 610 or AS 2605; a company disclosing an internal audit population agent reporting to the audit committee. With C46 that would support a claim that the auditor's own agent and internal audit's agent split the population by risk.
