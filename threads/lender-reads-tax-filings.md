Draft claim (0006B, scored 6 net < add bar 7; g1 n2 m1 s2 c2 t0 e1 r1; not kept):

> ## C37. The first outside party to act on a company's numbers without the company's close will be the small-business lender reading the borrower's tax filings through a consent rail, as India's lenders already do, so for those borrowers the monthly lender package disappears and the close's external job becomes making the ledger agree with the return before it is filed, because the filed return, not management's package, is what the lender reads.
> 
> The rail exists and is in use. In November 2022 the Reserve Bank of India, with a view to facilitating cash flow-based lending to MSMEs, made the GST Network a Financial Information Provider under the Account Aggregator framework, with GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B returns as the financial information; the borrower's authorised signatory consents and the lender receives completed returns filed in the last 18 months. By March 2026, 989 institutions had gone live as information users and 284.6 million accounts were linked, and in the second half of fiscal 2026 consent-based data supported 8.4% of India's retail and MSME lending by value. SIDBI's GST Sahay lends to micro enterprises on the basis of GST invoices in a paperless journey from origination to repayment.
> 
> The step that matters is what the lender stops asking for. A lender that pulls the return and the bank feed itself has no use for a package the borrower prepares, so the borrower's close is no longer an input to the loan; what the loan reads is a document filed to the tax authority on the tax calendar. Any gap between the ledger and the return then surfaces at the lender, not at the borrower's review. Consequence: for these borrowers the accountant stops assembling a monthly lender package and instead reconciles the ledger to the outward supplies being declared before each return is filed, with the agent's residual list going to the person who signs the return; close speed matters only up to the filing date. Signpost: lenders outside India (EU e-invoicing countries after 2026, or US cash-flow underwriting on bank feeds) dropping management-prepared monthly financials for small loans in favour of consented filings within one loan renewal cycle.
>
