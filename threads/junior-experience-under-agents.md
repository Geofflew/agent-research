# How does a junior accountant get qualifying experience when the agent prepares?

Status: live
Opened: session 0007C
Yield: 12.48 (0007A C17 +0.82)
Touches: C17, C12, C9
Sources: [cpaexp-nasba-new-pathways-blog], [cpaexp-tsbpa-pathway-b-faq], [cpaexp-cba-ab1175-licensure-requirements], [cpaexp-ohio-orc-4701-06], [talent-cpa-practice-advisor-junior-training]

What we know so far: NASBA and the AICPA added a 120-hour pathway to the Uniform Accountancy Act in 2025 that requires two years of professional experience. Texas requires two years of non-routine accounting work directly supervised by a CPA, with its new pathway effective 1 August 2026; California's new route starts 1 January 2027 with two years of supervised general accounting experience; Ohio's statute took effect 1 January 2026 and leaves satisfactory experience to the board. None of these sources mentions AI or says whether reviewing machine output counts. Thesis C17 now says the junior's queue becomes the blind re-performance sample and the supervising CPA compares the junior's figure with the agent's.

What it would take to become a claim of its own: a board rule or NASBA guidance on AI-assisted work as experience, or data on how many experience verifications are rejected.
