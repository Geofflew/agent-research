# What does a company do after a material weakness for too few accounting staff, and does an agent count as remediation?

Status: live
Opened: session 0008C
Yield: 0.0
Touches: C17, C30, C33; scenarios/2036-nobody-left-who-can-judge; threads/junior-experience-under-agents
Sources: [expq2-bbot-10k-fy2025-mw-remediated], [expq2-vivakor-10k-fy2025-mw-not-remediated], [talent-kpmg-material-weakness-trends]

What we know so far: Remediation today is hiring plus outside consultants, and staffing churn keeps it open. One filer's weakness "related to BBOT not having sufficient full-time accounting personnel", reported for 2023 and 2024, was remediated by 31 December 2025 after it "Hired additional accounting personnel" and "Engaged third-party consultants and specialists" [expq2-bbot-10k-fy2025-mw-remediated] (primary, 10-K). Another hired "external accounting consultants" yet says its weaknesses "have not been fully remediated ... primarily due to ongoing staffing challenges, including turnover and the time required to train and integrate new personnel" [expq2-vivakor-10k-fy2025-mw-not-remediated] (primary, 10-K). Neither names software or agents as remediation. So the 2036 scenario's boards buying judgment would follow the path already in filings, hiring and consultants, and the open question is whether an agent ever appears in an Item 9A remediation paragraph and whether auditors accept it for a people weakness.

What it would take to become a claim: a 10-K naming an AI agent or close automation as remediation of a resource weakness, with the auditor's view, or remediation-duration data by weakness type.

Open sub-questions:
- Does remediation by outside firm move the work into C27's outsourced close, with C34's independence limit if the auditor is the same network?
- How long do resource weaknesses stay open on average (Audit Analytics)?
